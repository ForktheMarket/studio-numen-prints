The Biarritz surf poster is a vintage style travel print for surfers, van owners and anyone who has driven down to the French Atlantic coast with boards on the roof.

An old red and cream camper van is parked side on at the edge of a grassy clifftop, three longboards strapped to its roof rack, with a flat blue sea behind it and a clear blue sky filling most of the print. The cliff face drops away in the foreground in browns and sandy grays, and the word Biarritz is set along the bottom in sky blue capitals. It looks like a photograph from a 1960s holiday, softened and framed in cream.

It suits a hallway, a bedroom, a living room, a beach house or a garage, and it works well above a bed, a sofa or a console table. It pairs with the Newquay, Bondi Beach and Nazare prints for a gallery wall of surf towns.
