This white peony print shows a single huge peony in full bloom, cropped by all four edges and lit against a deep black background, made for bedrooms, living rooms and anyone who loves bold florals.

The petals are painted in cream and warm white, curling and folding over one another, with soft shadows in their creases and a cluster of golden stamens at the center. The flower fills the whole sheet, so the picture is all petal and light. The black behind it makes the white glow.

The palette is cream, gold and black, dramatic but still soft, which suits dark painted walls as well as plain white ones. It works above a bed, a sofa or a dressing table, and belongs to anyone who likes flowers on a grand scale.
