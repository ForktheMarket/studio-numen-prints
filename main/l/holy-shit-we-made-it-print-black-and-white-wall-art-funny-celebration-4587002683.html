This Holy Shit We Made It print is a funny black and white quote poster for couples, families and friends who got through something hard and want to mark it.

Five words are stacked in thick black brush capitals down the middle of a cream background. Thin black rays flick outward from the lettering on every side, like the lines around a cartoon light bulb. The paint is dense and slightly speckled, with rough ends where the brush dragged, so it reads as painted by hand and not typeset. There is plenty of open cream space around it, which lets the words do the shouting.

It is the sentence people say out loud when the keys are finally in hand, the degree is finished, the business has survived its first year or the last box is unpacked. It is blunt, and it is happy. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits almost any color scheme and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits an entryway, a living room, a hallway, a home office or the wall by the front door of a first home. It is a good match for new homeowners, newlyweds, graduates, business partners and anyone coming out the other side of a long year. The wording is strong, so it is best for homes that are fine with a swear word on the wall.
