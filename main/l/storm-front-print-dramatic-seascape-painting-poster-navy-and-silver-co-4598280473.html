This Storm Front print is a painterly seascape poster of a dark storm cloud moving in from the left over a choppy sea, with clear silver light still on the right, for anyone who wants drama on the wall.

The cloud is a single soft mass of charcoal and navy that takes up the top left of the picture, with rain visible beneath it as a gray veil. To the right the sky is pale and bright, and the sea below reflects both, dark under the storm and silver under the light. White caps pick out the waves in the foreground.

It suits an office, a living room or a hallway, and it looks right in rooms with dark walls or a lot of wood. It is also a natural pair with the rough sea print, which shares the same navy water and torn sky.
