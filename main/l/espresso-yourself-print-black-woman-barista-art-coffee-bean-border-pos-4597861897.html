This Espresso Yourself print is a retro poster of a Black barista in a cream apron holding out a small cup of espresso, for anyone who takes the first shot of the day seriously.

She stands inside a mint green oval with a towel over her shoulder and a gold hoop at her ear, offering the cup on its saucer with a small smile. A thick border of hand painted coffee beans surrounds her, and the words sit beneath in chunky brown serif capitals on cream.

The colors are cream, mint, coffee brown, coral and deep brown, warm and a little old fashioned. It suits a kitchen, a coffee bar, a dining room, a cafe or a breakfast nook, and it sits well above the coffee machine. A print with a smile for baristas, espresso drinkers and anyone who likes a pun.
