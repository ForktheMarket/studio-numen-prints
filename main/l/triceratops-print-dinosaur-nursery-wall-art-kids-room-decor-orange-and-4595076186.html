The Triceratops print is a bold two color poster for a dinosaur loving child, a nursery or a playroom.

This one is a head on portrait. The Triceratops looks straight out of the picture, its wide bony frill spread like a shield and edged with a row of small spikes. Two long horns curve up and out above the eyes and a short third horn sits on the nose over a hooked beak. The whole head is drawn in orange and deep teal green, with fine engraved lines running across the frill and face so the skin looks wrinkled and leathery. The word TRICERATOPS is printed in chunky orange capitals across the top, and everything sits on a plain cream background with a soft speckled texture.

It works in a kids bedroom, a nursery, a playroom or a hallway, above a bed, a desk or a dresser. Orange and teal go with white walls, pale wood, sage green and mustard, and it pairs with the Stegosaurus and the Velociraptor from the same series for a small dinosaur wall.
