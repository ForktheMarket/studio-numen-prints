This border collie print is a vintage style poster for collie owners, farmhouses and anyone with a working dog at home.

A black and white collie lies on a patch of olive green grass, front paws stretched out, head turned to the right as if watching the field. The coat is drawn in black and cream with soft olive shading in the ruff and the legs, and the grass is a rough edged block of the same green, so the whole print uses only three tones on a cream background. The words BORDER COLLIE run across the top in heavy olive capitals. The feel is calm and alert at the same time, a dog waiting for the next command.

It suits a hallway, a boot room, a kitchen or a study, hung beside the back door or above a bench. Olive, black and cream sit well with natural wood, wool, stone and sage painted walls, and it fits anyone who walks a collie every day. It pairs with the other breeds in the same series.
