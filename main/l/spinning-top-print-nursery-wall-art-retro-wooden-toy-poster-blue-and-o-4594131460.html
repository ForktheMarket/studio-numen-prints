This spinning top print is a bold blue and orange poster for a nursery, a playroom or a living room that likes mid century color.

A wooden spinning top leans to one side on a deep blue surface, caught as if it is halfway through a spin. Rings of red, orange and amber are painted around its body, with bare wood showing at the handle and around the rim. You can see the fine ridges left by the lathe, and a dark shadow pools underneath on the right. The background is the same rich blue from top to bottom, so the warm stripes stand out sharply.

It is the most grown up picture in the series and works as well in a hallway or home office as in a child's room. Blue and orange are opposites, which is why the pairing looks so lively. It goes well with white walls, navy, walnut or oak furniture and rust colored fabrics.
