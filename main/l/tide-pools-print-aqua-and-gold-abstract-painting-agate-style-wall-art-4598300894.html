This Tide Pools print is an abstract coastal poster of rounded aqua pools scattered across warm sand, each one ringed in gold, for anyone who likes abstract art with a seaside feel.

The pools come big and small and overlap slightly, painted in layered washes of turquoise, teal and pale blue with watery blooms inside them like polished agate. Their rims are gold, thick in places and thin in others, and the ground between them is a soft sandy cream. From a distance it reads as pattern, up close each pool is different.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom or a modern living room, and it is the most pattern like piece in the series, so it pairs well with the plainer seascapes. Hang it where people stand close, such as beside a mirror or over a basin, so the detail in each pool gets seen.
