This Rejoice print is a gospel choir poster showing a repeating pattern of Black women singing in plum robes with gold stoles, for anyone who feels most alive when the choir stands up.

Row after row of singers fill the print edge to edge, heads tilted back, eyes closed, mouths open mid note. Their robes are painted in deep plum with gold sashes, and the whole pattern has the loose, warm feel of a gouache painting. A cream panel sits in the center with REJOICE in plum serif capitals.

The colors are plum, marigold, cream and warm brown, rich enough to carry a large wall. It suits a living room, a music room, a hallway or a church office, and it pairs well with the Sunday Best print from the same set. A joyful piece for choir members, praise leaders and anyone who sings in the car.
