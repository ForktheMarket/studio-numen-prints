This Reclining Figure print is a cubist poster of a person stretched out on a chair with their head resting on one arm, built from angled planes of orange, ochre and olive, for anyone who wants a figure piece that feels easy and unhurried.

The body is broken into blocks: a bright orange top, a dark head with a small painted face turned toward you, cream and tan limbs folded into the chair, and a black and rust shape where the legs cross. Everything is set against a wall of mustard and ochre panels with thick, visible brush strokes, so the surface looks like a real painted board.

The colors are orange, ochre, olive, cream, rust and black, warm and golden. It suits a living room, a lounge, a bedroom or a reading corner, and it sits well above a sofa, an armchair or a bed. A print for anyone who likes mid century interiors, thick paint and figures reduced to simple shapes.
