This Neutral Checkerboard print is a textured abstract poster of knife squares in bone, beige, gray and rust packed edge to edge in a loose grid, for anyone who likes pattern that stays soft and earthy.

There are thirty squares, five across and six down, each one a slab of thick paint with its own direction of stroke. Most are pale, from chalk white through sand and putty, with a scatter of slate gray and charcoal squares and a few warm rust and burnt orange ones dropped in to keep the eye moving. The slight gaps between them read like grout.

The colors are bone, beige, taupe, slate gray, charcoal and rust, warm and neutral. It suits a living room above the sofa, a hallway, a dining room or a home office, and it sits well with stone, concrete, oak and linen. A good anchor for a gallery wall of plain neutral pieces.
