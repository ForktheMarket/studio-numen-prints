The Drop Your Pants Here print is a funny retro poster for a laundry room, a utility room or a bedroom door.

Most of the top half is a flat block of pale peach, like a folded sheet laid on the page, with a thin bright red line along its right and bottom edges so it looks slightly lifted off the cream background. Below it, DROP YOUR PANTS HERE runs over three lines in heavy red capitals with soft rounded corners and a faint inked texture. A small red arrow points straight down at whatever hamper or basket sits beneath it. Red, peach and cream, nothing more, and the joke lands from across the room.

It suits a laundry room, a utility room, a teenager's bedroom, a mudroom or the wall above a laundry hamper. Red and peach go with white walls, pale wood, black metal hooks and terracotta tiles. It fits busy parents, roommates, newlyweds and anyone whose laundry pile tends to end up on the floor instead of in the basket. It pairs with the Laundry Today or Naked Tomorrow print from the same series.
