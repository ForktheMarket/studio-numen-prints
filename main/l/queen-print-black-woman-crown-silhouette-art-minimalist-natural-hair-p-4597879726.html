This Queen print is a minimalist poster of a Black woman's profile cut as a solid black silhouette, her hair rising into the three points of a crown, for anyone who likes a message said in one shape.

She faces right with her chin level and her lips full, the neck and shoulders dropping away in a clean curve. The crown grows straight out of the hairline, so the hair and the crown are one thing. QUEEN sits beneath in heavy black capitals on a plain cream background.

The colors are black and cream, nothing else, so it fits any scheme and any gallery wall. It suits a bedroom, a hallway, a living room or a dressing room, and it sits well above a bed or a mirror. A print for anyone who wears the crown without needing to say so.
