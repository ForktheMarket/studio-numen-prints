The brass globe print is a moody still life poster for a home office, a study or a traveler.

An old globe on a brass stand sits on a dark wooden desk, with a brass magnifying glass on a turned wooden handle standing beside it. The globe is painted in dark green and black with the continents in worn gold and ochre, and a brass meridian ring curves over the top. The light comes from the upper right and catches the equator line, the rim of the magnifier and the ridges of the stand. The background is a flat black green, and the desk front below is dark brown. The paint is loose and thick on the map, so the land masses have a cracked, aged texture.

It suits a home office, a study, a living room or a hallway, above a desk or beside a bookcase. The green, gold and dark brown go with walnut, leather, brass lamps and navy walls. It fits travelers and map collectors, and it pairs with the marble bust and the library shelves from the same series.
