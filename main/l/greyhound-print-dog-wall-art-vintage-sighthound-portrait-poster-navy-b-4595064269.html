This greyhound print is a vintage style poster for greyhound owners, rescue homes and anyone who likes a long elegant line.

The coat is built from a deep navy blue for the back, the head and the haunches, a lighter slate blue for the mid tones, and a warm peach for the chest, the forelegs and the stripe down the muzzle.
