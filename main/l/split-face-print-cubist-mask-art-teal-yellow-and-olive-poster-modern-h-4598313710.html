This Split Face print is a cubist mask poster of a single head divided straight down the middle, one half in sun yellow and pale blue, the other in deep teal, for anyone who wants a bold two tone piece with a calm, steady stare.

The face is a long oval with flat almond eyes, a small dark mouth and a nose that runs down the center line where the colors change. The yellow half catches the light and the teal half sits in shadow, and behind the head are blocks of olive, burnt orange and cream painted in loose, dry strokes. An orange ear shape sits off to the right.

The colors are teal, yellow, olive, orange and cream, balanced between cool and warm. It suits a hallway, a living room, an office or a bedroom, and it looks right above a console table, a sideboard or a desk. A print for anyone who likes masks, mid century color and faces built from simple shapes.
