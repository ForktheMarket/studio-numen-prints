The stag beetle print is a vintage entomology plate poster for a study, a boys room or a dark academia hallway.

A single stag beetle is shown from above in the center of the plate. Its two great antler jaws curve up and inward, each tipped with a small hook, and they glow deep red where the light catches them. The head and thorax are glossy black, the wing cases are a rich chestnut red brown with a dark seam down the middle, and six spiked legs reach out to the sides. The background is a warm cream, and STAG BEETLE is lettered in small capitals underneath. The style is painted and sharp.

It suits a study, a hallway, a teenage bedroom or a downstairs bathroom, and sits well above a desk. Black, red and cream go with dark wood, leather, brass and forest green, and it pairs with the jewel beetle and emperor moth from the same series. It fits collectors and bug lovers.
