This Bet on Yourself print is a black and white motivational poster showing a young bull calf standing in a pool of light, with the shadow of a full grown bull on the wall behind it.

The calf stands side on in the middle of a dark floor, legs a little apart, head turned to look straight at you. Light from the left catches its coat and ears and leaves the rest in silhouette. On the gray wall behind, where its own small shadow should be, there is the outline of a heavy adult bull with a muscled shoulder, a lowered head and long curved horns. The words BET ON YOURSELF. cross the upper part of the picture in bold white capitals.

The bull has long stood for confidence and rising markets, so this one speaks to traders, investors, founders and anyone who has just gone out on their own. It also works without any of that, as a picture of something small that already knows what it will be. The tones are smoky grays with one strong black shape. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a trading desk, a study, a garage or a den.
