This tugboat print is a bold linocut style poster for a nursery, a kids bathroom or a family that loves the water.

A small tugboat with a red hull and a red wheelhouse pushes through a navy sea, tipped slightly as it rides a wave. The hull is navy below the waterline with red showing through the rough print marks, the wheelhouse has three windows, and a tall navy funnel and a thin mast stand up behind it. Below the boat, five rows of navy waves roll across the page in a loose zigzag, with white spray breaking around the bow. Above it, the words LITTLE TUG sit in big red block capitals. The background is warm cream and the whole design uses just red, navy and cream, with the speckled, slightly uneven look of block printed ink.

It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a kids bathroom or a coastal hallway. Red and navy go well with white tongue and groove, striped bedding, rope and pale wood. It fits new parents, sailing families and anyone who grew up near a harbor.
