This Winding River print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a pale river curving down through a wide valley from distant misty peaks, for anyone who wants a landscape with depth and calm.

The river starts as a thin thread below the far mountains and widens as it comes toward the viewer, its water the same cream as the sky. Dark wooded banks and low hills line both sides in layered gray and black ink, and the mountains at the top fade back in soft bands. A small red seal sits at the lower right. The eye follows the river the whole way up.

It suits a living room, a hallway, a study or a dining room, and it is a good central piece for a group of ink landscapes. Hung above a sofa it reads as a window onto somewhere far away and very quiet.
