The butterfly peony print is a blue and white chinoiserie poster for a bedroom, a guest room or a powder room.

A double line oval in cobalt holds the design on a cream white ground. Inside it a large peony opens at the center, its ruffled petals built up in layers of pale blue wash and deep indigo, with a closed bud leaning in from the right and a sprig of leaves at the left. Dark glossy leaves fill the bottom of the oval. Above the bloom a butterfly hovers with its wings spread wide, patterned with rows of white spots and crescents on dark blue, its antennae just touching the border. The line work is fine and even, with the look of a painted plate or a tile.

It suits a bedroom, a guest room or a powder room, above a bed, over a dressing table or beside a mirror. It goes with white walls, pale wood, brass, linen and pale blue or navy fabrics, and it fits gardeners and anyone who collects blue and white. It pairs with the orchid from the same series.
