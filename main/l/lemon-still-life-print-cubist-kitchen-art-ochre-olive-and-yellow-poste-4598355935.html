This Lemon Still Life print is a cubist kitchen poster of a jug, a bowl of lemons and a face caught in the curve of the jug, painted in ochre, olive, cream and yellow, for anyone who wants a figure hidden inside a still life.

The jug takes up most of the page, its body broken into orange and olive planes with a round handle on the left. Look into the middle of it and a face appears, an eye, a nose and a mouth built from the same angled pieces, as if someone is reflected in the glaze. Four bright yellow lemons sit in a rust bowl at the bottom right, the one clear, simple thing in the picture. The background is split between a dark olive wall on the left and a pale cream wall on the right.

The colors are ochre, orange, olive, cream and lemon yellow, warm and sunny. It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a breakfast nook or a hallway, and it looks right above a table, a counter or a sideboard. A print for cooks, for anyone who likes mid century still life, and for a kitchen that wants one strong piece of color.
