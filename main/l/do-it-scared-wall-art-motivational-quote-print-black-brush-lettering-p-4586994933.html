This Do It Scared print is a motivational quote poster for anyone about to start a new job, a business, a move or anything else that feels too big.

Three words are painted in tall, narrow black capitals with a dry brush, so the strokes break up and show streaks of the background through them. Behind the words is a pale peach arch on a cream background, soft enough that you notice it second. The letters are slightly uneven in height, which makes the line feel spoken and not shouted.

The message is simple: you do not have to wait until the fear goes away. Black, peach and cream are neutral enough for most rooms and sit easily with wood, stone and white walls. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a bedroom, a hallway or a studio, anywhere you will see it before doing the hard thing.
