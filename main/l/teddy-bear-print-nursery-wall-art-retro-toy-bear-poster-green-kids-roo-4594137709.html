This teddy bear print is a green and orange poster for a nursery, a child's bedroom or a playroom.

A plain teddy bear sits slightly slumped on a muted green background, turned a little to one side. Its fur is a warm ginger orange with a fuzzy, felted texture, and you can see the seams down its head, arms and feet. It has two small black bead eyes, a black stitched nose and round ears, with its arms hanging loose and its legs stuck out in front. A soft shadow falls behind it, and a fine speckle across the green gives the picture the feel of an old toy catalog page.

The bear has the patient look of a toy waiting on the bed for someone to come home. The picture suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or a reading corner. Green and orange go well with white or cream walls, light wood, rattan and terracotta fabrics.
