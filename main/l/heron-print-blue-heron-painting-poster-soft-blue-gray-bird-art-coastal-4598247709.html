This Heron print is a painterly bird poster of a great blue heron standing on one leg in still water, its neck drawn back and its long beak pointed into the light, against a soft gray blue sky, for anyone who wants a calm coastal piece with a real bird in it.

The heron is painted in slate blue and cream with a black stripe over the eye and long loose feathers trailing from its chest. Its thin orange legs drop into the water, where the reflection ripples back in soft dark strokes. The water and sky are almost the same pale blue gray, so the bird seems to float in the middle of the picture.

The colors are slate blue, blue gray, cream and a touch of orange, cool and quiet. It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a living room or a hallway, and it is a natural fit for coastal, lake house and nautical rooms. A good piece for anyone who watches birds or lives near water.
