This Panther print is a painterly wildlife poster of a black panther walking straight toward the viewer out of a plain sand colored ground, head low and pale eyes fixed ahead, for anyone who wants a wall piece with some quiet menace in it.

The cat is painted almost entirely in black with the sheen of its coat caught in soft gray highlights along the shoulders and brow. Its eyes are two small points of amber, and its long whiskers spread out white against the dark face. One front paw is set down ahead of the other. The background is a bare beige wash with a few brush marks and nothing to distract from the animal.

The colors are black, charcoal, beige and amber, dark and dramatic. It suits a living room above the sofa, a home office, a hallway or a bedroom with darker walls, and it works well with black frames, brass and leather. A good piece for a moody room or anyone who likes their animals a little dangerous.
