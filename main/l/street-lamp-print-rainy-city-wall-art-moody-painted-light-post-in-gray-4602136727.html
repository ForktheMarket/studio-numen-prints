This street lamp print shows a single old lamp post glowing in the rain, the rest of the city fading into gray mist behind it, made for hallways, living rooms and apartments.

The lamp stands tall and dark just right of center, its globe a soft white glow with a halo of light in the wet air. To the left a stone building rises into the fog with one warm lit window, and the pavement below is a sheet of wet reflections in silver, gray and amber. Everything else is left loose and unfinished, so the eye stays on the lamp.

The palette is smoke gray and soft gold, quiet and atmospheric, which makes it an easy print for a narrow hallway wall, a landing or beside a bed. It suits anyone who loves foggy evenings and the small lights of a city.
