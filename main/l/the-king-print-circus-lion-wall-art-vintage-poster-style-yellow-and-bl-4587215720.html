The King is a vintage circus style poster of a golden lion on a blue star pedestal, for kids rooms, nurseries and anyone who likes a bit of quiet confidence on the wall.

A lion sits upright on a round blue circus pedestal with a large pale star on its side. His mane is huge and yellow, and he looks off to the right with his chin up. His tail trails down onto the floor behind the pedestal. The words THE KING run across the top in dark teal blue capitals. The lion is drawn in yellow and gold with dark shading, like an old lithograph, on a warm, aged cream background.
