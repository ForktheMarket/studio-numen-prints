This bird and chick print is a calm teal poster for a nursery, a child's bedroom or a playroom.

Two toy birds stand facing each other on a soft teal background. The big one is a rounded golden yellow bird with a small cream beak, a round black eye and a simple wing shape on its side. The little one is bright red and less than half its height, and it looks straight up at the big bird. Both stand on thin orange wire legs, and a low shadow runs along the ground beneath them.

It reads as a parent and child having a quiet word, which is why it works so well in a baby's room. Nothing else is in the picture, so the two birds and the space between them do all the talking. It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or a reading corner. Teal, yellow and red go well with white or cream walls, light wood, rattan and mustard fabrics.
