This Made With Love print is a minimalist kitchen poster of a Black woman kneading bread dough, for anyone who bakes slowly and by hand.

She looks down at the dough with her eyes half closed, hair pulled into a low bun, wearing a soft peach top. Her hands press into a pale round of dough on a sage green board, with a little flour spilled at the edge. The background splits into cream above and a warm peach band below, and the words MADE WITH LOVE sit in wide rust capitals across the top.

The whole print is calm and pared back, five colors and clean shapes with a faint grain texture. Peach, sage and rust sit easily with wood counters, white tile and plants. It suits a kitchen, a pantry wall, a bakery counter or a dining room where someone is proud of their sourdough. Pairs well with the Grandma's Kitchen and Eat Good prints from the same set.
