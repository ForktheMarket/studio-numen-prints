This Sleeping Fox print is a painterly wildlife poster of a red fox curled nose to tail in a tight circle, eyes closed, for anyone who wants something warm and calm on the wall.

The fox fills the middle of the picture, painted in soft rust, copper and cream with a white bib and dark ear tips, against a plain warm tan ground with nothing else in it. The brushwork is loose and the light falls from one side, so the fur reads as thick and soft rather than photographic. The whole thing is one small warm shape resting on a quiet background.

It suits a bedroom, a nursery, a reading corner or a cabin, and it works on its own above a bed or a dresser. Pair it with the sleeping leopard or the gray horse from the same series if you want a trio of calm animals.
