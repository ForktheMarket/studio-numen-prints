This Discipline print is a black and white gym poster showing a slim teenager in a white T-shirt facing a mirror, where a stronger, older version of himself looks back.

We see the boy from behind, sharp and close, with narrow shoulders and short dark hair. In the glass, soft and slightly out of focus, stands a muscular fighter with a bare chest and loose trunks, hands at his sides. Heavy punch bags hang in the dark around them and a single lamp burns overhead. The word DISCIPLINE crosses the middle in thin white capitals with wide spacing.

The picture is about the gap between the two figures and the years of early mornings that close it. Nothing in it is loud. The light is low, the blacks are deep, and the only bright things are the lamp and the white shirt. It suits a home gym, a garage, a teenager's bedroom, a home office or a studio wall. It is a good match for boxers, lifters, runners, coaches and anyone who trains when nobody is watching.
