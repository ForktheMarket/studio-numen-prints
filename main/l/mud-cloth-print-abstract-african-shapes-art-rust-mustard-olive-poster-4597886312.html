This Mud Cloth print is an abstract poster of hand painted shapes, arches, half circles, stripes and zigzags, scattered loosely across a cream page, for anyone who wants earthy color without a single face or figure.

The shapes are blocked in with a dry brush so the edges stay rough, in rust, mustard, olive and black. Stacked pebbles, rainbow arches, fringed combs and bold slashes sit side by side with plenty of cream between them, loosely in the spirit of West African mud cloth.

The colors are rust, mustard, olive, black and cream, warm and grounded. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a dining room, and it pairs well with wood, linen, rattan and plants. A print for boho homes, mid century rooms and anyone who likes a pattern with a hand in it.
