This figs still life is a dark painting of ripe figs, some split open to show red flesh, made for kitchens, dining rooms and food lovers.

Whole purple figs sit at the back, and in front several are cut in half, showing their bright red and cream centers full of seeds. A few drops of juice lie on the dark green black surface. Warm light picks out the glossy skins. The glossy purple skins of the whole figs contrast with the soft, grainy texture of the cut halves, which look ready to eat.

Burgundy, red and black make it rich and late summer. It suits a dining room, a kitchen, a breakfast nook or a dark painted wall. It pairs well with the plums and grapes in the same series. The red centers glow like embers, which gives it warmth even in a dark room.
