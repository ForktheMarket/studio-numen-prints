This tin robot print is a bold orange and blue poster for a child's bedroom, a playroom or a game room.

A boxy wind-up robot stands at a slight angle on a solid bright orange background. It has two round cream eyes, a small triangle nose and a slot for a mouth, with a red dial on the side of its head, chunky red feet and a big red winding key in the middle of its chest.
