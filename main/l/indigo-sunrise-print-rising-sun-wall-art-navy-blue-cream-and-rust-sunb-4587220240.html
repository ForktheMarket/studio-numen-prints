This indigo sunrise print is a bold abstract of a rust red half sun with cream rays on deep navy blue, for bedrooms, living rooms and anyone who likes a warm start to the day.

A half circle of rust red sits at the bottom edge, like the sun just coming up. From it, thin cream rays fan out to every edge of the picture, close together and slightly uneven. Behind them the background is deep indigo blue. The sun has a faint woven texture and the rays have rough, chipped edges.

It is graphic and cheerful without being loud. The rays pull your eye upward, which helps a wall feel taller. Navy, cream and rust sit well with wood, leather, terracotta and white walls. It suits the wall above a bed, a living room, an entryway, a home office or a stair landing. It is a good match for people who like boho and mid century modern rooms, early risers and anyone who wants one strong picture instead of several small ones.
