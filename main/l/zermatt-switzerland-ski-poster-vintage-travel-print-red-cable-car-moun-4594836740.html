The Zermatt Switzerland ski poster is a vintage style travel print for skiers who know the view from the top station and want it on the wall at home.

A bright red cable car hangs from its wire high over a valley of snow dusted firs, heading toward the great white horn of the mountain behind it. The sky is a soft pale blue, the snow is painted in cool whites and blues, and the resort name sits in bold black capitals across the top. The single red cabin against all that cold color is what makes the print.

The palette is light and quiet, so it suits a bedroom, a hallway, a living room or a home office, and it works well above a bed or beside a window. It pairs with the other Alpine resorts in the set for a gallery wall of mountain towns.
