This Miss You Already print is a funny heron poster for anyone saying goodbye to a friend, a child leaving for college or a partner who lives too far away.

A gray heron stands side on, its thin neck rising from the bottom corner to a white head with a long, sharp orange beak. It wears tiny round black sunglasses, and a few wispy dark feathers trail from the back of its head. The words Miss you already... sit above it in a small serif. The bird is drawn in fine black ink with gray and white feathers, against a flat, bright lime green background.

The heron keeps a straight face, like someone trying hard not to show they care, and that is what makes the line land. The lime green is loud and cheerful, so the picture never turns sad. It suits a bedroom, a dorm room, a first apartment, a hallway or a home office. It works with white walls, black, pale wood and houseplants.
