This Throne print is a painterly portrait poster of a Black woman in a deep red gown seated on a carved wooden throne with her hands on the armrests, for anyone who wants their wall to say that this is her house.

She sits square and still, chin lifted, eyes lowered, gold earrings and a thin gold necklace catching the light. The gown plunges at the neck and spills over her knees and the sides of the chair in heavy folds of burgundy and maroon. Behind her the throne's carved posts rise against a plain cream wall.

The colors are burgundy, maroon, cream, gold and warm brown, dramatic and warm. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or an office, and it holds a wall on its own above a sofa or a bed. A print for women who run things and the people wise enough to know it.
