This pier print is a black and white photographic poster for a bedroom, a bathroom or a coastal home.

A long wooden jetty runs straight away from the viewer into calm, flat water, narrowing to a point just below the horizon. Weathered posts line both sides, large and dark in the foreground and shrinking to thin pegs in the distance, and the deck boards show every ridge and knot of the old wood. The water is pale silver and almost still, the sky is a soft overcast gray with a faint warm glow along the horizon, and the darkest tones sit in the shadowed sides of the posts. The composition is symmetrical, which gives it a quiet, orderly feel. The mood is early morning, nobody about.

It suits a bedroom, a bathroom or a hallway, above a bed or at the end of a corridor where the long perspective draws the eye. The grays go with white walls, driftwood, linen and pale blue, and it pairs with the lighthouse from the same series. It fits anyone who finds the coast calming.
