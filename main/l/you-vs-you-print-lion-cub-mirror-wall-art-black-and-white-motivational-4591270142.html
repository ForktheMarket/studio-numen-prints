This You vs You print is a black and white motivational poster showing a lion cub sitting in front of a round mirror, with a full grown lion looking back from the glass.

The cub sits with its back to us on worn wooden floorboards, ears up, tail curled to one side. The mirror in front of it is taller than the cub and has a heavy dark rim. In it, instead of a cub, there is an adult male with a thick mane and a calm, level stare aimed straight out of the picture. A soft light falls from above and the rest of the room drops away into black. The words YOU VS YOU. sit at the top in bold white capitals.

The idea is that the only real opponent is the one in the mirror, and the only comparison worth making is with who you were yesterday. The picture is dark and high in contrast, with fine detail in the fur, so it stands out most on a light wall. It suits a home gym, a home office, the wall above a desk, a garage, a study or a teen bedroom. It is a good match for lifters, runners, students and anyone working toward something.
