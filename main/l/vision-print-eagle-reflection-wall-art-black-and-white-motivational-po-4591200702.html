This Vision print is a black and white motivational poster showing a fluffy young eaglet on a rock, with a full grown eagle in flight reflected in the lake below.

The chick stands on a dark ledge at the water's edge, still covered in scruffy down, looking off to the side. Behind it, pale mountains fade into mist and bright cloud. In the calm water beneath, the reflection shows what it will be: an adult bald eagle with a white head and both wings spread wide, feathers fanned out like fingers. The word VISION sits across the rock in bold white capitals with wide spacing.

It is about seeing where you are headed before you get there. The top half of the picture is light and airy and the lower half is darker, so the spread wings stand out sharply. The palette is pure black, white and soft gray. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a meeting room, a study, a cabin or a teen bedroom. It is a good match for founders, managers, new graduates, bird lovers and anyone with a long term plan.
