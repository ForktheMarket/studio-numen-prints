This Dancer print is an abstract poster of a figure caught mid turn, the arms and legs drawn as repeating, overlapping arcs so the body seems to spin on the page, for anyone who wants movement on the wall rather than a still pose.

The arms sweep up and over the head in curved bands of ochre and olive, the torso is a narrow wedge of red and rust, and the skirt flares out into a wide circle of orange and tan. The legs taper down to a point in dark red and brown. Where the shapes cross, the colors darken, so the figure has depth without any outline. The background is a plain warm cream.

The colors are ochre, olive, orange, red and cream, warm and earthy. It suits a dance studio, a living room, a hallway or a bedroom, and it sits well above a sofa, a bench or a bed. A print for dancers, for anyone who loves music and motion, and for anyone who likes mid century abstraction with a figure hidden inside it.
