The orange circle print is a Bauhaus style poster for a living room, an office or a hallway that wants one bold shape on the wall.

A large circle in bright red orange sits just below the middle of a cream background. A thin black bar cuts across it at a diagonal, running from the lower left corner up through the circle and out the other side. Behind it, a tall upright column stands on the right, split lengthwise into deep navy and black, so the circle looks like it is hanging in front of a doorway. The colors are flat and clean with a faint printed texture, and the empty cream space around the shapes does most of the work.

It suits a living room, a home office, a dining room or a stairwell, above a sofa, over a sideboard or at the end of a hallway. Orange, navy, black and cream go well with white walls, oak, walnut, leather and gray linen. It fits anyone who likes a graphic, no fuss look, and it pairs with the other circle prints from the same series.
