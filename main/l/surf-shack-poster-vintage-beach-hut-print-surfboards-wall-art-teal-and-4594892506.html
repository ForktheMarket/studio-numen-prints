The Surf Shack poster is a vintage style beach print for anyone whose best days started at a wooden hut with boards lined up outside and sand on the floor.

Three longboards lean against a white weatherboard shack, one pale blue, one cream with stripes and one bright red, with palm shadows falling across them and a hand painted sign on the wall. The sky is flat teal and the lettering across the top is cream. It has the slightly faded ink of an old print and a lot of warmth in the red and sand tones.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a hallway or a beach house kitchen, and it works well above a toilet or a bath where a cheerful print earns its place. It also fits a gallery wall of coastal scenes, and it is the brightest print in the set.
