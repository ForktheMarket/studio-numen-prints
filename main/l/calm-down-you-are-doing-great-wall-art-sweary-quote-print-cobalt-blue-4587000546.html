This Calm Down You Are Doing Fucking Great print is a sweary pep talk poster for anyone who is harder on themselves than they need to be.

The whole poster is a solid cobalt blue with a narrow cream border around the edge. Seven words are brushed down the middle in cream capitals, one or two to a line, with a fast, dry brush that leaves streaks and feathered ends on every stroke. The letters lean a little and vary in width, so it looks written by hand in a hurry, the way a friend would say it.

It is kind and rude at once, which is why it works. The blue is strong enough to carry a plain white or gray wall and sits well with oak, denim and other blues. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a bedroom, a dressing area or a studio, anywhere a wobble tends to happen. It is a good one for a friend in a new job, a new parent or someone in the middle of exams.
