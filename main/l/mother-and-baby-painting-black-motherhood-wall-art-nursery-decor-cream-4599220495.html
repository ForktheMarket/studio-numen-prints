This mother and baby painting shows a young Black mother holding her child close, made for nurseries, family rooms and anyone who wants a tender portrait of motherhood on the wall.

She wears a cream head wrap with a few curls escaping and a large round gold earring, and she looks straight at us. Her baby sits on her hip, wrapped in the same cream cloth, leaning into her neck with wide brown eyes. Loose cream drapery fills the lower half of the canvas.

The colors are cream, soft gold and warm brown, quiet enough for a nursery and warm enough for a living room. It suits a wall above a crib or a nursing chair, a bedroom, or a family hallway. The baby's steady look back at the viewer gives it a small, real moment of character.
