This Mindset print is a black and white motivational poster showing a lion cub who looks into the water and sees a full grown lion looking back.

The cub stands at the edge of a waterhole on cracked, dry mud, head low, front paws together, staring down. Behind it the savanna is soft and out of focus, with two flat topped acacia trees under a heavy sky. In the still water below, the reflection is not a cub at all but an adult male with a full dark mane. The word MINDSET runs across the bank in clean white capitals with wide spacing.

The idea is simple. What you believe about yourself comes before what you become. The image is all grays, deep blacks and soft whites, with fine detail in the fur and whiskers, so it sits easily in a black and white gallery wall or on its own. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a home gym, a study, a teen bedroom or a team room. It is a good match for entrepreneurs, athletes, coaches, students and anyone working toward something bigger.
