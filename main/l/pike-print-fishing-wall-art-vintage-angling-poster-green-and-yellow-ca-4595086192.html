This pike print is a vintage fishing poster for a cabin, a den or anyone who fishes for pike.

A pike drives up through the print from bottom left to top right, long jaw open and one gold eye fixed ahead. Its back and head are a deep bottle green, its flanks carry rows of pale yellow oval spots, and its belly is a warm golden yellow that runs down to the tail. The fins and tail are green with yellow rays, and a few strokes of mint green water sit below the fish. The background is plain pale yellow, and the word PIKE is printed in dark green capitals along the bottom. The colors are flat with a little grain, like a poster from an old tackle shop.

It suits a cabin, a den, a hallway or a home office, above a desk or beside a bookcase. Green, yellow and cream go well with dark wood, leather, brass and forest green walls. It fits pike and predator anglers, and it pairs with the brown trout and the salmon from the same series.
