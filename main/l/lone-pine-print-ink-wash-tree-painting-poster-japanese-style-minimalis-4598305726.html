This Lone Pine print is an ink wash style landscape poster of one gnarled pine leaning out from a rocky ledge, with a misty forest floating behind it, for anyone who likes a picture with plenty of empty space.

The pine is the darkest thing on the page, its trunk twisting up from the lower left and its flat crown spreading toward the center. A tiny figure in red stands on the rock beneath it, barely bigger than a brushmark. The forest behind is a soft gray wash that dissolves into the warm cream sky. Most of the picture is deliberately left open.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a hallway, a study or a tea corner, and it is the simplest piece in the series. It suits a quiet corner better than a busy one, since the picture asks for a little space around it.
