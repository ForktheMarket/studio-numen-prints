This dandelion print is a black and white photographic poster for a bedroom, a bathroom or anyone who likes quiet, minimal walls.

A single dandelion seed head fills the upper right of the picture, shot close up against a plain white background. The stem is a deep black curve that enters from the bottom left and bends up to meet the head, and the seeds are soft gray with bright white highlights on their tips. A handful have already broken free and drift down to the left, each one a tiny black stalk with a fine parachute of hairs. Focus is sharp at the center and softens toward the edges, with almost no grain. The mood is still and light, like a held breath just before someone blows.

It suits a bedroom, a bathroom, a nursery or a hallway, above a bed or a dressing table. The white background goes with pale walls, light oak, linen and black metal, and it hangs easily beside the single tree or the flock of birds from the same series. It fits anyone who prefers calm, uncluttered rooms.
