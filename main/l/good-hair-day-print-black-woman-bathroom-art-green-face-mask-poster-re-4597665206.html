This Good Hair Day print is a retro bathroom poster of a Black woman in a mint green face mask and a towel turban, for anyone whose wash day is a whole ritual.

The crop is tight on her face, which fills most of the picture. Her skin is deep brown, the clay mask is pale green with soft brush marks, and her lips are a bright coral that pulls the eye straight to the center. A cream towel is wrapped high on her head with loose green strokes suggesting the folds. The background is a flat sage green with the words GOOD HAIR DAY stacked in heavy black capitals in the lower corner. It feels confident and a little amused, like she knows exactly how good she looks.

It suits a bathroom, a dressing table wall or the mirror corner of a bedroom, and it works in a hair salon or beauty room too. Green and coral sit well with white walls and wood.
