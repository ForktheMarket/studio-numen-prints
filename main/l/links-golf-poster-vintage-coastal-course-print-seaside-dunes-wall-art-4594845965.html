The Links Golf poster is a vintage style course print for golfers who would rather play in the wind by the sea than on a manicured parkland course.

A fairway in warm mustard yellow runs between banks of dark green marram grass and dunes, down toward a small flag on the green and a strip of gray sea beyond it. The sky is a pale cream wash that takes up the top half of the print, with the words Links Golf in wide dark green capitals along the bottom. The colors are limited to ochre, deep green, cream and one line of blue gray, which gives it the look of a railway poster from the 1930s.

It suits a study, a home office, a hallway, a living room or a holiday home, and it works well above a desk, a sofa or a sideboard. It pairs with the other prints in the set for a gallery wall of golf scenes.
