This owl writing a letter print is a woodland animal poster for home offices, studies and anyone who still likes to write things down by hand.

A round little owl sits at a dark wooden desk in a mustard yellow cardigan with brown buttons and blue cuffs. It wears round black glasses and looks straight at you over them with heavy, slightly tired eyes, as if you have interrupted. One wing holds a striped feather quill over a letter sealed with red wax. Behind its head is a soft cream arch on a cornflower blue wall.

The colors are cornflower blue, mustard, cream and dark brown, with the grainy texture of oil pastel. The mood is studious and a little dry. It is a good match for writers, teachers, students, letter writers and owl fans.
