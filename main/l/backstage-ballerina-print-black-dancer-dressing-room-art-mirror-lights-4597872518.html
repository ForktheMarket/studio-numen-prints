This Backstage Ballerina print is a painterly poster of a Black dancer sitting on a dressing room counter tying her pointe shoe, for anyone who loves the quiet minute before the music starts.

She sits side on in a cream tutu with her hair in a bun, one leg bent up as she knots the ribbon at her ankle. Behind her a row of round mirror bulbs glows along the wall, and warm peach light pours across the room from somewhere off the page. The counter top and the far wall fall into soft olive shadow.

The colors are peach, cream, olive and brown, lit like late afternoon. It suits a bedroom, a dressing area, a dance studio or a hallway, and it sits well above a bed or a vanity. A calm print for dancers, performers and anyone who knows the work happens before the curtain.
