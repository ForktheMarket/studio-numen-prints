The Air Dry print is a laundry room poster with a single wooden clothespin, for a utility room, a mudroom or a hallway cupboard that could do with a bit of calm.

One old fashioned wooden clothespin stands upright in the middle of a warm cream background, lit from the side so the grain and the little wire spring show. It is drawn in soft sepia browns with a slightly faded, printed look, like a page from an old hardware catalog. The words AIR DRY sit underneath in small, widely spaced black capitals. There is a lot of empty space, and that is the point.

It suits a laundry room, a utility room, a mudroom or a bathroom, above the washer, beside the drying rack or on the back of a door. Cream, brown and black go well with white walls, wicker baskets, linen and pale wood. It fits anyone who finds hanging out the washing oddly satisfying, and it pairs with the other prints in the same series.
