The Laundry Today or Naked Tomorrow print is a funny letterpress style poster for a laundry room, a utility room or a shared house.

The words LAUNDRY TODAY OR NAKED TOMORROW fill the page in heavy black condensed capitals, stacked on four lines with the small OR tucked in the middle. Each letter has a speckled, inked texture and a mustard yellow drop shadow that makes the type look pressed into the page. Around the edge runs a hand drawn hatched border in the same mustard yellow, with a ribbon banner across the top and another across the bottom. The background is warm cream. It suits a laundry room, a utility room, a mudroom, a downstairs toilet or the wall above a washing machine.
