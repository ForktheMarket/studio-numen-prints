This river delta map print shows a great river splitting into hundreds of branching channels as it reaches the sea, drawn as a vintage chart, made for offices, studies and modern homes that like a map with an abstract feel.

The colors are gentle, mostly cream, sage and pale blue, so it works in a calm bedroom or living room as well as a desk wall.
