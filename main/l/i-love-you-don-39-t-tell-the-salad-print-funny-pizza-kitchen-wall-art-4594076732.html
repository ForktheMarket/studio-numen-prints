This I Love You, Don't Tell the Salad print is a funny two panel comic poster for pizza lovers and anyone whose healthy eating plan has a well known weak spot.

In the top panel a small yellow egg-shaped character hugs a slice of pepperoni pizza with its eyes closed, three red hearts floating above. The caption reads I LOVE YOU. In the bottom panel it is still holding the slice, but now it is glancing sideways at a bowl of green salad sitting on the floor. The salad has a face, and it looks hurt. The caption reads DON'T TELL THE SALAD. Thick black outlines and flat color sit in two warm cream panels on a watermelon red background.

It reads like a tiny love triangle, and the sad salad gets most of the laughs. It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a breakfast nook, the wall above a table or a shared apartment where pizza night is a fixed event. Red and cream go well with white tile, wood and green plants.
