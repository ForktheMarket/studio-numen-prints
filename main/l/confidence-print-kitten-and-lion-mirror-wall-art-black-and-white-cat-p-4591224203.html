This Confidence print is a black and white poster showing a small tabby kitten sitting in front of a tall mirror, with a full-grown lion looking back from the glass.

The kitten sits on bare wooden floorboards with its back to us, ears up, whiskers catching the light, tail curled behind. In the mirror the reflection is a male lion with a heavy mane, sitting in the same pose, chin slightly raised. Daylight falls in from the left and leaves the rest of the room in soft gray. The word CONFIDENCE sits low in the picture in white serif capitals with wide spacing.

It is an old idea, that how you see yourself counts for more than how big you are, and the kitten gets it across without any effort. People smile at it first and think about it second. The tones are gentle and silvery, which keeps it calm on the wall. It suits a bedroom, a nursery, a kids room, a home office, a hallway or a dressing area. It is a good match for cat owners, teenagers, new starters, public speakers and anyone working on their self belief.
