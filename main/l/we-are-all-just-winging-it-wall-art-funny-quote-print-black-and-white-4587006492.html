This We Are All Just Winging It print is a funny black and white quote poster for anyone who has noticed that nobody else has it figured out either.

Six words are painted in tall black brush capitals across three lines on a warm cream background. A border of loose black dabs runs around all four edges, each one a quick press of the brush, some solid and some dry and broken. The letters are narrow and a little uneven, heavy with paint in the middle and scratchy where the brush lifted off. It looks like a sign someone painted in one go and did not fuss over, which suits what it says.

The line is a relief more than a joke. The new parents, the boss, the person who seems to have every answer: all of them are working it out as they go. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits almost any color scheme and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a living room, an entryway, a home office, the wall above a desk, a kitchen or a staff room. It is a good match for new parents, first-time managers, students, small business owners and anyone who has ever nodded along in a meeting.
