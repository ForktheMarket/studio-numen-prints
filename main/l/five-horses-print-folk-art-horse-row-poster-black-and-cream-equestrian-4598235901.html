This Five Horses print is a folk art poster of five tall horses standing in a line facing the viewer, their long legs forming a row of black and cream stripes across the whole picture, for anyone who likes pattern that turns out to be something.

From a distance it reads as vertical bars. Up close, each bar is a horse seen head on, with a dark blocky head, two pricked ears and a pale chest, and the legs below drawn in alternating black and cream so the row becomes a rhythm. The first horse is painted cream against the sand ground, the other four are black. The whole thing sits on a warm beige wash.

The colors are black, cream, beige and sand, graphic and calm. It suits a hallway, a living room above the sofa, a dining room or a landing, and it works well in farmhouse, Scandinavian and neutral modern rooms. A good piece for anyone who wants horses without the picture looking like a stable.
