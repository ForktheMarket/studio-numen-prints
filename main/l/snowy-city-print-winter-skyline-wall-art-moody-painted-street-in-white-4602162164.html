This snowy city print shows a wide avenue in falling snow, with tall towers fading into a pale blue white sky, made for living rooms, bedrooms and apartments.

Everything is painted in white, silver and soft blue, from the snow covered street to the skyscrapers that dissolve into the sky. A couple walks away under one umbrella, dark against the white, and a few cars and shop windows add small touches of warm orange. The brushwork is loose and cold, with the feel of a day when the city goes quiet.

The palette is light and airy, unusual for a city scene, so it works in bright modern rooms and pale neutral bedrooms where darker cityscapes would feel heavy. It suits anyone who loves winter in a big city, especially the first snow. Hung beside one of the warmer night scenes it makes a strong pair of opposites.
