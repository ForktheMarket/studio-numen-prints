This Ambition print is a black and white sports poster showing a young boy in a mud-covered soccer uniform, standing at the edge of a puddle with the ball under his arm.

He is seen from the side, head bowed, shirt and shorts streaked with dirt, socks pulled up over muddy cleats. The field around him is torn up and waterlogged. Behind him stand an empty goal and two sets of floodlights glowing through an overcast sky. The lower half of the picture is the puddle, which holds his full reflection upside down, ball and all. The word AMBITION crosses the middle in bold white capitals with wide spacing.

This is the moment after everyone else has gone home. It is about the hours nobody sees, the wet evenings and the extra practice. The low camera angle lets a small boy stand tall, and the reflection doubles him. It suits a boys room, a teenager's bedroom, a game room, a home office, a clubhouse or a locker room. It is a good match for young players, coaches, soccer parents and anyone who grew up kicking a ball in the rain.
