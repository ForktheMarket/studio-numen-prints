This otter with a soup bowl print is a cozy kitchen poster for home cooks, soup lovers and anyone who thinks dinner should smell good before it tastes good.

An otter in a thick red knitted sweater holds a bowl of chunky vegetable soup in both dark paws. Its head is tipped back, its eyes are closed and its nose is lifted to catch a thin curl of steam, with long white whiskers fanning out on each side. The bowl is dark outside and yellow inside, filled with orange broth and pale yellow cubes. The background is a flat, warm peach orange.
