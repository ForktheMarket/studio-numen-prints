This gold hoop portrait is a tight close up of a Black woman's face in profile, made for bathrooms, bedrooms, dressing areas, beauty salons and makeup studios.

The crop is extreme. We see one eye, a cheek, the edge of her lips and a large gold hoop earring that swings almost out of view. Warm light catches her brow, cheekbone and lips in glossy highlights, and the canvas texture shows through the paint. The background is dark, so the skin and gold carry the whole image.

The palette is rich brown, black and gold, which suits warm, moody rooms, dark paint colors and brass fittings. Hang it above a vanity, beside a mirror, or in a salon or studio waiting area. Up close you see every brushstroke; from across the room it reads almost like a photograph.
