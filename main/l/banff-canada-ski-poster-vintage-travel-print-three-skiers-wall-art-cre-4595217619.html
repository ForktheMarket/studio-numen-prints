The Banff Canada ski poster is a vintage style travel print for skiers who would rather go down the mountain with friends than alone.

Three skiers drop down an open slope in a line, one in dark green, one in mustard yellow and one in red, all crouched low over their skis with poles trailing behind them. Their long shadows stretch across the snow, and the slope is a plain warm cream against a single wedge of soft gray blue sky. The resort name sits in heavy black capitals at the base. It has the clean, sunlit feel of a 1930s Canadian railway poster, with more empty space than most, which is what makes it so calm on a wall.

The cream, blue and red palette suits a living room, a hallway, a home office or a chalet style bedroom, and it works well above a bed or a sofa. It pairs with the Aspen and European resorts in the set for a gallery wall of mountain towns.
