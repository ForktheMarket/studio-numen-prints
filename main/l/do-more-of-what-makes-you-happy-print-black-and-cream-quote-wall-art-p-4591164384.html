This Do More of What Makes You Happy print is a black and cream quote poster for anyone who keeps putting the good stuff at the bottom of the list.

The seven words are painted in big cream brush capitals across four lines on a solid black background, and they fill almost the whole poster. A small painted sun sits beside the last word, a round dab with short rays around it. The letters are thick and slightly wonky, with streaks inside each stroke where the bristles dragged, so it looks like a sign lettered by hand in one go.

It is a plain instruction and a cheerful one. More walks, more music, more long lunches, whatever your version is. The cream is warm, close to the color of butter, which keeps the black from feeling heavy. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a living room, a home office, the wall above a desk, a kitchen or an entryway. It is a good match for retirees, people starting a new chapter, busy friends and anyone who works too much.
