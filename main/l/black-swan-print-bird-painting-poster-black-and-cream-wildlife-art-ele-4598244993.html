This Black Swan print is a painterly bird poster of a black swan in profile, its long neck curved into a question mark and its red beak tipped down, set against a soft cream ground, for anyone who wants an elegant piece with one sharp point of color.

The neck rises from the body in a smooth dark curve, the feathers along it tight and velvety, and the head turns to show a single red eye and the bright coral beak. The body below is painted in loose dark strokes with the wing feathers ruffled up along the back. The background is a pale warm cream that darkens to gray at the bottom corner.

The colors are black, charcoal, cream, soft gray and red, elegant and restrained. It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a bathroom or a dressing room, and it sits well with black and white rooms, marble and brass. A good piece for anyone who likes birds or simply likes a clean, graceful shape.
