This Dancers Pattern print is a poster of dozens of small Black women leaping and stretching in pink leotards and maroon tights, repeated across a cream page, for anyone who wants a wall full of movement.

Each figure is painted in a few quick strokes, arms out, one leg kicked back, hair flying, with a tiny flash of yellow at a heel here and there. The rows shift as they go so the whole page seems to be in motion, like a flip book spread flat.

The colors are cream, blush pink, maroon and warm brown, soft but busy. It suits a dance studio, a girls bedroom, a yoga room, a hallway or a gallery wall, and the repeat breaks up a run of single portraits. A print for dancers, teachers and anyone who likes a pattern with a pulse.
