This North America map print is a vintage style relief map of the whole continent, from Alaska and Hudson Bay down to Mexico and the Caribbean, made for offices, studies and dens.

The land is drawn on aged parchment in soft tan and sand, with the Rockies, the Sierra Nevada and the Appalachians shaded as fine ridged relief so they stand out from the plains. Rivers wind across the map as thin blue threads, the Great Lakes sit in deep teal, and the surrounding sea is a pale wash of blue. A small compass rose rests in the lower left corner.

The palette is warm cream, tan and slate, so it works in a classic study, a modern office or a hallway gallery wall. It suits anyone who loves road trips, national parks or the shape of the continent itself.
