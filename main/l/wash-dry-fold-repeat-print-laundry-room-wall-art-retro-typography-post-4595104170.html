The Wash Dry Fold Repeat print is a bold letterpress style poster for a laundry room, a utility room or a mudroom.

Four words sit stacked down the page in heavy navy blue capitals: WASH, DRY, FOLD, REPEAT. The letters are big and square, with worn edges and tiny white flecks where the ink seems to have missed, so they look like old wood type pulled by hand. The background is a plain warm cream with nothing else on it, which keeps the whole thing calm despite the heavy type. Navy and cream is a quiet combination, and the print reads clearly from the far end of a hallway.

It suits a laundry room, a utility room, a mudroom or a bathroom, hung above the washing machine, beside the dryer or over a row of hooks. Navy goes with white tile, pale oak, brass fixtures, striped towels and butcher block countertops. It fits new homeowners, busy parents and anyone who likes a tidy routine spelled out on the wall.
