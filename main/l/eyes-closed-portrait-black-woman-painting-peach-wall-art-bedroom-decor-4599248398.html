This eyes closed portrait is a warm painting of a young Black woman with her face tipped up toward the sun, made for bedrooms, bathrooms and quiet corners.

Her curls are pinned up loosely with a few strands falling around her ear and neck. The light comes from above and to one side, catching her forehead, nose and lips in soft highlights. The background is a flat peach, painted with loose strokes, so the whole picture glows. The skin is painted with real care, warm browns and golds with small glints of light on the cheek and collarbone.

The colors are peach, honey and deep brown. It has the feeling of a slow, sunny morning with nowhere to be. Hang it above a bed or a dresser, beside a window, or in a bathroom where you want something calm to look at.
