This Reading Outdoors print is a minimalist poster of a Black woman in a blue head wrap reading on a wooden bench in an open field, for anyone who likes a book best in fresh air.

She sits in profile in a cream dress with a cobalt blue wrap over her lap, holding an orange book, with a single round tree on the horizon. The sky is a wide wash of peach, the field is layered in bands of sage and olive, and the grass in the foreground is painted in loose strokes.

Most of the print is empty space, which makes it restful on a wall and easy to live with. Peach, cobalt, sage and cream suit a bedroom, a hallway, a nursery or a calm living room, and it sits well above a bed or a reading chair.
