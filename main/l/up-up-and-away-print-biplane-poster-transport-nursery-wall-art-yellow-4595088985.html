The Up Up and Away print is a retro biplane poster for a kids room, a nursery or anyone who looks up when they hear a plane.

A small yellow biplane climbs toward the top right corner of a pale turquoise sky, leaving a loose dotted trail of smoke behind it from the bottom left. The sky is flat and slightly mottled like old printed paper, and the plane is the only object in it, so it feels high up and far away. The words UP UP AND AWAY fill the bottom half in heavy black capitals, with a cream border around the whole thing.

It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or a landing, and works just as well over a crib as above a child's desk. Turquoise, yellow and cream go well with white walls, pale wood, gray and navy. It fits children who run with their arms out and parents who like a calm, simple picture on the wall.
