This plaster squares print shows a rough rectangle of darker plaster set inside a lighter one, set inside another, like three layers of an old wall, made for living rooms, offices and calm modern homes.

The surface is coarse and chalky throughout, in soft warm grays and off whites, with the edges of each rectangle slightly raised and uneven where the plaster has been troweled. The smallest rectangle near the center is the warmest in tone, a hint of pale sand. Light falls from the upper left, so the texture shows in fine shadow.

The palette is gray, bone and cream, quiet and architectural. It suits a home office, a hallway or a living room with concrete, oak or linen, and anyone who likes geometric art softened by a handmade surface. It pairs well with the black square and single line prints from the same set for a trio of quiet geometry.
