This running horse print is a black and white photographic poster for a bedroom, a living room or a horse lover.

A black horse gallops from left to right through shallow water on a beach, caught mid stride with its mane and tail flying. It is a near solid silhouette, pure black against a bright white sky, with a few highlights on the curve of its back and the muzzle. The water around its hooves blurs into soft streaks of silver and gray, and a faint reflection smears across the wet sand below. The horizon sits a third of the way down, so most of the picture is sky and pale water. The grain is fine, the contrast strong, and the mood is all speed and freedom.

It suits a bedroom, a living room or a hallway, above a bed or a console. Black and white goes with almost anything, and looks especially good with oak, leather, cream walls and dark metal. It pairs with the lighthouse from the same series. It fits riders, horse owners and anyone who likes some motion on the wall.
