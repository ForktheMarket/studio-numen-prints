The Bondi Beach surf poster is a vintage style travel print for surfers, for Sydney expats and for anyone who wants a bit of Australian sunshine on the wall.

Five striped longboards stand upright in pale sand, leaning gently against each other, in yellow, coral, teal, orange and cream with pinstripes down the middle of each one. The background is a plain warm cream with soft footprints in the sand at the bottom, and the words Bondi Beach run across the top in dark teal capitals. The colors are faded in the way an old surf shop sign fades after a few summers.

It suits a bathroom, a child's bedroom, a hallway, a beach house or a laundry room, and it works well above a bath, a bed or a bench. It pairs with the Newquay, Biarritz and Nazare prints for a wall of surf towns.
