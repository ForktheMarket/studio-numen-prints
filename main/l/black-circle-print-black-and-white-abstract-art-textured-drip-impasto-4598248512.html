This Black Circle print is a black and white textured abstract poster of one giant smudged black disc pushed into the top of a cream ground, with two long drips running down from its edge, for anyone who wants a bold monochrome piece with some roughness to it.

The circle is scraped on in charcoal and black with the canvas weave showing through, its lower edge breaking into glossy black ridges and torn flecks. Thick cream paint is heaped below and to the right, and a second smaller black shape sits in the bottom corner. The drips are thin and straight, like the paint was still wet when it was hung.

The colors are black, charcoal, cream and off white, with no color at all, so it suits any room. It fits a living room above the sofa, a home office, a hallway or a dining room, and it works with black frames, concrete, oak and anything neutral.
