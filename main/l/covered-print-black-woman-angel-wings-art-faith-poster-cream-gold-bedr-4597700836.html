This Covered print is a faith poster of a Black woman wrapped in soft cream angel wings, for anyone who finds comfort in the promise of being sheltered.

She is seen from behind with her head turned, eyes closed, dark curls catching the light, bare shoulders draped in a fold of cream cloth. Large feathered wings curve around her from above and behind, painted in warm ivory, honey and pale gold with visible brush strokes. The word COVERED sits in thin white serif capitals across her shoulders.

The palette is all warm neutrals, cream, gold and brown, so it feels calm and glowing rather than loud. It suits a bedroom, a nursery, a hallway or a quiet corner of a living room, and it works well above a bed. A tender print for mothers, new homes and anyone who leans on Psalm 91.
