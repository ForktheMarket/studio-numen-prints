This Mars print is a retro red planet poster for a kids bedroom, a playroom or a space themed nursery.

The word MARS sits top right in chunky cream capitals against a deep teal sky with faint stars. A huge orange planet rises behind a sloping orange ground that fills the bottom third of the picture, streaked with dark shadows and rocks. A small six wheeled rover with a camera mast sits on the slope, tiny against the planet behind it. Two cream bands run down the left and right edges like the margins of an old travel poster. The colors are grainy and speckled, in teal, orange, cream and near black, and the mood is calm and a little lonely.

It suits a boys bedroom, a playroom, a study corner or a hallway, and it looks right above a desk or a bed. Teal, orange and cream go with white walls, oak furniture, terracotta pots and denim, and it pairs with the Saturn and earth prints from the same series. It fits children who know the names of the planets.
