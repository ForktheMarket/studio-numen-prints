This pewter jug and lemons painting is a dark still life of an old metal jug with three lemons, one peeled, made for kitchens, dining rooms and lovers of old master style.

The jug is dented, tarnished pewter with a curved handle, catching soft reflections. In front of it on a dark wood table are whole lemons, a cut half and a curl of peel. The background is close to black. The peel of the lemon curls in a long spiral across the table, and small flecks of light dance across the worn surface of the jug.

Bright lemon yellow against silver and black gives it a fresh, sharp look. It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a breakfast nook, or a pantry. It pairs well with the copper pot and oysters in the same series.
