This salmon print is a vintage fishing poster for a lodge, a cabin or anyone who fishes for salmon.

An Atlantic salmon fills the upper half of the print, in profile and facing left, so large that its tail runs off the right edge. Its back is a deep blue black fading to silver blue scales flecked with dark spots, and its lower jaw is hooked up into the kype of a cock fish. The belly and chin are warm salmon pink. Behind it a pale blue green sky sits over low hills in pink and orange, then a strip of still water in bands of pale and deeper blue. The word SALMON is printed in large navy capitals on the cream band at the bottom. It is painted in flat blocks of color like a railway poster from the thirties.

It suits a lodge, a dining room, a hallway or a study, above a sideboard or a fireplace. Blue, peach and cream go well with tartan, dark wood, brass and stone. It fits salmon anglers, and it pairs with the brown trout from the same series.
