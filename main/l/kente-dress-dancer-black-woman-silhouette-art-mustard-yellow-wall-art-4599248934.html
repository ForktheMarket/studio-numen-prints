This kente dress dancer print shows a Black woman in silhouette with her arms raised, made for living rooms, entryways and anyone who loves bold pattern and color.

Her figure is a flat black shape with one gold hoop earring, set against a warm mustard yellow background. Her long dress is built from strips of striped kente style pattern in red, orange, green, blue and black that flare out wide at the hem as she moves. The pose is open and joyful, with her head turned in profile and her hands lifted high as if she has just heard her favorite song.

It has the look of cut shapes laid down by hand, clean and graphic but full of energy. The mustard and red tones warm up a room fast. Hang it above a sofa or a console, in a hallway, or in a bright bedroom.
