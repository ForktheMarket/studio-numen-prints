This Mountain Road print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a pale road winding up through foggy pine forest toward a hidden peak, for anyone who likes a picture with a path in it.

The road enters at the bottom right and switches back up the slope, getting fainter as it climbs. The pines on either side are painted in dark layered ink, and above them ridge after ridge fades into the mist until the last peak is barely a shadow against the cream sky. The light is soft and even, as if it were early morning.

It suits an office, a hallway, a living room or a landing, and it pairs well with the misty mountains and rice terraces prints from the same series. It also reads well from a distance, since the road gives the eye a clear line to follow.
