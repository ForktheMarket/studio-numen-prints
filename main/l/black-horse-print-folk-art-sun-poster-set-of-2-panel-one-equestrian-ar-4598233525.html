This Black Horse print is panel one of a pair, a folk art poster of a single tall black horse facing right under a small red sun, standing on a strip of golden sand against a cream sky, for anyone who wants a matched pair of prints for either side of a bed or a doorway.

The horse is a flat black silhouette with long legs, a raised head and a wispy tail, its paint scuffed at the edges so it feels hand done. The sun is a small rust red disc high in the top right corner. The sky is a pale plastered cream and the ground is brushed in warm ochre and gold.

The colors are black, cream, ochre and rust red, calm and earthy. It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a hallway or a landing, and it is designed to hang beside its partner, which shows the same horse facing left. Together they frame a space and face each other.
