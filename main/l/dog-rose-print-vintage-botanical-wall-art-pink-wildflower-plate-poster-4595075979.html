The dog rose print is a vintage botanical plate poster for a bedroom, a living room or anyone who loves an English hedgerow.

One large open dog rose sits in the center of the plate, five wide pale pink petals around a crown of golden stamens. Above it to the left is a tight crimson bud with green sepals folded around it, and to the right a glossy red rose hip. Dark green toothed leaflets sit in groups around the flower, and a thorny brown stem curves down to the left. The ground is a warm aged cream, and the words DOG ROSE are lettered in small spaced capitals at the bottom. It has the fine lines of an old engraved plate, with bud, flower and fruit together.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a hallway or a bathroom, and it sits beside the foxglove and honeysuckle from the same series. Pale pink, crimson and dark green go well with white walls, oak, linen and sage green, and it fits gardeners and anyone who walks the lanes in June.
