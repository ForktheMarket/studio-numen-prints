This black and white pier print shows a long wooden dock running straight out into thick fog until it disappears, made for lake houses, coastal homes and quiet rooms.

The weathered planks start large at the bottom of the picture and narrow toward a vanishing point in the mist. Wooden posts line both sides. Above and around it everything is soft white fog, so the dock seems to lead into nothing. The wood grain on each plank is sharp up close and softens with distance, which makes the fog feel even thicker.

The strong perspective pulls the eye in, and the fog keeps it calm. Gray, white and black make it easy to place in any room. Hang it in a living room, a bedroom, an office, a hallway, or a lake cabin. It works especially well on a long narrow wall.
