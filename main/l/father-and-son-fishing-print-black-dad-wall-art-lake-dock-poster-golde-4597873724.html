This Father and Son Fishing print is a painterly poster of a Black dad and his little boy sitting on the end of a wooden dock with one rod between them, for anyone whose best memories were made waiting for a bite.

The father sits with his legs over the edge in a plaid shirt and tan trousers, rod held out over the water. The boy sits close beside him in a yellow tee and a red cap, watching the line. Behind them the sky is a wide wash of golden yellow with soft clouds, and the water below is deep blue with light on the ripples.

The colors are gold, cream, slate blue and warm brown, lit like early evening. It suits a living room, a boys bedroom, a cabin, a hallway or a dad's office, and it sits well above a sofa or a bed. A still print for fathers, sons and anyone who learned patience on a dock.
