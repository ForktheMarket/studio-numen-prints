The rainy window print is a moody painted poster for a reading nook, a bedroom or anyone who likes a wet afternoon indoors.

A tall arched window fills the middle of the picture, its glazing bars dividing the glass into small panes with a fan across the top. Rain streaks every pane, and beyond it the garden is a wall of soft green leaves. In front of it a wooden spindle back armchair with a green cushion faces left, and a small side table holds a lamp with a warm orange shade, the only bright light in the room. Dark green curtains hang at both sides and a worn rust rug covers the floor. The room is painted almost black, so the garden and the lamp carry the picture. The brushwork is soft, with small flecks of white for the rain.

It suits a bedroom, a reading corner or a study, above a bed or an armchair. The deep green, rust and black go with dark wood, velvet and brass. It fits readers and homebodies, and it pairs with the reading table from the same series.
