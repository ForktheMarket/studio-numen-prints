This Be Proud of How Far You've Come print is a dark red and cream quote poster for anyone who keeps looking at how far there is to go and forgets to look back.

The seven words are painted in cream block capitals across four lines on a deep red background with a fine woven texture, like painted canvas. A period closes the last line, with a tiny cream flower beside it. The letters are square and solid, closer to an old shop sign than to handwriting, and each one shows the ridges the bristles left in the paint.

The message is about counting progress. The exam passed, the miles run, the year survived, the habit dropped. It is steady and warm without being loud. The deep red brings color to a neutral room and sits well beside wood, cream, black and brass. It suits a bedroom, a home office, the wall above a desk, a hallway, a home gym or a dorm room. It is a good match for graduates, runners, people in a new job and anyone who has worked hard to get where they are.
