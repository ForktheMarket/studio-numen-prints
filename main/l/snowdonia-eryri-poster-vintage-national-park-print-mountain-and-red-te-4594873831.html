The Snowdonia poster is a vintage style national park print for anyone who has pitched a tent by a dark Welsh lake and watched the light go off the summit.

A snow capped peak rises from a still black lake under a pale yellow sky, and a single red tent sits on the far shore, tiny against the mountain. Eryri, the Welsh name, sits above Snowdonia in bold cream and dark green lettering. The whole print is built from deep green, cream and one small dot of red, which gives it a quiet, cold morning feel.

It suits a hallway, a bedroom, a living room or a home office, and it works well above a bed or a desk. It is a good match for walkers and campers, and it sits with the other parks in the set for a gallery wall.
