The honeysuckle print is a vintage botanical plate poster for a hallway, a kitchen or anyone who loves a scented summer evening.

A curling honeysuckle stem leans in from the left and opens into a crowded head of flowers on the right. The open flowers are long orange red tubes that split into a creamy yellow lip, with thin stamens reaching out past the petals, and a few paler buds are still closed at the top. Four broad dark green leaves sit in pairs along the vine, and fine tendrils twist out from the stem in loose coils. The ground is a plain warm cream, and the word HONEYSUCKLE is lettered in small spaced orange capitals at the bottom. It has the fine lines and soft shading of an old engraved plate.

It suits a hallway, a kitchen, a dining room or a sunroom, and it hangs well beside the dog rose and foxglove from the same series. Orange, cream and deep green go well with white walls, oak, terracotta and rattan, and it fits gardeners and anyone with honeysuckle over the back door.
