This fly box print is a vintage fly fishing poster for a study, a cabin or a fly tying corner.

Eight fishing flies sit in a grid of eight colored panels, two across and four down, with the words FLY BOX in dark green capitals across the middle. The panels are orange red, olive green, navy and mustard yellow. At the top a yellow hackle fly and an olive winged fly sit on orange, then a dark nymph on olive and a white and orange fly on navy. Below the title are a black streamer, an orange and white hackle fly, a white bushy streamer and an olive and yellow fly on mustard. Each fly has loose painted feathers and a plain black hook. It reads like a page from an old tackle catalog.

It suits a study, a hallway, a den or a cabin, above a desk or a fly tying bench. Orange, navy, olive and mustard go well with oak, cane, wool and green walls. It fits fly fishers and fly tiers, and it pairs with the dry fly print from the same series.
