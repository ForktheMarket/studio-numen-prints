This golden retriever print is a bright retro poster for retriever owners, family kitchens and anyone who has thrown the same ball a thousand times.

The dog looks up at you with a tennis ball held in its mouth, head and chest filling a square of marigold yellow. The portrait is cut into three flat tones, dark teal for the shadow side of the face, the nose and the ear, cream for the lit side and the chest fur, and the yellow of the background showing through as a mid tone. Below the square the words GOLDEN RETRIEVER are set in tall condensed teal capitals on a cream margin. The mood is pure retriever, pleased with itself and ready to go again.

It suits a kitchen, a mudroom, a playroom or a hallway, hung near the back door or above the spot where the leads live. Yellow and teal go with white walls, pale wood, navy and rattan, and it fits any family whose dog greets them at the door. It pairs with the other breeds in the same series.
