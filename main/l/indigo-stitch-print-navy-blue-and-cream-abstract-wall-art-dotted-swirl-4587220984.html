This indigo stitch print is an abstract of cream dotted lines curving across deep navy blue, with one arc picked out in red, in the style of hand-stitched indigo cloth, for living rooms, bedrooms and anyone who likes dark, quiet wall art.

Hundreds of short cream dashes run in tight curved rows, like lines of running stitch. In the center they wind inward to form a large whorl, a little like a fingerprint or the rings of a tree. More sets of arcs sweep in from each corner and overlap it. One row on the left of the whorl is red instead of cream, and a few red dots turn up again near the bottom right. The background is a very dark indigo with a faint woven texture.

It is detailed but restful, and the red gives your eye one place to land. Dark navy, cream and a little red sit well with wood, leather, white walls and plain linen. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a home office, a hallway or a reading corner. It is a good match for people who sew or quilt, people who like Japanese textiles and anyone who wants a dark picture on a pale wall.
