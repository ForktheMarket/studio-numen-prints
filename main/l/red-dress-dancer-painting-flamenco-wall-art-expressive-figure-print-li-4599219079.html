This red dress dancer print shows a woman caught mid turn, made for anyone who loves dance, movement and a strong splash of color on a wall.

She is painted from behind with her bare back to us, one arm bent at the hip and her face turned over her shoulder. Her skirt breaks into wide, loose sweeps of scarlet paint that spiral out across the page, as if the brush moved with her. The background is a plain warm sand, so the red does all the work and the figure stays light and quick.

It has the energy of a flamenco floor without any clutter. Hang it in a living room above the sofa, in a hallway that needs one bold piece, in a bedroom, or in a dance studio or practice space where it will be seen every day.
