This Two Cranes print is a painterly bird poster of a pair of cranes standing close together in still water, necks crossed, for couples, bird lovers and anyone who wants a calm wall.

The birds are painted in pale gray and white with dark legs and a small red cap on each head, the only warm note in the picture. Behind them the fog is a wash of silver blue that fades into the water, so there is no horizon and no background detail, just the two birds and their faint reflections. The feeling is quiet and a little formal.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a bathroom, an entrance hall or anywhere you want soft blue and gray, and the pair of birds gives it an obvious place in a couple's room. The blue gray also sits easily beside linen, pale oak and white tile.
