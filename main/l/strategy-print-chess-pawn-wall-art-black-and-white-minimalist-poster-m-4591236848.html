This Strategy print is a black and white poster showing a single dark chess pawn on a wooden board, lit by a hard beam of light and throwing a long shadow.

The pawn stands just off center, turned from dark wood with a visible grain and a small shine on its rounded head. A diagonal band of sunlight crosses the board, picking out the pale and dark squares, and the shadow stretches down and left toward the viewer, much longer than the piece itself. Everything above the board is solid black. The word STRATEGY sits low in the picture in thin white serif capitals with wide spacing.

A pawn is the smallest piece, and the only one that can become something else if it gets to the far side. That is the idea, and the long shadow hints at it. It is the most graphic picture in the set, close to abstract, with clean shapes and very strong contrast. It suits a home office, a study, a library corner, a game room, a meeting room or a den. It is a good match for chess players, founders, managers, students and anyone who thinks a few moves ahead.
