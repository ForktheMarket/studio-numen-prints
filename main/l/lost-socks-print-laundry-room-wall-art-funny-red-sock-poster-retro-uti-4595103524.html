The Lost Socks print is a funny retro poster for a laundry room, a utility room or anyone who is always one sock short.

A single red sock hangs from a thin black washing line that runs the full width of a cream background, held up by one small wooden clothespin. The sock droops toward the bottom edge, its toe just brushing a faint scribble of shadow, and the red is shaded darker at the heel and cuff so it looks like a screen print of an old photograph. Underneath, the words LOST SOCKS sit in heavy black capitals with a speckled, inked texture. Nothing else is in the picture, which is the joke.

It suits a laundry room, a utility room, a mudroom or a downstairs toilet, hung above the washing machine or beside the door. Red and cream go with white tile, navy cabinets, pale wood and terracotta floors. It fits busy families, people with a dry sense of humor. It pairs with the Sorting Out Life socks from the same series.
