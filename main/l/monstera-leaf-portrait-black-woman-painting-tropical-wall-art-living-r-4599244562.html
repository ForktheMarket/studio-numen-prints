This monstera leaf portrait is a painting of a Black woman half hidden behind one giant tropical leaf, made for living rooms, plant filled spaces, sunrooms and warm modern interiors.

She tips her head back with heavy lidded eyes, her natural curls pinned up and gold disc earrings hanging against her neck. The monstera leaf curves across her shoulders like a collar, dark glossy green with its familiar split holes and pale green veins. Behind her the background is a flat warm mustard.

The mix of mustard, deep green and bronze is bold but easy to live with, especially alongside real plants, rattan and wood. Hang it above a sofa or console, in a bright sunroom, or in a bedroom that needs color. The pose is relaxed and a little dreamy, like a warm late afternoon.
