This Still Dancing print is a painterly poster of an elderly Black couple slow dancing cheek to cheek, his hair white and hers silver, for anyone who hopes to be exactly this in fifty years.

He holds her hand up and his other arm around her waist, in a cream shirt and dark trousers. She leans into him in a long navy dress that swings out at the hem in strokes of blue and cream, one heel lifted. Behind them is nothing but a wide wash of golden yellow and their shadows on the floor.

The colors are gold, navy, cream and warm brown, warm and tender. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a dining room, and it sits well above a bed or a sofa. A print for long marriages, golden anniversaries and grandparents who still clear the floor.
