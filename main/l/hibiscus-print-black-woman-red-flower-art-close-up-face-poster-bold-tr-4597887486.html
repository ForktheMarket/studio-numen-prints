This Hibiscus print is a close up poster of a Black woman's face with a huge red hibiscus tucked behind her ear, for anyone who wants a wall that feels like a warm island afternoon.

The colors are red, deep green, gold, cream and warm brown, bold and warm. It suits a bedroom, a bathroom, a living room or a hallway, and it sits well above a bed or a bathtub. A print for flower lovers and anyone who wears one behind the ear on purpose.
