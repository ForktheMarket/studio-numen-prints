This lit match and glass painting is a dark minimalist still life of a single burning match beside a glass, made for home bars, studies and moody rooms.

A thin wooden match stands upright with a small flickering flame and a trail of smoke. Next to it is a heavy cut glass tumbler, half full, catching the orange glow in its facets. Everything else is deep black. The smoke curls up from the match in a thin gray line, and the light from the flame turns the glass and its contents a deep amber gold.

It is spare, warm and a little mysterious. Black and amber suit dark walls, wood and leather. Hang it in a home bar, a study or office, a man's bedroom, or a hallway with low light. It reads as calm and focused, a small moment of light held still, and it pairs well with the books and candle print.
