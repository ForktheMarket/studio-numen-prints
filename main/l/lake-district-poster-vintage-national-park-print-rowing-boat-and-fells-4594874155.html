The Lake District poster is a vintage style national park print for anyone who has rowed out onto still water with the fells going quiet behind them.

A single wooden rowing boat drifts on a calm lake, oars shipped, with its dark reflection under it and the mountains folding back in layers of blue and olive green under a pale cream sky. The name is set in navy capitals on a soft gray band along the bottom. Flat shapes and a small number of colors give it the look of a mid century railway poster.
