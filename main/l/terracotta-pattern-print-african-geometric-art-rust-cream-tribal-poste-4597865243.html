This Terracotta Pattern print is a poster of hand painted geometric bands, diamonds, zigzags, dashes and dots, running edge to edge in cream on a deep rust ground, for anyone who wants warmth and texture on a plain wall.

The rows repeat like a woven cloth, each band a little uneven where the brush dragged, with soft patches of lighter orange showing through the rust. Nothing is perfectly straight, which is the point, and the whole thing has the feel of a dyed textile hung flat.

The colors are terracotta, rust and cream, two tones and nothing else. It suits a hallway, a living room, a bedroom or a dining room, and it pairs well with wood, linen, cane and the Mud Cloth print from the same set. A print for boho homes and anyone who likes a pattern that feels made by hand.
