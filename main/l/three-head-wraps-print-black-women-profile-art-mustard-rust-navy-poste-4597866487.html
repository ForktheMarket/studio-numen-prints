This Three Head Wraps print is a painterly portrait poster of three Black women standing in a row in profile, their wraps tied high in mustard, rust and navy, for anyone who wants three strong women on one wall.

They stand close with their eyes closed and their chins lifted, bare shouldered, each wrap painted in thick folded strokes and each face a slightly different shade of brown. The woman at the back wears a fold of pale cloth at the shoulder. The background is a plain cream.

The colors are mustard, rust, navy, cream and warm brown, bold and warm. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a salon, and it sits well above a sofa or a bed. A print for sisters, best friends and anyone with a drawer full of wraps.
