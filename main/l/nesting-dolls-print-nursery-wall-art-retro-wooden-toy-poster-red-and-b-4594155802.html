This nesting dolls print is a bright red and blue poster for a child's bedroom, a playroom or a nursery.

Six wooden nesting dolls stand in a line on a solid tomato red background, stepping down from the tallest at the back to the smallest at the front. Each one is pale cream wood painted with big, simple flowers in cobalt blue and orange, like a folk pattern from the sixties. Their faces are no more than a tiny dot or two, and each has a blue flower on top like a cap. Light from the left gives every doll a soft highlight and a short shadow.

The row gets smaller step by step, so small children like counting the dolls and picking out the baby at the end. The picture suits a toddler bedroom, a playroom, a nursery or a kitchen with a folk feel. Red, blue and cream go well with white walls, natural wood and floral or striped fabrics.
