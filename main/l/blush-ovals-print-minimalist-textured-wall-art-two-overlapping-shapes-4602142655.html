This blush ovals print shows two soft oval shapes, one dusty pink and one pale beige, overlapping on a rough white plaster ground, made for bedrooms, living rooms and gentle neutral homes.

The upper oval leans in from the left in warm blush, and the lower one sits beneath it in cream beige, their edges raised in thick plaster so each shape casts a thin shadow where it meets the other. The surface is heavily textured throughout, with knife marks and ridges catching soft light. The ground is white with a touch of warm gray.

The palette is blush, beige and white, calm and a little romantic, so it suits a bedroom above the headboard, a dressing room or a living room with pink or terracotta accents. It belongs to anyone who likes abstract shapes with a sculptural, handmade feel.
