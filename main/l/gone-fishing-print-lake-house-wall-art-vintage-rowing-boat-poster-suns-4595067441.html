This gone fishing print is a vintage lake poster for a lake house, a cabin or a quiet corner of the home.

A wooden rowing boat sits tied to a short jetty on a flat lake at sunset. The hull is cream with orange planks and a thin red rim, and its reflection stretches across the water in long orange ripples. The sky is a soft coral pink, the far shore is a dark line of trees, and a wooded point on the right is reflected upside down in the still water. The near water turns a deep slate blue. The scene sits inside a cream border with the words GONE FISHING in teal blue capitals underneath. It is painted in flat blocks of color with a little grain, warm and quiet.

It suits a lake house, a bedroom, a hallway or a bathroom, above a bed or a sofa. Coral, teal and cream go well with white walls, driftwood, rattan and pale blue linen. It fits anyone who would rather be on the water, and it pairs with the tight lines angler from the same series.
