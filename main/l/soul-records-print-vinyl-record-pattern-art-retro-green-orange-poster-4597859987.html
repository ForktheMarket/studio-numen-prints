This Soul Records print is a pattern poster of vinyl records, record brushes, cassettes and turntable cartridges repeated edge to edge, for anyone whose weekend starts with lifting the needle.

The records are drawn in olive green with bright orange labels, laid out in neat rows with brushes and cartridges between them and a strip of cassettes across the lower third. A cream panel in the middle carries SOUL RECORDS in chunky black letters, the only words on the page.

The colors are olive, orange, cream and black, warm and a little faded like a shop sign from 1972. It suits a music room, a living room, a den, a home bar or a hallway, and it sits well above a record player or a shelf of LPs. A print for collectors, DJs and anyone who still buys the album.
