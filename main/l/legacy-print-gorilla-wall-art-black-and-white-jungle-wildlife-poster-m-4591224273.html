This Legacy print is a black and white wildlife poster showing a baby gorilla crouched at the edge of a jungle pool, with a giant silverback rising out of the mist behind it.

The young gorilla balances on a wet black rock and leans down toward the water, one hand almost touching the surface. Its outline is mirrored in the pool beneath. Above and behind, filling the top of the picture, are the broad face and shoulders of an adult male, watching with a steady, heavy-browed look. Vines hang from the left, forest covers the hills, and low cloud drifts across the water. The word LEGACY runs across the middle in white capitals with wide spacing.

You can read the big gorilla two ways: the father keeping watch, or the adult the little one will become. Either way it is about what gets passed down. It is the darkest and most dramatic picture in the set, with deep blacks and bright fog. It suits a living room, a study, a home office, a den or a boardroom. It is a good match for fathers, grandfathers, founders, mentors and wildlife lovers.
