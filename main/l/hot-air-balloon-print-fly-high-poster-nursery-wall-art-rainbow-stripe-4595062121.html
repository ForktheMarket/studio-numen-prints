This hot air balloon print is a soft retro poster for a nursery, a playroom or a child who loves looking up.

A single striped balloon floats in the center of the page, its envelope made of tall panels in orange, mustard yellow, cream and dark teal that curve up to a rounded top. A small woven basket hangs below on thin lines. Behind the balloon a block of teal sky with puffy cream clouds fills the upper part of the print, while the lower half is plain warm cream, so the balloon seems to drift down out of the sky into the margin. The words FLY HIGH sit underneath in dark teal serif capitals. The painting is flat and slightly faded, like a travel poster that has hung in the sun, and the mood is calm.

It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or a stairway. Orange, mustard and teal go well with white walls, rattan, pale wood and sage green fabrics. It fits new parents and anyone decorating a room meant for daydreaming.
