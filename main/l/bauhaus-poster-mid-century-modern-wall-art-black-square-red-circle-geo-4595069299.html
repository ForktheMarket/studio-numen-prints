The black square print is a Bauhaus style poster for an office, a living room or a hallway.

One large black square is turned on its corner so it sits as a diamond in the middle of a warm cream background, filling most of the width. Its surface has a faint speckle like ink rolled onto a block. Tucked against its upper right edge is a single bright red circle, about a fifth of the square's width, just touching the corner as if it were about to roll off. Nothing else is on the page, and the weight of the black against the small spot of red is the whole idea.

It suits a home office, a living room, a hallway or a landing, above a desk, over a sofa or on a wall of its own. Black, red and cream go well with white walls, pale wood, concrete, chrome and gray fabrics. It fits anyone who likes bold, stripped back graphic art, and it sits well next to the red circle and the mustard and navy circle from the same series.
