This Dublin print is a vintage style travel poster for a hallway, a living room or anyone who misses the city.

A Georgian doorway fills the picture, set into dark brown brick with a black drainpipe down the left edge. The door is sage green with six panels, a brass letterbox and a small brass knob, and it stands between two cream columns with scrolled tops. Above it is a fanlight with glazing bars spread like wheel spokes, then a shallow brick arch. A black bicycle leans against the right column. Low sun from the left throws long shadows across the pale stone pavement. The word DUBLIN is printed underneath in rust orange capitals on a cream border, spaced wide like an old railway poster.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a study or a landing, above a console table or at the top of the stairs. Sage green, cream and brown go with white walls, oak, rattan and linen, and it fits anyone with Irish roots or a soft spot for Dublin. It hangs well beside the other cities in the series.
