This Three Faces print is a cubist portrait poster of three heads stacked and overlapping down the page, each one a different mix of olive, rust, mustard and cream, for anyone who wants a crowd of faces in one frame.

Two faces sit side by side at the top, one in olive and ochre and one in sage and cream with a red ear, and a larger third face fills the lower half with a long cream nose, one orange rimmed eye and a red and mustard jaw. Each eye is a flat almond with a black pupil, each face is cut into angled planes, and the heads share edges so the three become one tall shape. Bands of mustard and olive run down either side.

The colors are olive, rust, mustard, cream, dark green and a flash of blue, deep and earthy. It suits a living room, a hallway, a dining room or an office, and it sits well above a sofa, a sideboard or a desk. A print for anyone who likes faces, families and mid century painting.
