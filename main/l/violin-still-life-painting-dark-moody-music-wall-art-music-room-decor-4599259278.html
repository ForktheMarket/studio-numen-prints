This violin still life is a dark painting of a violin resting against a chair in a shaft of warm light, made for music rooms, musicians and classical music lovers.

The violin is a deep glowing red brown, leaning on a curved wooden chair with a bow beside it. The chair seat is covered in faded red tapestry. Warm light falls across the instrument against a near black background. The bow leans against the chair leg, and the grain of the violin's wood glows under its varnish where the light falls across it.

It is warm and quiet, like a practice room after the lesson ends. Red, brown and black suit traditional and dark academia interiors. Hang it in a music room, above a piano, in a study, or in a living room. It also works in a music teacher's studio.
