This Too Shall Pass is a black and cream quote print for anyone in the middle of a bad week, a bad month or a rough year.

The four words are painted in tall cream brush capitals across three lines on a deep, solid black background. A small crescent moon is brushed in after the last word. The letters are narrow and upright with thick, even strokes, and the edges are slightly ragged where the paint dragged, so it reads as lettered by hand and not typeset. The words get larger as they go down, which gives the last one the most weight.

The saying is centuries old and it cuts both ways. The hard days end, and so do the easy ones, so it is a comfort and a nudge to notice the good while it is here. The little moon makes it feel like something to read last thing at night. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a bedroom, the wall above a bed, a hallway, a bathroom or a home office. It is a good match for worriers, night owls and friends going through it.
