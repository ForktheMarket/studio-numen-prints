This Brown Bear print is a painterly wildlife poster of a massive brown bear sitting in profile in long dry grass, its head turned slightly up as if it has caught a scent, against a pale misty sky, for anyone who wants a big quiet animal on the wall.

The bear is painted in deep browns and black with the light catching the fur along its back and the top of its head, so its outline glows against the sky. The grass around it is brushed in soft strokes of tan, umber and olive, with a few tall seed heads standing up in front. The sky is a plain warm white that fades into the mist.

The colors are dark brown, umber, tan, olive and warm white, earthy and still. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a nursery, a study or a cabin, and it sits well with wood, leather, wool and anything rustic. A good piece for anyone who loves bears, hiking or the mountains.
