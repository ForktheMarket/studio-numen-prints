This Calm Sea at Sunset print is a soft painterly seascape poster of flat aqua water meeting a peach sky, with a thin line of gold light down the middle, for anyone who wants a coastal piece that stays quiet.

The horizon sits just above center. Above it the sky moves from warm apricot at the top to a pale haze at the water line, and below it the sea goes from milky turquoise to a deeper teal in the foreground, broken by one low line of white foam. The setting sun is not shown, only its reflection, painted as a soft gold streak that the eye follows up the page.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a bathroom, or a beach house living room, and it pairs with the other aqua and gold pieces in the series for a gallery wall.
