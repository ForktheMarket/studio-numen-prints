The Stegosaurus print is a speckled picture book poster for a nursery, a toddler bedroom or any child who knows every dinosaur by name.

A Stegosaurus walks from right to left across a warm cream background, head low and tail stretched out behind it. Its body is a deep navy blue broken up with orange speckles that gather along the back and thin out toward the legs, with a plain orange belly underneath. A row of tall orange plates runs from the neck to the tip of the tail, the biggest ones standing over the hump of its back like a crown. The word STEGOSAURUS is printed in bold navy capitals underneath. The whole thing has the grainy, slightly uneven look of an old picture book page, friendly rather than fierce.

It suits a nursery, a kids bedroom, a playroom or a reading corner, hung above a crib, a bed or a low bookshelf. Navy and orange sit well with white walls, pale wood, mustard and denim blue, and it lines up with the Triceratops and the Diplodocus from the same series.
