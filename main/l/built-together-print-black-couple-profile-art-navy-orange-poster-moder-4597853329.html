This Built Together print is a modern poster of a Black man and woman standing back to back in profile, for any couple who built what they have side by side.

He faces left with a full beard and close cropped hair, she faces right with her locs wrapped up high and a large gold hoop in her ear. The picture is split down the middle, cream and dark on his side, burnt orange on hers, so the two halves lock together like one shape. BUILT TOGETHER sits below in heavy dark capitals.

The colors are cream, burnt orange, navy and warm brown, bold and graphic. It suits a bedroom, a living room, a hallway or an entryway, and it sits well above a bed or a console. A strong print for anniversaries, new homes and couples who are a team first.
