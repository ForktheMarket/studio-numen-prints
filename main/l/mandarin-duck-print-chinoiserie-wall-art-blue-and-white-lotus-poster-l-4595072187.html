The mandarin duck print is a blue and white chinoiserie poster for a bedroom, a bathroom or a couple setting up home together.

Two mandarin ducks sit face to face on a pair of lotus leaves, each with a crest swept back, a striped collar at the throat and a fan of wing feathers lifted behind. The composition is almost a mirror image, with a large open lotus in each top corner, a spray of leaves between them, and buds on tall stems down each side above a strip of water. Everything is painted in cobalt and indigo with pale blue washes in the petals, on a cream white ground, with fine brush lines in the feathers. Mandarin ducks are a traditional symbol of a loyal pair, so the picture has a quiet, affectionate feel.

It suits a bedroom, a bathroom or a hallway, above a bed or over a bath. It goes with white walls, pale wood, brass, linen and navy or pale blue bedding, and it fits newlyweds and anyone who collects blue and white. It pairs with the crane from the same series.
