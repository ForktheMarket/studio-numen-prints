This Howling Coyote print is a folk art poster of a coyote drawn as one flat black shape, head thrown back in front of a big cream moon on an orange ground, for anyone who wants a bold, simple desert picture.

The coyote stands on the left with its muzzle pointing up and to the right, its outline rough and painterly rather than clean cut. The moon sits behind its head, slightly off center, and the background is a warm burnt orange with visible brush texture. Three colors, one shape, no detail to distract.

It suits a living room, a kid's room, a hallway or a cabin, and it makes a strong pair with the rooster print in the same series. It also makes a good single piece above a desk or a chest of drawers, where the moon lands at eye level.
