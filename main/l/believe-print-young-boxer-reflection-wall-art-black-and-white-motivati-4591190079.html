This Believe print is a black and white motivational poster showing a young boy with boxing gloves on a wet city street, looking down into a puddle.

The boy stands at the right of the picture in a hooded sweatshirt and heavy boots, a pair of big old gloves hanging from his hands. Behind him, out of focus, a grown fighter in trunks holds his fists up under a street lamp, with brick apartment blocks on either side. The puddle at the boy's feet reflects the fighter and the lamp, so the man he could become is right there in the water. The word BELIEVE crosses the middle in heavy white capitals.

It has the grain and soft blur of old street photography, which gives it a gritty, lived in feel. The story is about a kid with a long way to go and a clear picture of where he wants to end up. It suits a home gym, a boxing or martial arts gym, a garage, a home office, a boys room or a teen bedroom. It is a good match for boxers, coaches, trainers, young athletes and anyone who started with very little.
