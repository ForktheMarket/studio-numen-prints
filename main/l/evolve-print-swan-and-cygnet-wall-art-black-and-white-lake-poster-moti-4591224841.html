This Evolve print is a black and white poster showing a fluffy gray cygnet on a reed nest and a white adult swan gliding on a misty lake beside it.

The cygnet stands on the left on a mat of dark reeds, head down, looking at its own small reflection. A little way off, the adult swan floats with its neck in a full curve and its wings slightly raised, and the still water gives back an exact dark copy. Behind them a line of trees dissolves into fog and the sky is almost white. The word EVOLVE sits across the middle in thin white capitals with wide spacing.

It is the ugly duckling story in a single picture, the scruffy start and the graceful result a few feet apart. The mood is very quiet. Most of the picture is pale mist and smooth water, so it brings light to a wall instead of weight. It suits a bedroom, a bathroom, a living room, a hallway, a yoga room or a therapy room. It is a good match for bird lovers, late bloomers, graduates and anyone partway through a change.
