The scattered shapes print is a Bauhaus style poster for an office, a living room or a hallway.

Five flat shapes and two thin lines float across an off white background as if they had been dropped on the page. A red circle sits in the upper right, a smaller cobalt blue circle lower left, and between them a tall triangle split into yellow and blue halves points down toward the blue circle. Lower right, a red square sits with a black circle tucked under it. The two black lines cross the page at steep angles, one through the triangle and one from the red circle to the bottom, like pins holding everything in place. Red, blue, yellow and black on cream, with a lot of air between the shapes.

It suits a home office, a living room, a hallway or a playroom, above a desk, over a sofa or in a gallery wall. These colors go well with white walls, pale wood, black metal and gray fabrics. It fits anyone who likes playful geometry, and it pairs with the quieter prints from the same series.
