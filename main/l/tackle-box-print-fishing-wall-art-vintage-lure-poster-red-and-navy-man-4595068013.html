This tackle box print is a vintage fishing poster for a man cave, a garage or an angler's office.

The words TACKLE BOX sit across the top in navy capitals. Below them a red tackle box stands open on gray blue wooden boards, its lid up and its tray full of lures. There are six plug lures in red, navy and cream, a couple of them striped, with their treble hooks showing. A reel rests on the rim of the box, a second red reel lies on the boards to the left, and a round red and white float with its line and hook sits at the bottom right. It is printed in three colors, red, navy and cream, with a coarse grain like an old magazine advertisement.

It suits a man cave, a garage, a home office or a boot room, above a workbench or a desk. Red, navy and cream go well with dark wood, gray walls, metal shelving and leather. It fits lure fishers and anyone who likes old tackle, and it pairs with the fly box print from the same series.
