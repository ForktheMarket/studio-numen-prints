The Innsbruck ski poster is a vintage style travel print for dog lovers and skiers alike, built around the old story of the mountain rescue dog.

A black and white dog with a small wooden barrel on its collar sits chest deep in soft snow, looking out across the slope with a calm, patient face. Drifts of white and pale blue fill the lower half, a band of deep blue sky runs along the top, and the city name is set in heavy black capitals between them. Cream, blue and black keep it simple, with a flash of red at the collar.

It suits a child's bedroom, a hallway, a mudroom, a living room or a vet's waiting room, and it works well above a bed or beside a dog bed. It pairs with the other Alpine resorts in the set for a gallery wall of mountain towns.
