This Pine Island print is an ink wash style coastal landscape poster of a small rocky island with a single pine on top, standing in a flat, pale sea, for anyone who wants a calm picture with a lot of air in it.

The island sits left of center, its rock face painted in rough gray strokes and the pine in dark, flat layers above it. The sea is suggested with a few thin horizontal lines and a scatter of dark rocks in the foreground, and the rest of the page is a warm cream wash with a hint of misty hills at the top. A red seal sits in the lower right.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a hallway or a small study, and it works well where a busier picture would feel crowded.
