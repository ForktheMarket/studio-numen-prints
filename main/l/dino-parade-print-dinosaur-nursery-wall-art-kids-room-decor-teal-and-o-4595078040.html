The Dino Parade print is a simple two color poster for a nursery, a toddler bedroom or a child who cannot pick just one dinosaur.

Four small dinosaurs walk in a line from right to left across the middle of the picture, each one a little smaller than the one in front. A teal Triceratops leads, head down and frill up. Behind it comes a tall orange long necked dinosaur, then a smaller orange one with its mouth open, and a little teal one bringing up the rear. The words DINO PARADE are printed in rounded teal capitals above them. The background is plain cream and there is plenty of empty space around the line, which keeps it calm and easy to look at.

It suits a nursery, a kids bedroom, a playroom or a shared room for two children, above a crib, a bed or a long low shelf. Teal and orange go with white walls, pale wood, mustard and gray, and it pairs with the single dinosaur prints from the same series.
