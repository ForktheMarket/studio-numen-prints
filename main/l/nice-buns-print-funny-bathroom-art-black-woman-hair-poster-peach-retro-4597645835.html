This Nice Buns print is a funny bathroom poster of a Black woman in two high hair buns checking her reflection, for anyone who likes a compliment with a double meaning.

She is shown from behind in a white bath towel, shoulders bare, turning toward a round gold rimmed mirror. In the glass her face is caught mid pout, eyes closed, bright red lipstick, the two buns sitting neat and high. The palette is soft and warm: peach walls, a band of cream, a wash of pale teal at the bottom and the deep teal of the mirror glass. The words NICE BUNS run across the top in a dark teal serif. It is playful without being crude, and the joke lands on the hair first.

It suits a bathroom, an ensuite or the wall beside a mirror, and it makes a good pair with the Flush print from the same set.
