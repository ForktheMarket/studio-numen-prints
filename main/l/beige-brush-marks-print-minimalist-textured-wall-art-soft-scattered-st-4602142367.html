This beige brush marks print shows dozens of soft smudged strokes of beige scattered loosely across a white plaster surface, made for living rooms, bedrooms and bright neutral homes.

Each mark looks like a thumb or a dry brush dragged once across the wall, feathered at the edges, in shades of oat, sand and pale taupe. They drift across the sheet without a pattern, denser in the middle and thinning toward the corners. The plaster behind is bright warm white with a fine grain.

It is the lightest print in the set, airy and gentle, so it suits pale rooms, bedrooms and nurseries where a darker piece would feel heavy. The palette is white and soft beige, and it belongs to anyone who likes abstract art that is barely there. Hung beside the blush ovals print it makes a soft, pale pair for a bedroom wall.
