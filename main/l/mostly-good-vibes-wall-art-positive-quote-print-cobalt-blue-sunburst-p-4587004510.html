This Mostly Good Vibes print is a funny positive quote poster for people who are cheerful most of the time and honest about the rest.

Three words are stacked in thick cobalt blue brush capitals on a warm cream background. Painted rays fan out from the lettering in every direction, like a child's drawing of the sun. The strokes are slightly uneven, with a few dry streaks where the brush ran thin, so it looks painted by hand and not typeset.

The word Mostly does the work. It takes a line you have seen a hundred times and makes it true, which is why it gets a smile. Blue and cream go well with white walls, denim, pale wood and striped fabric. It suits a living room, an entryway, a hallway, a dorm room, a kitchen or a home office. It is a good match for roommates, optimists with limits and anyone who needs coffee before they are nice.
