This I'm Going to the Gym print is a funny two panel comic poster for anyone whose fitness plans are better in theory than in practice.

In the top panel a small yellow egg-shaped character with pink cheeks strides toward a white gym building, a blue gym bag slung over its shoulder, under the words I'M GOING TO THE GYM. In the bottom panel it stands outside the same building holding a pink ice cream cone, and the caption finishes the thought: TO LOOK AT IT. The drawing is thick black outline with flat color, set in two cream panels on a bright vermilion red background.

The joke is honest and kind about it. Nobody gets told off, and the character is just as pleased with the ice cream as it was with the plan. It suits a home gym, a bedroom, a dorm room, a hallway or the wall beside a treadmill that mostly holds laundry. Red and cream go well with white walls, black accents and pale wood.
