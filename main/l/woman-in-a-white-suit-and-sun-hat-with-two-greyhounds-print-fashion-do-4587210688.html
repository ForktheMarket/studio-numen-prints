This Woman in a White Suit and Sun Hat with Two Greyhounds print is a fashion portrait for sighthound owners and anyone who likes sunny, graphic wall art.

A woman sits cross-legged on a checkerboard floor in a loose off-white suit with wide trousers. She wears a broad sun hat and black sunglasses and looks straight ahead without smiling. A slim gray dog sits on each side of her, upright and alert, with the narrow head and folded ears of a greyhound or whippet. Hard sunlight comes in from the left and throws a sharp diagonal shadow across the mustard yellow wall behind them. The paint is thick and the brushstrokes show, especially in the folds of the suit.

The mood is cool and unbothered, like a long afternoon somewhere hot. Mustard, off-white, gray and black sit well with wood, rattan, tan leather and plain white walls. It suits a living room, a home office, an entryway or a hallway. It is a good match for greyhound, whippet and Italian greyhound owners and for rooms that need a warm yellow.
