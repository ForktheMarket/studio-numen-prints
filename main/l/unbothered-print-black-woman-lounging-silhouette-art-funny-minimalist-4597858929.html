This Unbothered print is a minimalist poster of a Black woman stretched out on a lounger with one hand behind her head, cut as a solid black silhouette on white, for anyone whose weekend plan is no plan.

She lies back with her face tipped up and her knees bent, the lounger a long rounded shape beneath her, the whole scene reduced to one black form. UNBOTHERED runs across the top in heavy black serif capitals, which is the only other thing on the page.

There is no color at all, which makes it an easy fit for a tiled bathroom, a white bedroom or a gallery wall of black and white prints. It sits well above the toilet, beside a bathtub or over a bed. A print with a smile for self care Sundays and anyone who has mastered the art of not replying.
