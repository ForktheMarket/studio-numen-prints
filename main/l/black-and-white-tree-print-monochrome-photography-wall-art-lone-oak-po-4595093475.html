This single tree print is a black and white photographic poster for a living room, a bedroom or anyone who likes calm, minimal walls.

One bare oak stands alone on a low rise, dead center in the picture, its trunk short and thick and its branches spreading into a wide, rounded crown. Every twig is picked out in black against a sky that fades from pure white at the top to faint gray mist behind the branches. The ground is the opposite, dark frosted grass that runs almost black along the bottom edge, with a thin band of fog where land meets sky. The contrast is strong, the grain is fine, and the tree is the only thing there. The mood is still and patient, a winter morning with nothing moving.

It suits a living room, a bedroom or a hallway, above a sofa or a bed. The blacks and whites go with pale walls, light oak, wool and linen, and it pairs with the flock of birds from the same series. It fits anyone who wants one quiet image rather than a busy wall.
