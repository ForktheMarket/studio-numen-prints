This Home Is Wherever Mama Is print is a poster of a Black mother in an orange dress waving from the doorway of a small white house in an open golden field, for anyone whose home has always been a person, not a place.

The house is tiny at the bottom of the picture, white clapboard with a dark green roof and two windows, set in dry grass under a huge pale yellow sky. She stands in the open door with one hand raised. The words run across the top in three lines of bold red capitals.
