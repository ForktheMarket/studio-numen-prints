This heron print is the first of a pair, a single olive green heron standing in shallow water on a cream background, made for living rooms, bedrooms and hallways.

The heron faces left with its long neck curved and its beak held level, its body painted in soft sage and olive with darker feathers along the back and wing. Thin dark legs stand in a pale wash of water at the bottom of the page. The background is a warm cream with loose brush marks and a faint texture, so the bird looks painted rather than drawn.

This is panel one of two herons, and the second faces the other way so they can be hung either side of a bed, a sofa or a doorway as a matched pair. Each also works on its own. The muted greens suit rooms with linen, oak and white walls.
