This Stag print is a painterly wildlife poster of a stag with wide antlers seen from behind, walking away from the viewer into a wall of warm sepia mist, for anyone who wants a moody piece that feels like the end of a walk.

The stag is painted in dark brown with the light picking out its shoulders and rump, its antlers spread in sharp black silhouette against the pale glow of the sky. The ground is a rough track of umber and black with scrubby grass at the edges, and everything beyond the stag fades to a soft, brushed haze.

The colors are sepia, umber, dark brown, cream and soft gold, atmospheric and warm. It suits a hallway, a living room, a study, a bedroom or a cabin, and it works well with dark wood, tweed, leather and anything rustic. A good piece for anyone who likes deer, autumn or quiet walks in the woods.
