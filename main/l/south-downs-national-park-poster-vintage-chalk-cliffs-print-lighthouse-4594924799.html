The South Downs poster is a vintage style national park print for anyone who has stood on the cliff path with the wind coming off the Channel.

A sheer white chalk cliff drops into calm green water, with a red and white striped lighthouse standing on its rocky base just offshore and a clear teal sky above. The name sits beneath in dark green capitals. The palette is teal, chalk white, soft green and a single stroke of red, kept flat and clean so the cliff face does most of the work.

It suits a living room, a bathroom, a hallway or a bedroom by the coast, and it works well above a bed or in a gallery wall of seaside prints. It is a natural pick for walkers, Sussex locals and anyone who misses that stretch of coastline.
