The dragonfly print is a vintage entomology plate poster for a bathroom, a bedroom or a nature lover.

A single dragonfly is shown from above in the center of the plate. Its four wings stretch out wide, clear and glassy with a fine lattice of dark veins and a faint wash of pale green and blue. The long body is a deep teal blue broken into neat segments that run down the page to a forked tip, and the two large eyes are a rounded blue green. The body casts a soft shadow to the right.
