This Waterfall print is a painterly nature poster of a wide, tall waterfall dropping in pale aqua glazes into a misty pool, for anyone who wants a cool, calm picture for a bathroom or bedroom.

The fall spans almost the full width of the page, painted in long vertical strokes of white, mint and pale turquoise, with a warm cream sky above and spray fading everything at the base. The pool below is the only deeper color, a band of teal with flecks of gold where the water lands. The tall format suits the subject and makes the drop feel long.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a spa room or a landing, and it works especially well in a narrow space. Hung above a bath or at the end of a corridor the long fall draws the eye down the wall, which makes a small room feel taller.
