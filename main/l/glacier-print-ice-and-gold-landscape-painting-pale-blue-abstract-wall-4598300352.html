This Glacier print is a painterly landscape poster of pale blue ice shelves stacked across the picture, each edge traced in gold, for anyone who wants cool color with a little warmth in it.

The ice is painted in soft washes of powder blue, slate and white, in flat tiers that step back toward a hazy sunrise on the horizon. Thin lines of gold run along the lip of each shelf and down into the water, and the sky is a pale blend of cream and peach. The gold keeps it from feeling cold.

It suits a bedroom, a bathroom or a modern living room with white and gray, and it sits well next to the blue mountains print from the same series. It is one of the few landscapes in the shop that suits a room decorated in silver, white and pale blue.
