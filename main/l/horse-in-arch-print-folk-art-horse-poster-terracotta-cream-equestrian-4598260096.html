This Horse in Arch print is a folk art poster of a small white horse standing inside a chalk white arch painted on a wall of terracotta, looking back over its shoulder from a dark doorway, for anyone who likes warm Mediterranean color with one quiet figure in it.

The arch is drawn in a thick rough line of cream, and the space inside it is a deep brick red that reads as shadow. The horse stands near the front of the opening, painted in pale cream with a soft shadow pooling under its feet. Around the arch the wall is washed in brushed orange and terracotta.

The colors are terracotta, burnt orange, brick red and cream, warm and sunny.
