This rocking horse print is a classic nursery poster for a baby's room, a playroom or a child's bedroom.

A small wooden rocking horse stands in profile on a plain teal background, facing left. It is carved in pale, grainy wood with thick legs set on two curved rockers. The mane and tail are painted a warm orange-yellow, the saddle is bright red, and the eye is a single dark dot. A thin shadow under the rockers makes it look as if it has just come to rest. The shapes are rounded and soft, like a toy that has been handled for years.

The rocking horse is about as traditional as nursery pictures get, but the flat teal and bold red keep this one from looking old fashioned. It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom or a playroom. Teal, red and warm wood go well with white walls, oak furniture, cream and mustard fabrics.
