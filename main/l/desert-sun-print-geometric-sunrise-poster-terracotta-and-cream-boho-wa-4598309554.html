This Desert Sun print is a geometric boho poster of a big stepped sun in rings of terracotta, rust and cream rising over soft red hills, for anyone who wants warm color and a simple shape.

The sun is built from concentric rings cut into wedges, dark rust at the center, pale apricot at the outer edge, with rays fanning down toward the land. The hills below are layered in brick red, salmon and sand. The paint has a soft, slightly uneven finish, so it feels hand done rather than printed.

It suits a living room above the sofa, a bedroom, a hallway or an entrance, and it goes with terracotta, rattan and plants. It also works in a child's room, since the shape is simple and the color is cheerful without being loud. Hang it where it catches afternoon light and the rust and apricot warm up further.
