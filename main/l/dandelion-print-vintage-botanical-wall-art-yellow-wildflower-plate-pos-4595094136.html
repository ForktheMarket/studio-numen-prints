The dandelion print is a vintage botanical plate poster for a kitchen, a nursery or anyone who loves a common weed.

Two dandelion stems rise from one base. The left one carries a full golden yellow flower head, a dense mop of fine petals above a cup of narrow green bracts. The right one stands taller and carries the seed head, a round pale clock of silky parachutes around a brown center, drawn so fine you can see every stalk. One long toothed leaf sits at the base, dark green with jagged edges. The ground is a plain warm cream, and the word DANDELION is lettered in small spaced capitals at the bottom. It has the fine lines and even light of an old engraved plate, flower and clock side by side.

It suits a kitchen, a nursery, a bathroom or a hallway, alone or with the daisy and poppy from the same series. Yellow, cream and dark green go well with white walls, pale wood and sage green, and it fits gardeners, parents and anyone who blew the seeds off a dandelion as a child.
