This Bloody Lovely print is a funny British quote poster for anyone who says it about a cup of tea, a roast dinner or a sunny afternoon.

The two words are painted in thick orange red brush capitals that lean a little to the right, on a warm cream background. A long red stripe runs down each side, and a row of short, chunky stripes sits along the top and bottom, like the awning of a seaside kiosk or the edge of a deck chair. You can see the brush marks where the paint runs thin, which keeps the look of a hand lettered sign.

It is cheerful and loud without being rude, so it works in rooms that guests see. The red sits well next to white tiles, pale wood and blue and white china. It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a hallway or the wall beside a drinks shelf, and it holds its own in a gallery wall with quieter pictures around it.
