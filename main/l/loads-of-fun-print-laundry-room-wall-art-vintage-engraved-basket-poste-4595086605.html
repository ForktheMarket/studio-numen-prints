The Loads of Fun print is a vintage engraved style poster of a laundry basket for a laundry room, a utility room or a mudroom.

A wide wicker basket sits on a cream background, drawn in dark green with the fine hatched lines of an old engraving. Every strand of the weave is picked out, the rim is thick and plaited, and a short shadow of horizontal lines anchors it to the floor. Piled on top is a heap of rumpled laundry, sheets and towels and maybe a pair of jeans, stippled in the same green. Above it, LOADS OF FUN sits in chunky green slab serif capitals. Green on cream, and it looks like it came off a nineteenth century catalog page.

It suits a laundry room, a utility room, a mudroom or a guest bathroom, hung above the hamper or beside the door. Dark green goes with white tile, brass, natural wicker, cream walls and warm wood floors. It fits anyone who can laugh at housework and likes a farmhouse or country look. It pairs with the Wash Day print from the same series.
