This Wave Face print is a close cropped painterly seascape poster of the glassy face of a wave just before it breaks, lit from behind so the water glows green, for surfers and anyone who loves being in the sea.

The crop is tight, with the curl of the wave sweeping up from the bottom left and spray flying off the top edge. The water is painted in deep emerald, jade and bottle green with white foam and bright highlights where the light comes through the thinnest part. There is no horizon and no sky, just water in motion.

It suits a bathroom, a surf themed bedroom or a coastal living room, and it is a strong single piece for a narrow wall. It also works as the bold piece in a group of calmer seascapes, since it is the only one with no horizon.
