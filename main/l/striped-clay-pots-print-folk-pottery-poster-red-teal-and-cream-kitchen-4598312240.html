This Striped Clay Pots print is a folk art poster of big round pots stacked and overlapping until they fill the whole page, each one banded in red, teal, cream and black, for anyone who likes pattern and pottery.

The pots are painted as flat rounded shapes with wide horizontal stripes, no two striped the same way, and they crowd together so there is almost no background showing. The colors are warm brick red and burnt orange against cool teal and navy, with cream to rest the eye. It has the feel of a market stall seen all at once.

It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a hallway or a living room, and it is a good choice above a table or a sideboard. It also brings color to a plain kitchen without needing to match anything, since it already holds most of the warm and cool colors a kitchen might have.
