This Golden Field print is a painterly poster of a Black mother and her small child walking hand in hand through tall golden grass under a single red sun, for anyone who feels most at home in open country.

They are seen from behind, both in loose cream robes, the mother's hair cropped short and the child reaching up for her hand. The grass glows amber around their legs, a dark hill rises on the right, and the sky is a wide wash of warm sand with the sun a flat red disc near the top.

The colors are sand, amber, cream, red and warm brown, calm and golden. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a nursery, and it sits well above a sofa or a bed. A print for mothers, travelers and anyone who grew up walking somewhere wide.
