This Just a Quick Nap print is a funny two panel comic poster for nap lovers, heavy sleepers and anyone who has lain down for twenty minutes and woken up in the dark.

In the top panel a small round yellow character is tucked into bed under a lilac blanket, eyes closed and smiling, with a bright sun in the window. The caption reads JUST A QUICK NAP. In the bottom panel it is in exactly the same spot, mouth open, with a row of z z z floating above its head. Outside the window it is now snowing hard, and snow has piled up on the sill. The caption reads SEE YOU IN SPRING. Thick black outlines and flat color sit in two cream panels on a soft lilac purple background.

It is a calm, sleepy joke, and the colors are quiet enough to live with in a room meant for rest. It suits a bedroom, the wall above a bed, a guest room, a dorm room or a teenager's room. Lilac and cream go well with white bedding, gray and pale wood.
