This Forehead Kiss print is a modern poster of a Black man pressing a kiss to a woman's forehead, cropped tight so the two faces fill the page, for anyone who knows that kiss means more than the other kind.

His eyes are closed, beard against her hair, one hand cupping her cheek. She leans into it with her own eyes shut and a single gold hoop at her ear. Both are painted in flat blocks of burnt orange and deep brown, with no outlines, against a pale cream corner.

The colors are burnt orange, chocolate brown, black and cream, bold and warm. It suits a bedroom, a living room, a hallway or a home office, and it sits well above a bed. A tender print for couples, anniversaries and anyone who was raised on forehead kisses.
