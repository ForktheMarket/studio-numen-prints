This terracotta circle print shows a single irregular circle of warm clay brushed onto a pale sand colored plaster surface, made for living rooms, bedrooms and neutral modern homes.

The circle sits just left of center, its edge soft and uneven where the pigment thins into the texture, so it looks pressed into the surface rather than painted on top. Around it the background is cream and sand with the fine grain of plaster, catching light in small ridges and hollows. Nothing else is in the picture.

The palette is terracotta, sand and cream, warm and earthy, which suits boho, Scandinavian and Japandi rooms alike. It works above a sofa, a bed or a console table, and pairs well with other neutral prints on a gallery wall. It suits anyone who likes art that is quiet, textured and warm.
