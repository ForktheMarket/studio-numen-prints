This Galloping Horse print is a folk art poster of a tall black horse caught mid stride, running up and to the left across a ground of brushed red with a pale diagonal streak behind it, for anyone who wants an animal print with real movement in it.

The horse is a loose black silhouette, its front legs reaching forward, back legs kicked out, mane and tail streaming in rough strokes. Behind it a soft band of pale peach cuts across the red from the lower left to the upper right, so the whole picture leans in the direction the horse is going.

The colors are black, scarlet, coral red and peach, bold and energetic. It suits a living room above the sofa, a home office, a hallway or a teenager's room, and it works well with white walls, black frames and dark wood. A good piece for anyone who rides, runs or just likes a strong red.
