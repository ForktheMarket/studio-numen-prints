This rainbow trout print is a vintage field guide style poster for a cabin, a kitchen or a trout fisher's wall.

The words RAINBOW TROUT sit across the top in navy capitals, and below them a single rainbow trout is shown in full profile, swimming to the left. Its back is a deep blue green speckled with fine dark dots, a band of coral pink runs the length of its side, and its belly fades to silver and cream. The fins and tail are olive green with darker rays picked out in thin lines. The background is plain peach cream with nothing else on it. The fish is drawn with a fine stipple texture, the way an old natural history plate would be, so it reads as a specimen as much as a poster.

It suits a kitchen, a cabin, a hallway or a boy's bedroom, above a sideboard or a desk. Navy, pink and cream go well with pale wood, white tiles and blue walls. It fits anyone who fishes for trout, and it pairs with the brown trout and the pike from the same series.
