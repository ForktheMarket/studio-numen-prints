The blue and white vase print is a chinoiserie still life poster for an entryway, a living room or a dining room.

A tall lidded jar stands at the center of a warm white ground with a branch of spring blossom rising from its neck. The jar is painted with full peonies and leaves in cobalt and indigo, with a band of scroll panels around the shoulder and another around the foot. The blossom is white with pale blue shading, dozens of small flowers with a touch of gold at the centers, on a bare brown branch that spreads to both sides. The line work on the porcelain is fine and controlled, while the blossom is softer and looser, so the picture balances the two.

It suits an entryway, a living room or a dining room, above a console table or a mantel. It goes with white walls, pale wood, linen, rattan and navy or sage fabrics, and it fits anyone who likes classic rooms with a few blue and white pieces. It pairs with the ginger jar from the same series.
