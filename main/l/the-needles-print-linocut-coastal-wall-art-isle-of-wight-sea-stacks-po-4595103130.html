The Needles print is a linocut style coastal poster for a hallway, a living room or anyone who knows the Isle of Wight.

Three jagged chalk stacks rise from a dark sea in a row, the tallest in the middle, each one lit along its left face in bright orange as if the sun is setting behind the viewer. The shaded sides are cut with long vertical gouges in dark slate, and the orange faces carry the same marks so the rock looks scoured and layered. A tiny red lighthouse sits on the middle stack. The sea below is near black with pale strokes for the swell, and the sky is plain cream. Three colors, dark slate, orange and cream. The words THE NEEDLES sit across the top in tall dark capitals.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a study or a boat cabin, above a sofa or a desk. Orange, slate and cream go with white walls, gray paint and tan leather. It fits sailors, kayakers and anyone who sails the Solent, and it pairs well with Beachy Head and Godrevy from the same series.
