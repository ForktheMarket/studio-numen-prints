This Saxophone Player print is a painterly poster of a Black jazz musician in a loose white shirt leaning back into a solo, for anyone who knows the exact note where the room goes quiet.

He plays with his eyes shut and his head thrown back, the brass of the saxophone picked out in thick strokes of gold and amber. Above him two curved brush marks, one orange and one cream, arc across a deep navy background like sound leaving the horn.

The colors are navy, cream, gold and orange, rich and warm. It suits a living room, a music room, a home bar, a hallway or a study, and it sits well above a sofa or a record player. A print for jazz lovers, horn players and anyone who closes their eyes for the good bits.
