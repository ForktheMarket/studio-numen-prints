This Bamboo print is an ink wash style botanical poster of tall bamboo stalks rising from the bottom edge, their leaves spreading in dark, quick strokes near the top, for anyone who wants a plant on the wall that is not a leafy green poster.

The stalks are painted in bold black and gray ink, jointed and slightly bent, against a warm cream ground that fades to a soft gray mist on the left. The leaves are sharp clusters of brushmarks. A red seal sits in the lower right. The tall format suits the subject, and the picture is mostly empty space with the bamboo cutting through it.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a hallway or a narrow wall by a door, and it pairs well with the plum blossom print. Hung beside a mirror or a doorway it reads as a tall, slim accent rather than a picture that demands attention.
