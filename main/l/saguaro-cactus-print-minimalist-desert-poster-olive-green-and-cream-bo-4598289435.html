This Saguaro Cactus print is a minimalist desert poster of one tall olive green cactus with two raised arms, a small orange sun beside it, on a plain cream ground, for anyone who wants a desert picture reduced to the basics.

The cactus is painted as a flat shape with soft edges and visible brush texture, standing on a thin band of sand at the bottom of the page. The sun is a single orange disc in the upper right. That is the whole picture, and the empty space around the cactus is as important as the cactus.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a hallway or a home office, and it works well in a white room with wood and a few plants. It also pairs with the prickly pear print, which gives you one tall cactus and one wide one for a pair.
