This Leaf Face print is a cubist portrait poster of a face built entirely from leaf shaped planes in sage, olive and dark green, for anyone who wants a figure piece in the soft greens of a plant filled room.

One large eye in olive and cream looks straight out from the right side of the page. A red stripe runs down the center as the nose, the lips are a pair of flat olive and ochre shapes, and the rest of the head fans out in pointed leaves of pale sage, moss and forest green. An orange disc with a yellow center sits on the cheek like a small sun, and the corners are filled with mustard and cream.

The colors are sage, olive, mustard, cream, dark green and one spot of orange, calm and natural. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a sunroom or a hallway, and it looks right above a sofa, a bed or a plant shelf. A print for anyone who likes green walls, houseplants and mid century portraits.
