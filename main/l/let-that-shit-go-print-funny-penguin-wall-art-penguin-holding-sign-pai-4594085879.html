This Let That Shit Go print is a funny penguin poster for worriers, overthinkers and anyone who wants a calm, blunt reminder on the wall.

A very small penguin stands alone on a tiny slab of ice in the middle of a flat, empty sea. It holds a cream sign above its head with both flippers, and the sign is several times taller than the penguin. It says LET THAT SHIT GO in plain black capitals. The penguin has a white belly, a black back and orange feet, and it looks off to one side as if the matter is closed. The water is deep teal blue and the sky fades from pale yellow at the horizon to soft aqua at the top. It is painted in smooth, visible brushstrokes, like a small oil painting.

There is nothing else in the picture, only one bird, one piece of ice and one piece of advice, which is why it lands. It suits a bathroom, the wall above a toilet, a bedroom, a home office or a coastal themed room. Teal, aqua and cream go well with white tile, navy, sand tones and light wood.
