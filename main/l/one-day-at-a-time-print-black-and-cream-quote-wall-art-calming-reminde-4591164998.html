This One Day at a Time print is a black and cream quote poster for anyone getting through a hard stretch by keeping their eyes on today.

The five words are painted in cream brush capitals across two lines, set in the upper half of a large black block. The block is brushed onto a cream background and leaves a border all the way around, with rough, dry edges where the paint feathers out. Low in the right corner there is a small painted sun with short rays. Most of the black is left empty, and that quiet space is what gives the print its calm.

The phrase is well known to people in recovery, and it works just as well for new parents, carers, anyone grieving and anyone facing a long list. It asks for nothing more than today. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. The cream border also helps it sit well on a dark wall. It suits a bedroom, the wall above a bed, a hallway, a bathroom or a counseling office.
