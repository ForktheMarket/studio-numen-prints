This Proud of You Mate print is a funny ostrich poster for a friend who has just passed an exam, landed a job, finished a race or simply got through a hard year.

An ostrich stretches its long neck up at an angle and turns its head to the side, wearing gold mirrored aviator sunglasses. It has scruffy feathers on its head, a flat beak and a completely deadpan face. The words Proud of you mate.. are typed above it in small typewriter letters. The bird is drawn in scratchy black ink with sandy cream coloring, on a muted teal green background with a painted shadow behind it.

It suits a home office, a dorm room, a bedroom, a hallway or a home gym. Teal and gold go well with white, gray, walnut and brass.
