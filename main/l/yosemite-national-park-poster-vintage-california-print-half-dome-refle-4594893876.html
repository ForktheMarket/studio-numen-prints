The Yosemite poster is a vintage style national park print for anyone who has stood at the edge of a still meadow pool and watched the granite reflect in it.

Half Dome rises above a line of tall pines, the whole scene mirrored in dark water below, with pale pink light on the rock face and a cream sky. The name sits beneath in heavy dark green capitals. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a home office, and it works well above a bed or a sofa. The greens sit comfortably with plants and natural wood, and it pairs with the other parks for a gallery wall.
