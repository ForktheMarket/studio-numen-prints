This skyscraper print shows a canyon of tall city towers in the rain, their upper floors fading into translucent gray mist, made for living rooms, offices and apartments.

The buildings are painted as overlapping vertical blocks of charcoal, teal and smoke gray, with grids of warm lit windows and a crown of gold light on the tallest tower. At street level the road is soaked, and the headlights of a few cars stretch into long reflections of amber and white across the wet asphalt.

The composition is strongly vertical, which makes the most of a narrow wall or a tall slot between windows, and it hangs well as a pair with the rainy avenue print. The colors are dark gray with warm gold, so it pairs well with dark wood and industrial interiors. It suits anyone who loves a big city skyline seen from the sidewalk looking up.
