This Deep Breaths Small Steps Big Coffee print is a funny quote poster for anyone who gets through a hard week one mug at a time.

Six words are stacked down the middle, one per line, in rounded cobalt blue capitals on a soft peach background. A single hand drawn blue line boxes the words in, slightly wobbly at the corners. The letters are chunky and close together, so it reads like a calm instruction until you reach the last line, which is where the joke lands.

The blue and peach together are warm and easy to live with, and they sit well beside wood shelves, white mugs and stainless steel. It suits a kitchen, the wall above a coffee machine or coffee bar, a home office or the space above a desk. It also works in a staff room or a shared office where everyone needs the same advice on a Monday.
