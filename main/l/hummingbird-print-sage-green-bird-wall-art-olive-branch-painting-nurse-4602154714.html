This hummingbird print shows a small sage green hummingbird hovering beside a single olive sprig on a warm cream background, made for nurseries, bedrooms and quiet corners.

The bird hangs in the air on the left of the page with its wings blurred in motion and its long thin beak pointing toward the leaves. Its body is painted in soft sage and olive with a pale cream chest. On the right, a slim branch with narrow olive leaves reaches down from the top edge. The space between the two is left open, which makes the moment feel still and light.

The colors are gentle, green and cream with hints of gray, and they work in a neutral nursery, a bedroom with linen and wood, or a reading nook. It pairs well with other sage prints from the same series, especially the swallows and the herons.
