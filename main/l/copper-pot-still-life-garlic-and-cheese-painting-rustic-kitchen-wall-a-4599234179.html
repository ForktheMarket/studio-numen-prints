This copper pot still life is a dark painting of a kitchen table with garlic and a wedge of hard cheese, made for kitchens, dining rooms and anyone who loves cooking.

A shiny copper pot with a long brass handle sits on a rough wood table beside two bulbs of garlic and a big wedge of aged cheese. A linen cloth hangs over the edge of the table. The background is a deep black. The cheese has a hard, crumbly texture and a thick rind, and the copper of the pot glows with warm reflections from the light.

Copper, cream and warm wood give it a rustic, farmhouse warmth. It suits a kitchen above a stove or table, a dining room, a breakfast nook, or a pantry door. It pairs with the lemons and oysters in the same series.
