The violin print is a moody still life poster for a music room, a study or a string player.

A violin lies on its back on a dark table, its scroll pointing up to the right, with the bow laid across it from the lower left. Under it two loose pages of handwritten sheet music are spread out, the notes painted as dark marks on cream. Behind it a black cloth is bunched and folded, catching a few cool highlights. The violin is a deep red brown, glossy where light from the upper left hits the belly, and the four strings run as pale lines over the bridge. The background is dark brown fading to black, and the paint is thick on the cloth and the table, smoother on the instrument.

It suits a music room, a living room or a study, above a piano or a desk. The red brown, cream and black go with dark wood, velvet, brass and deep green or burgundy walls. It fits musicians and music teachers, and it pairs with the hourglass and the marble bust from the same series.
