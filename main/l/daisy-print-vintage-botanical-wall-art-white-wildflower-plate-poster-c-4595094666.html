The daisy print is a vintage botanical plate poster for a bathroom, a bedroom or anyone who loves a simple white flower.

Three large ox eye daisies sit in a loose bunch, one at the top seen from the side and two below facing out, each with a ring of white petals around a domed golden yellow center. The petals are creamy white with soft shadows where they overlap, and the centers are drawn as tight clusters of tiny florets. Two green stems run down to a single long dark green leaf with a toothed edge. The ground is a warm cream with a faint paler oval behind the flowers, and the word DAISY is lettered in small spaced brown capitals at the bottom. It has the fine lines of an old engraved plate.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a kitchen or a hallway, and it pairs well with the dandelion and poppy from the same series. White, yellow and green go well with white walls, pale wood, linen and sage green, and it fits gardeners and anyone who likes a wildflower meadow.
