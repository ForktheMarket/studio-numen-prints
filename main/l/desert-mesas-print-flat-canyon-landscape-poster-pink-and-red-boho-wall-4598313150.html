This Desert Mesas print is a flat painted landscape poster of two red rock mesas rising from layered pink and crimson ledges under a peach sky, for anyone who wants desert color in a softer, pinker key.

The mesas are flat topped shapes in brick red and rose, one tall on the right and one lower on the left, and the land in front of them steps down in horizontal layers of blush, coral, raspberry and dark red. The sky is a warm apricot with no clouds. The style is simplified, like layers of cut and painted card.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a dressing room or a hallway, and it pairs with the canyon river and zigzag mountain prints. It also pairs with the desert sun print, which shares the same soft reds and peach.
