This Growth print is a black and white nature poster showing a sprouting acorn in still water, with a giant old oak tree standing in the mist behind it.

The acorn sits half in the water at the center of the picture, with a thin stem and two crisp oak leaves opening above it. Directly behind, soft and slightly out of focus, a wide oak spreads its branches over a foggy pond. The water mirrors everything, so the tree's crown appears again below like a root system, and the small seedling has its own dark twin. The word GROWTH runs across the middle in white serif capitals with wide spacing.

Seed in front, tree behind. It is the old saying about mighty oaks and little acorns, told in one picture. The picture is nearly symmetrical top to bottom, which makes it restful to look at, and the tones run from bright mist to deep black. It suits a home office, a living room, an entryway, a classroom, a study or a therapy room. It is a good match for teachers, gardeners, new business owners, parents and anyone playing a long game.
