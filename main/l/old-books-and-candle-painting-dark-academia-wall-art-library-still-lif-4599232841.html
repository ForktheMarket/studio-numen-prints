This old books and candle painting is a dark still life of leather books and a lit candle, made for libraries, studies and dark academia lovers.

A stack of worn leather books in brown, rust and black sits on a dark table beside a tall brass candlestick. The candle burns with a small bright flame, and its warm light picks out the gilt on the spines and the shine on the brass. Small drips of wax run down the candle, and a little brass key lies on the table, as if someone has just stepped away from the desk.

It is warm, quiet and bookish. Brown, gold and black make it right at home with wood shelves and leather chairs. Hang it in a study or home office, above a reading chair, beside bookshelves, or in a dark hallway.
