This Three Women print is an abstract portrait poster of three Black women's faces stacked one above the other in profile, for anyone who wants a wall that feels like a conversation.

The top face looks left in warm brown with a cobalt head wrap, a large hoop drawn in one thin line. The middle face looks right, in deeper brown, over a wash of marigold. The bottom face looks left again, painted in burnt orange with a cobalt lip, against a sweep of blue. All three have their eyes closed.

The colors are cobalt, marigold, burnt orange, brown and cream, confident and warm. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or an office, and it sits well above a sofa or a bed. A print for sisters, friends and anyone with three strong women in their life.
