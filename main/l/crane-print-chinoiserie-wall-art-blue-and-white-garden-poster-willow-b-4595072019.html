The crane print is a blue and white toile style poster for a hallway, a dining room or a bird lover.

A single crane stands on a riverbank, its neck curved and its beak pointing left. The body is left mostly white with the wing and tail feathers shaded in indigo, and the long legs are drawn in fine hatched lines. A weeping willow hangs from the left, a broader tree spreads across the top, and behind the bird an arched stone bridge crosses a stretch of pale blue water. Flowering bushes fill the lower corners. The whole scene is painted in one color, from a pale wash in the sky to deep cobalt in the foliage, on a warm white ground, like a scene lifted from a porcelain charger.

It suits a hallway, a dining room, a bedroom or a bathroom, above a hall table or on a landing. It goes with white paneling, dark wood, brass and blue striped fabrics, and it fits bird watchers and anyone who likes old blue and white china. It pairs with the pheasant from the same series.
