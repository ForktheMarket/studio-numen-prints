The leather books print is a candlelit still life poster for a library, a reading corner or a book lover.

Eight old books are piled in a loose, slightly tilted stack, spines facing out with raised bands and the ghost of gilt tooling, too worn to read. The covers are red brown, chestnut, dark olive and near black, and the page edges show as pale cream and gold. A single lit candle stands in a small brass holder on top of the pile, the flame bright yellow with a trail of melted wax down one side. The light fades down the stack into a deep brown and black background. The paint is laid on thickly, so the spines and page edges have a ridged texture.

It suits a library, a study, a living room or a bedroom, above a reading chair or in a hallway. The browns and ochres go with dark wood shelves, leather chairs, brass lamps and deep green or oxblood walls. It fits readers, writers and collectors, and it pairs with the red apple and the fountain pen from the same series.
