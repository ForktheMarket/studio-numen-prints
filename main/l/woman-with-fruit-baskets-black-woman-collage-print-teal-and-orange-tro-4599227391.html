This fruit baskets print shows a Black woman in a patterned head wrap carrying two baskets of oranges, made for kitchens, living rooms and anyone who loves tropical color.

She walks away from us with her face in profile, a gold hoop at her ear and a bare shoulder above a patchwork dress. Two woven baskets piled with bright orange fruit hang beside her. Behind her are layers of tropical leaves in blues and greens on a teal background. The fruit is painted round and bright, with every orange catching a little light, and the woven baskets have a warm straw texture.

Teal and orange are a bold pair, and the patterns keep the eye moving. It suits a kitchen or dining room, a living room, or a sunroom with plants. A cheerful piece that feels like a market morning.
