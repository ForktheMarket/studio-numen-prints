This dry fly print is a vintage fly fishing poster for a cabin, a study or a fly fisher's wall.

One dry fly fills the middle of the print, drawn large. A broad rust red wing sweeps back from the head, with a fan of yellow and gold hackle behind it and two slim upright feathers at the top, red tipped with pale yellow bases. The body is a short yellow wrap, a few fine tail fibers trail off to the left, and the black hook curves down below. It sits on plain cream with the words DRY FLY printed in rust red capitals underneath. It is painted rather than hard edged, with soft brush marks in the feathers.

It suits a study, a hallway, a den or a cabin bunk room, above a desk or a fly tying bench. Rust, yellow and cream go well with oak, cane, wool blankets and olive green walls. It fits fly fishers who tie their own, and it pairs with the fly box print from the same series.
