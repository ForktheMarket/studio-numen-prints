This Work in Silence print is a black and white motivational poster showing a small wolf pup looking up at a full grown wolf that leans out of a tall arched mirror.

The pup sits on the left on a patch of lit stone floor, ears pricked, nose lifted. On the right stands a narrow mirror with a rounded top and a thin dark edge. The adult wolf in it lowers its head toward the pup until the two are almost nose to nose, its thick ruff picked out by the light and its front legs planted on the floor. Everything else is solid black. The words WORK IN SILENCE. run across the top in white capitals with wide spacing.

It is a quiet picture about getting on with it. No announcements, no audience, just steady effort until the result speaks for you. Most of the image is empty darkness, which gives the two animals a lot of room and keeps the mood still. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a home gym, a study, a den or a teen bedroom. It is a good match for wolf lovers, founders, students and anyone who prefers to let results do the talking.
