This Two Horses print is a folk art poster of two tall horses standing nose to nose, one black and one chestnut brown, their long necks bowed toward each other against a warm peach ground, for anyone who wants a quiet picture of two creatures choosing to stand close.

Both horses are flat silhouettes with long thin legs, the black one on the left and the brown one on the right, their heads meeting just above the center of the picture so their necks form an arch. The ground is a soft mottled peach and apricot with cloudy brush marks and nothing else in it.

The colors are black, chestnut, peach, apricot and cream, warm and tender. It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a hallway or a shared study, and it reads as a couple piece without a single heart or word on it. A natural choice for a wedding, an anniversary or a first home together.
