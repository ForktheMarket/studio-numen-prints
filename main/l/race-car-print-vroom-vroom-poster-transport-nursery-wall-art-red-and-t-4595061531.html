This race car print is a retro speed poster for a boys room, a playroom or anyone who grew up making engine noises.

A bright red open top racing car cuts across the picture from bottom left to top right, nose lifted as if it has just hit a bump at full tilt. A driver in a red helmet sits low in the cockpit, and a white roundel on the door carries the number 2 in dark teal. The wheels and the road are deep teal, and long teal streaks trail off behind the car so it looks like it is moving. The background is warm cream, and the words VROOM VROOM run along the bottom in chunky teal capitals. The style is painted and slightly rough, with visible brush marks, closer to an old motor racing poster than a cartoon.

It suits a kids bedroom, a playroom, a teenage boys room or a hallway. Red, teal and cream go well with white walls, navy bedding, pale wood and checkered fabric. It fits small children who line up toy cars and adults who still do.
