This You Are Exactly Where You Need to Be print is a black and cream affirmation poster for anyone who worries they are behind.

The eight words are painted in tall cream capitals across five lines on a solid black background, with a small cream heart in the bottom corner. The lettering is narrow and slightly uneven, with a dry, speckled edge where the paint breaks up, so it reads as lettered by hand and not typeset. The words fill the top two thirds and leave a band of plain black below them, which gives the heart room to be noticed.

The message is steady and kind. It does not tell you to try harder or want more. It says the place you are in now counts. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a bedroom, the wall above a bed, a hallway, a home office, a dorm room or a therapy or counseling office. It is a good match for graduates, people between jobs, new parents and anyone in the middle of a big change.
