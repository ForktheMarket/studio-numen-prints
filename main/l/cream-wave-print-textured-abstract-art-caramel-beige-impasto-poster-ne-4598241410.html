This Cream Wave print is a textured abstract poster of one enormous slow sweep of thick white and cream paint curling over a warm caramel ground, for anyone who wants a big calm shape on the wall and nothing else.

The wave starts low on the left, rolls up through the middle and breaks at the top right, every ridge of the knife showing in the paint. The outer edge is pale and feathered, the inside of the curl darkens to toffee where the paint thins, and small flecks of white spray off the crest. The ground is a flat tan with a faint linen weave.

The colors are cream, warm white, caramel and toffee brown, soft and quiet. It suits a living room above the sofa, a bedroom above the bed or a wide hallway, and it sits well with wood, rattan and other neutral pieces. It was made for rooms that are already calm and want to stay that way.
