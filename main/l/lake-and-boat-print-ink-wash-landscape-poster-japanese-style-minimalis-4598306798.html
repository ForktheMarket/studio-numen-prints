This Lake and Boat print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a still lake under a misty mountain, with one small boat and its rower crossing the water, for anyone who wants a picture that feels like a deep breath.

The mountain rises in soft blue gray ink from a wooded shore on the right, its peak sharp and its lower slopes lost in haze. The lake takes up the bottom half of the picture in a wash so pale it is almost the color of the page, and the boat is a thin dark line near the lower left with a faint wake behind it. A red seal sits in the corner. The scale of the boat against the mountain is what makes it.

It suits a living room, a bedroom, a study or an office, and it is one of the most restful pieces in the shop.
