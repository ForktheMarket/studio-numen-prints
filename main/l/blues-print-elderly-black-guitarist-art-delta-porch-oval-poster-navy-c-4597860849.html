This Blues print is a painterly poster of an elderly Black guitarist in a straw hat and a blue work shirt picking an acoustic guitar on a porch, for anyone who thinks the best music was made by men who had lived a bit.

He sits with his eyes half closed and his white beard catching the light, fingers on the strings and a wooden wall behind him. The scene is set inside a tall oval on a cream background, with BLUES beneath in heavy navy serif capitals.

The colors are denim blue, cream, tan and warm brown, loose and sunlit. It suits a music room, a living room, a home bar, a porch or a hallway, and it sits well above a guitar stand or a sofa. A print for blues fans, guitar players and anyone whose record collection starts in the Delta.
