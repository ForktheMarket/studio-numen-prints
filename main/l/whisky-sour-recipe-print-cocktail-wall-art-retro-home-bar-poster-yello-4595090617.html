The Whisky Sour print is a retro cocktail poster for a home bar, a kitchen or a dining room.

The words Whisky Sour run across the top in bold black capitals, with the recipe lettered beneath on one line: Whisky, Lemon, Sugar. Below that a wide coupe glass sits in the middle of the print holding a bright yellow drink with a pale foam on top and a wheel of lemon hooked over the rim. Behind the glass a burst of fine mustard yellow rays spreads out like a sun, drawn with a speckled, screen printed texture. The background is pale cream and the whole print is yellow and black, nothing else.

It suits a home bar, a kitchen, a dining room or a breakfast nook, hung beside the drinks cabinet or above a counter. Yellow, cream and black go well with white walls, pale wood, black metal and marble. It fits whiskey drinkers who like their cocktails sharp, and it pairs with the Old Fashioned and the Negroni from the same series.
