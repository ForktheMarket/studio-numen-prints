The Sorting Out Life One Load at a Time print is a retro sock poster for a laundry room, a utility room or a family home.

Six socks hang in a row from a thin black line, each held by its own wooden clothespin. From left to right they are orange red, mustard yellow, teal, orange red again, pale teal with an orange heel, and black with an orange heel and toe. Each sock has a ribbed cuff and soft shading, like a mid century advertisement. Above the line, SORTING OUT LIFE sits in black serif capitals, and below the socks, ONE LOAD AT A TIME finishes the thought. The background is warm cream with a faint speckle.

It suits a laundry room, a utility room, a mudroom, a kids bathroom or a hallway, hung above the washing machine or beside a row of pegs. Orange, mustard and teal go with white walls, pale oak, navy cabinets and checkerboard floors. It fits busy parents and anyone who copes by doing one thing at a time. It pairs with the Lost Socks print from the same series.
