The Golf Club Est 1927 poster is a vintage style crest print for golfers who like the look of an old club badge, a members' tie or a weathered sign over a locker room door.

Two hickory shafted clubs cross behind a single golf ball inside a heavy navy border, with a curved mustard banner reading Golf Club across the bottom and Est 1927 on a smaller panel beneath it. Everything is drawn in navy and mustard on cream, with a fine speckled grain that makes it look like it has been printed and reprinted for years. The club is fictional, so it fits any golfer rather than one course.
