This sage green cloud print is a soft minimalist sky painting with pale green clouds drifting across the top of the page, made for bedrooms, nurseries and anywhere that needs calm.

A band of rounded sage clouds sits in the upper third, painted in muted green with soft gray shadows. Below them the sky fades through pale mint and cream to a thin line of horizon near the bottom edge, with a blush of warm pink in the top corner. The paint is loose and hazy, and there are no hard lines anywhere, so the whole piece feels like a quiet morning.

The palette is mostly cream, mint and sage, which makes it easy to hang above a bed, in a nursery, or in a light bathroom. It works alone or as a pair with the hills and dunes prints from the same series.
