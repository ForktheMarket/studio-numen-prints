This flock of birds print is a black and white photographic poster for a living room, a study or anyone who loves open countryside.

A dense cloud of starlings hangs in a pale sky, thousands of tiny black shapes packed so tightly in the middle that they read as one solid mass, thinning to scattered specks on the left where the flock trails off. Below them bare winter trees sit in mist, with one larger oak standing clear on the right, and a frosted field runs dark across the bottom. The tones move from near white at the top, through soft gray fog, to deep charcoal in the grass. A gentle grain sits in the sky and the haze softens the trees, so the sharp birds stand out. The mood is cold, quiet and a little wild.

It suits a living room, a study or a hallway, above a sofa or a desk. The grays go with white walls, pale wood, charcoal fabrics and brass, and it pairs with the lone tree from the same series. It fits birdwatchers and anyone who misses the countryside.
