This head wrap portrait is a calm painting of a Black woman in profile, made for a bedroom, a living room or a quiet reading corner.

She stands with her eyes closed and her chin lifted, a tall cream head wrap folded high above her and a single gold hoop catching the light. Behind her a large banana leaf in deep olive green rises up against a soft cream ground. The paint is thick on the fabric and smooth on the skin, so the wrap almost looks sculpted.

The palette is cream, olive and warm brown, which sits easily with natural wood, linen and plants. The mood is restful rather than dramatic, the kind of picture you notice more slowly over time. It works well above a bed or a dresser, or hung next to other warm portraits on a hallway wall.
