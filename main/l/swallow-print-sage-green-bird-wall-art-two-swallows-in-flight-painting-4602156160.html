This swallow print shows two sage green swallows crossing in flight on a plain cream background, made for bedrooms, living rooms and quiet neutral spaces.

The upper bird dives down from the top left with its wings spread wide and its forked tail trailing behind. The lower bird swoops up from the right to meet it. Both are painted in soft sage with olive and gray detail on the wings and a pale cream throat. The rest of the page is left empty, so the two birds and the space between them are the whole picture.
