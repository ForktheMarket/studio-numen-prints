This Sleeping Leopard print is a painterly wildlife poster of a leopard lying with its chin resting on its crossed front paws, eyes half closed, against a plain gold brown ground, for anyone who wants a big cat on the wall that feels calm rather than fierce.

The face fills the frame, every spot and whisker picked out, the fur soft and pale around the muzzle and darker along the brow. The paws are folded under the chin like a house cat on a windowsill. The background is a flat scrubbed ochre with a paler band of cream along the bottom edge.

The colors are gold, ochre, cream, warm brown and black, soft and warm. It suits a living room, a bedroom above the bed, a study or a hallway, and it sits well in safari, colonial and neutral rooms with rattan, leather and dark wood. A good piece for anyone who loves cats of any size.
