This Navy and Orange print is a color block abstract poster of a deep indigo band sitting over a bright rust orange one, split by a ragged seam of thick paint, for anyone who wants a bold piece that still reads as simple from across the room.

The top half is a flat dark navy with a linen texture. A thin hot line of orange cuts straight across, then the navy breaks up into heavy knife marks that crumble into the orange below. The lower half glows from deep rust at the bottom to an almost yellow flare at the right, with one thick drip of paint bridging the two.

The colors are navy, indigo, burnt orange, rust and gold, strong and warm. It suits a living room, a home office, a hallway or a dining room, and it holds its own on a large wall. It pairs well with mid century furniture, walnut and black metal.
