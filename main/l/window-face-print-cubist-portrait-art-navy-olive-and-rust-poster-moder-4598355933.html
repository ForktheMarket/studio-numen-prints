This Window Face print is a cubist portrait poster of a head seen through an open window, the face split into planes of cream, ochre and olive between tall bands of navy blue, for anyone who wants a bold figure piece with a calm, thoughtful mood.

The face fills the middle of the page. A heavy ochre brow sits over one dark, half closed eye, the nose runs down as a long cream and ochre wedge, and the mouth is a flat rust shape. Black hair curves over the right side of the head and falls into a deep shadow down the right edge. On the left, a rust disc with an orange center hangs like a low sun, and the navy bands either side read as the frame of the window, so the figure looks out from somewhere inside.

The colors are navy, cream, ochre, olive, rust and black, warm against cool. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or an entryway, and it looks right above a sofa, a bed or a console table. A print for anyone who likes mid century portraits with strong lines and a face that holds a mood.
