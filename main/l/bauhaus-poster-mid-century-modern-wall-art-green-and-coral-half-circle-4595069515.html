The half circle column print is a Bauhaus style poster for a hallway, a living room or a bedroom.

Seven half circles are stacked in a single column down the middle of an off white background, each one flat edge up and curve down, like a row of bowls. From the top the colors run forest green, coral pink, black, coral, black, coral and forest green again, so the column reads the same from either end. Each shape has a dry, printed texture with lighter streaks running through it, closer to a wood block print than a flat vector, and they sit almost touching. The result is tall, narrow and rhythmic, with the three colors taking turns down the page.

It suits a hallway, a landing, a living room or a bedroom, in a narrow gap beside a door, above a console table or over a bed. Green, coral and black go well with white walls, oak, terracotta pots, linen and brass. It fits anyone who likes calm, repetitive shapes, and it pairs with the stacked arches from the same series.
