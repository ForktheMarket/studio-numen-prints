This wooden caterpillar print is a calm, colorful nursery poster for a baby's room, a playroom or a family home that likes simple toys.

A pull along caterpillar sits low in the picture on a teal floor against a darker teal wall. Its body is a row of round wooden balls that alternate between bright orange-red and pale natural wood, and it rolls on small wooden wheels. The head is a smooth cream ball with two tiny dots for eyes, turned slightly toward you. Soft shadows fall underneath, so it reads like a quiet studio photograph of a single toy, with a lot of empty space above it.

That empty space is what keeps the picture restful, even with the orange. It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or a reading corner. Teal, orange and warm wood sit well with white, cream, oak furniture and rust or terracotta fabrics.
