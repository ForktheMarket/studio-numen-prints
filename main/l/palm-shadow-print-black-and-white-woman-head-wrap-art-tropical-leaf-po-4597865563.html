This Palm Shadow print is a black and white poster of a Black woman's face inside a soft oval, a palm frond laid across her wrapped head so its stripes become the pattern of the cloth, for anyone who likes a picture that takes a second look.

Her face sits low in the oval, lips full, eyes lifted, the wrap built entirely from the black and white stripes of the leaf. A second frond curls down the right side. The background is plain cream.

There is no color at all, so it fits any room and any gallery wall. It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a hallway or a living room, and it works well beside white tile and plants. A print for anyone who likes tropical without the color, and a clean pair with the Monstera Wrap print from the same set.
