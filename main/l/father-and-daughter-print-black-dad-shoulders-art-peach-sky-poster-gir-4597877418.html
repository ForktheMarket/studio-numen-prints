This Father and Daughter print is a minimalist poster of a Black dad carrying his little girl on his shoulders while she throws her arms out wide, for anyone who remembers being that high off the ground.

He stands with his back to us in a loose cream shirt and tan trousers, head turned to the side with a smile, holding her ankles. She sits up straight in a blue dress, puffs of hair catching the light, arms stretched like wings. Behind them is a wide wash of peach with a few pale brush strokes of cloud.

The colors are peach, cream, tan, blue and warm brown, warm and open. It suits a girls bedroom, a nursery, a living room or a hallway, and it sits well above a bed or a sofa. A print for fathers, daughters and anyone who was somebody's whole view once.
