This father and child print shows a Black dad lifting his small child high above his head, made for nurseries, family rooms and new parents.

Both figures are flat cut-out shapes in black and mustard yellow, with patches of pattern and rough texture inside them. The child flies above with arms out and legs dangling, and the father reaches up with both hands. The background is a clean pale cream. The child's open arms and the father's upturned face catch the moment just before a laugh, the kind of thing every parent remembers.

Two colors and a lot of white space keep it simple and joyful. It works in a nursery above the crib, in a kids' room, in a family hallway, or in a living room alongside family photos. A warm picture of fatherhood that does not get sentimental.
