This Golden Hill print is a soft painterly landscape poster of a single low hill in gold and amber, reflected in still water under a milky sky, for anyone who wants warmth and almost nothing else.

The hill sits on the right of the picture, its ridge picked out in bright gold cracks like gilding, and its reflection pools in the shallow water below. The sky and sea are washes of cream, blush and pale blue that barely separate at the horizon. The foreground is wet sand in warm honey tones. It is a very quiet image with one strong warm shape.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a calm living room or a hallway, and it gives a room a warm glow without a strong color. If you want a pair, hang it beside the sand dunes print, which shares the same warm sand and soft sky.
