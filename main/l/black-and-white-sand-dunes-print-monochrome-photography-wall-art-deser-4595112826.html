This sand dunes print is a black and white photographic poster for a bedroom, a living room or anyone who likes calm, neutral walls.

A trail of footprints leads away from the bottom of the picture across wind rippled sand, growing smaller as it climbs toward a smooth dune ridge on the horizon. The ripples run in tight diagonal lines from the lower left, each one bright on one side and shadowed on the other, so the whole surface reads as a pattern of light and dark stripes. The nearest footprints are deep and sharp, the far ones fade to dots, and the sky above the ridge is plain white. The grain of the sand shows everywhere, giving the print a soft, tactile texture. The mood is quiet and lonely, one person walking somewhere far off.

It suits a bedroom, a living room or a hallway, above a bed or a sofa. The grays go with white walls, oatmeal linen, rattan and pale wood, and it pairs with the pier from the same series. It fits travelers, walkers and anyone who likes a neutral wall.
