This olive green hills print is a minimalist landscape of rolling hills layered in shades of green beneath a wide cream sky, made for living rooms, hallways and modern neutral homes.

The hills rise in soft overlapping bands from the bottom of the page, starting in deep forest green at the front and fading through olive and sage to a pale misty ridge at the back. Above them, the sky takes up more than half the page in a warm cream with the faintest haze. The edges of each hill are softened so the layers seem to drift into each other.

It is a calm, simple composition with a lot of breathing room, and it works well as a single print above a sofa or sideboard, or grouped with the dunes and clouds prints from this series. The greens sit nicely with oak, linen, white walls and black metal.
