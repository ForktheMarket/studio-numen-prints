This Face to Face print is an abstract portrait poster of two Black women in profile, almost touching at the forehead, for anyone who wants a picture about closeness without a single detail spelled out.

The woman on the left is painted in bright red with a sweep of black hair, the woman on the right in deep brown against a block of marigold. A thin black line traces a necklace on each of them. The gap between the faces leaves a narrow shape of cream down the middle.

The colors are red, marigold, brown, black and cream, big flat shapes with nothing fussy. It suits a bedroom, a living room, a hallway or a studio, and it sits well above a bed or a sofa. A print for sisters, best friends, mothers and daughters, or two people who finish each other's sentences.
