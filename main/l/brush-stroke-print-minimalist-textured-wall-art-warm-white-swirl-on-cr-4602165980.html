This brush stroke print shows a single wide sweep of warm white paint curving across a cream plaster surface, made for living rooms, bedrooms and modern neutral homes.

The stroke arcs from the top down and around to the left in one movement, its ridges of thick paint catching light so the swirl looks raised off the wall. Behind it the ground is soft cream with a faint sandy grain. There is no other color, just the play of white on near white, light and shadow.

It is the most minimal print in the set, close to a plain textured wall, which makes it an easy piece to live with and a good quiet partner to bolder prints on a gallery wall. The palette suits Scandinavian and Japandi rooms, bedrooms especially, and anyone who likes art that whispers.
