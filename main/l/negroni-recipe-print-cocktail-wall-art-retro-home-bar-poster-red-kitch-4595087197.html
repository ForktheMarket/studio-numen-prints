The Negroni print is a retro cocktail poster for a home bar, a kitchen or a dining room.

The drink name sits across the top in heavy red block capitals, with the recipe lettered beneath it on three lines: Gin, Bitter, Sweet Vermouth. Below the words a short rocks glass holds the drink, a deep glowing red, over two large ice cubes with a curl of orange peel standing up against the rim. The background is warm cream and the glass throws a soft gray shadow off to the left. The printed look is a little grainy, like a mid century drinks advert, with the red of the drink carried straight into the lettering.

It suits a home bar, a kitchen, a dining room or a hallway, hung beside the drinks trolley or above a sideboard. Red and cream go well with dark wood, brass, bottle green and white tiles. It fits people who make a proper Negroni at home and like it bitter, and it pairs with the Old Fashioned and the Dry Martini from the same series.
