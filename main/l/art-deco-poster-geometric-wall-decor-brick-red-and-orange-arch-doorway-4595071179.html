The arched doorway print is an Art Deco style poster for a hallway, a living room or an office.

A tall doorway stands in the lower two thirds of a cream background. The opening is a deep brick red arch with a darker red shadow down its right side, which gives it depth, as if you were looking into a dim room. Around it sits a thin cream outline, then a wide amber orange arch with a small cream circle set into its crown like a keystone. On each side, stepped columns in brick red and amber climb toward the arch in the ziggurat shape of the 1920s. The edges are crisp and the whole thing is symmetrical from the center line out.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a home office or a landing, above a console table, over a desk or at the end of a corridor. Brick red, amber and cream go well with dark wood, brass, black tile, velvet and cream walls. It fits anyone who likes architecture and symmetry, and it pairs with the fan pattern from the same series.
