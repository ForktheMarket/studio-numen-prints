This cafe at night print shows the warm lit window of a corner restaurant seen from a wet city street, made for kitchens, dining rooms and apartments.

Inside the big windows, people sit at tables under amber lamps, and the whole room glows orange against the dark stone building and the rain. Outside a couple walks past under one umbrella, the sidewalk shining with reflected light. A striped awning, a bare tree and a tall building fading into gray mist complete the corner.

The feeling is warm and sheltered, the appeal of being inside while the weather does its worst. The palette is deep black and charcoal with a strong glow of orange and gold, so it suits a dining wall, a kitchen or a bar corner, and anyone who loves city cafes and late dinners.
