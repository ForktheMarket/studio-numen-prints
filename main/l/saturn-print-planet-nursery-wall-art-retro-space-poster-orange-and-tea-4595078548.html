This Saturn print is a retro space poster for a kids bedroom, a playroom or a nursery.

A big ringed planet sits tilted across the middle of a dark teal sky dotted with faint stars. The planet is banded in cream, orange and a strong red, and its wide rings sweep around it in three tones of cream and amber. Behind it, a soft teal shadow sphere shows through the rings, and two tiny moons, one teal and one orange, hang in the top right corner. The word SATURN runs along the bottom in wide cream capitals. The colors are printed with a speckled grain, like an old travel poster, so it feels warm rather than cold.

It suits a boys bedroom, a playroom, a nursery or a hallway wall, and it looks good above a bed or a desk. Teal, orange and cream go with white walls, oak furniture, denim and mustard cushions, and it pairs with the Mars and rocket prints from the same series. It fits space mad kids and parents who want something a bit more grown up than cartoon planets.
