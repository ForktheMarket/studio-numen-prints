This rhino print shows a single rhinoceros standing in profile in tall golden grass beneath a pale, almost empty sky, made for living rooms, offices and anyone who loves African wildlife.

The rhino is painted low and heavy in the lower half of the picture, its thick hide picked out in soft gray brown and warm highlights, its horn raised against the light. Around it the grass glows amber and straw yellow. Above, the sky is a soft wash of cream and peach that takes up most of the sheet, giving the animal room and quiet.

The palette is gentle gold, cream and taupe, calmer than most sunset scenes, so it works well in a neutral room. It suits a study, a living room or a child's room, and anyone who cares about rhinos and the wild places they live.
