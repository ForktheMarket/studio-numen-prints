This Root For Yourself print is a motivational quote poster for students, athletes, job hunters and anyone who cheers for everyone but themselves.

Three words are stacked in large black brush capitals on a bright spring green background. Each word takes a full line, with Yourself stretched widest along the bottom. The letters are thick and confident, with rough, dry edges and small streaks where the brush ran thin, so it feels written by a person and not typeset.

The message is plain: be on your own side. It is positive without being soft, and the bold black on green reads clearly from across a room. Green and black sit well with white walls, plants, pale wood and gym equipment alike. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a bedroom, a teen room, a home gym or a classroom. It is a good fit for someone starting college, training for a race or going for a promotion.
