This Flush print is a funny bathroom poster of a Black woman in a yellow robe pressing the toilet handle, for anyone who needs the reminder said with a bit of glamour.

She stands side on in a huge fluffy mustard robe with a white towel wrapped high on her head, red lips, one hand on her hip and the other reaching out with a single red nail to press the flush. The toilet is drawn in the faintest cream so the robe and the word FLUSH, set in big black serif capitals across the top, carry the whole print. The background is two flat blocks of butter yellow and cream. It is a one word joke told with total composure.

It suits the wall above the toilet, a downstairs cloakroom or a shared bathroom where the reminder actually matters. Yellow brings light to a small room with no window.
