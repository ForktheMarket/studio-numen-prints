The Hello World print is a baby dinosaur poster for a nursery, a new arrival or a parent waiting for one.

A small green dinosaur has just hatched. Its head pokes up out of a cracked egg, one big round eye looking off to the left, with the top of the shell lifted like a lid and jagged orange edges where it broke. The egg is cream with orange shading and sits on a strip of dark green ground inside a tall mint green oval. The words HELLO WORLD curve along the bottom in rounded golden orange letters. Everything has a soft speckled texture and the colors are gentle, mint, cream, orange and green, so it reads as sweet rather than scary.

It suits a nursery, a baby's bedroom or a playroom, above a crib, a changing table or a rocking chair, and it works well as the first print on an empty nursery wall. Mint and orange go with white walls, pale wood, linen and sage, and it pairs with the Dino Parade and the Diplodocus from the same series.
