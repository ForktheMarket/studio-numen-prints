The Edinburgh poster is a vintage style travel print for anyone who has looked up from the Grassmarket and seen the castle lit by the last of the sun.

The castle sits on its dark volcanic crag high above a jumble of Old Town rooftops and chimney pots, with low evening light catching the stone in soft gold and the sky fading to cream. The name runs beneath in heavy black capitals. The palette is navy, slate, cream and gold, with a slightly rough printed texture that suits a city of stone.

It suits a study, a hallway, a living room or a bedroom, and it works well above a bed or a desk, or in a gallery wall of city prints. It is a good fit for students who never quite left, Scots living elsewhere and anyone who loves the festival in August.
