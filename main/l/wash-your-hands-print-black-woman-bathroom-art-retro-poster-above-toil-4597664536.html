This Wash Your Hands print is a retro bathroom poster of a Black woman washing up, for anyone who wants a reminder by the sink that looks like art rather than a sign.

She stands in profile in a terracotta dress and a tall green head wrap, gold hoop catching the light, hands sunk into a bowl of suds with bubbles drifting up around her wrists. The background is split cleanly into warm cream above and sage green below, and the lettering sits bold and dark in the top corner. The shapes are flat and painterly, closer to a mid century travel poster than a cartoon, so it reads as a portrait first and a bathroom sign second.

It suits a family bathroom, a downstairs powder room or the wall above the toilet, and it works just as well in a kitchen by the sink. A good fit for homes that want their wall art to show Black women with warmth and a bit of style.
