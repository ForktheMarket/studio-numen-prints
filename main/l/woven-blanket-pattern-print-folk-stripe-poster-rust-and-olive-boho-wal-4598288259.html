This Woven Blanket Pattern print is a folk art poster of stacked horizontal bands painted like the stripes of a handwoven blanket, in rust, olive, brick and cream, for anyone who wants pattern with a handmade feel.

Each band is different: rows of dots, short vertical dashes, solid stripes and little rounded squares, all painted freehand so no two marks are quite the same. The colors are earthy and warm with a few bands of dark green for contrast. It reads as textile from across a room and as paint up close.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a hallway or a den, and it pairs with the desert sun and clay pots prints from the same series. It is also a good choice for a rented room, since the pattern gives a plain wall some texture without needing a change of paint.
