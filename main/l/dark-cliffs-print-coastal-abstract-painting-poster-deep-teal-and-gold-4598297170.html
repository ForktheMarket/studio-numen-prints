This Dark Cliffs print is an abstract coastal poster of a headland painted as one deep teal mass along the right edge, with pale water and gold flecked foam below it, for anyone who likes coastal art that leans modern.

The cliff is almost solid color, dragged down in long vertical strokes, and its reflection stretches out into a flat milky sea. The sky is a warm off white haze. At the bottom the water breaks up into loose strokes of white, teal and gold, as if the paint were still wet. It reads as a seascape from across the room and as an abstract up close.

It suits a modern living room, a hallway or an office, and it is a good anchor piece for a group of smaller aqua prints. In a group it reads as the dark note that lets the paler prints breathe.
