The Cortina Dolomites ski poster is a vintage style travel print for skiers who have stood at the top of a run in the Italian Alps and taken a moment before pushing off.

A skier in a red sweater, black trousers and a bobble hat stands on a snowy ridge, seen from behind, looking out at the rose colored rock walls of the Dolomites under a deep blue sky. The snow is painted in cool white and pale turquoise and the resort name sits in black Art Deco style capitals at the top. Blue, pink and red together make it one of the richest prints in the set.

It suits a living room, a hallway, a home office or a chalet style bedroom, and it works well above a sofa or a desk. It pairs with the other resorts in the set for a gallery wall of mountain towns.
