This White Horse print is panel one of a pair, a folk art poster of a tall pale horse standing on a terracotta ground beside a large red sun sitting low at the left, against a warm peach sky, for anyone who wants a soft, sunny pair of prints for a bedroom or landing.

The horse is painted in chalky white with a soft gray shadow along its belly, long legs that darken toward the hooves, a small alert head and a pale tail. The sun is a big flat disc of rust red resting almost on the horizon. The sky is a mottled apricot wash with visible brush marks.

The colors are white, peach, apricot, terracotta and rust red, warm and gentle. It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a nursery or a hallway, and it is made to hang beside its partner, which shows a dark horse under the same sun risen high. Together the pair reads as morning and evening.
