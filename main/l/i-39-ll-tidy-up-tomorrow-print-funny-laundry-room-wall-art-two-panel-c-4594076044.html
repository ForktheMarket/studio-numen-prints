This I'll Tidy Up Tomorrow print is a funny two panel comic poster for messy people, their roommates and every parent of a teenager with a floor wardrobe.

In the top panel a small yellow egg-shaped character stands with its arms out, smiling, next to one very small pile of clothes. The caption reads I'LL TIDY UP TOMORROW. In the bottom panel, under the words ONE WEEK LATER, the pile has become a hill that fills the room, a heap of pastel pink, green, lilac, yellow and blue laundry. The character is standing on top of it with exactly the same smile. Thick black outlines and flat color sit in two cream panels on a bright lime green background.

There is no guilt in it, which is why it works. The character has simply accepted the hill. It suits a laundry room, a bedroom, a dorm room, a teenager's room or a shared apartment hallway. Lime green is loud and cheerful and looks best against white walls and simple furniture.
