This mustard head wrap portrait is a painting of a Black woman looking back over her shoulder, made for living rooms, entryways and anyone who likes strong, confident color.

She wears a tall knotted wrap in bright mustard, with the same fabric slipping off one shoulder, and a pair of very large gold disc earrings. Her chin is raised and her lips are slightly parted, with a confident half smile that meets you head on. The background is a deep teal, painted with visible brushstrokes.

Teal and mustard sit opposite each other on the color wheel, so the portrait reads clearly from across a room. It works above a sofa, in a hallway or entry, or next to teal, ochre or wood furniture. The loose painting in the wrap contrasts nicely with the smooth, careful face.
