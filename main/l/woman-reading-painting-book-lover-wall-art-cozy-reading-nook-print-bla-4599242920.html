This woman reading painting shows a young Black woman lost in a novel, made for book lovers, home libraries and reading corners.

She sits with her head bowed over an open book, braids tied up and a chunky knit cardigan pulled around her. A single shaft of warm orange light cuts across the dark room behind her and lands on the pages and the folds of the knit. The brushwork is loose and heavy, close to an old master study in candlelight.

The colors are deep brown, black and burnt orange, so it feels warm and settled, the way an evening with a good book should. The face is calm and absorbed, and the light does the storytelling. Hang it above a reading chair, beside bookshelves, in a study or home office, or in a bedroom.
