This Less Fucks More Naps print is a funny sweary quote poster for the overtired, the overcommitted and anyone who has decided to care a bit less.

Four words are stacked one to a line in thick black brush capitals on a flat bubblegum pink background. The strokes are heavy and blunt, with ragged ends where the brush dragged, and the letters are big enough to fill most of the poster. There is no border and no decoration, only the pink and the black.

It reads as a life plan in four words: stop worrying about everything and go and lie down. The pink is bright and warm, and it looks good against white, gray or pale wood, or on a gallery wall that needs one loud piece. It suits a bedroom, the wall above a bed, a dorm room, a dressing room or a home office where the to-do list has got out of hand. New parents and shift workers tend to get it straight away.
