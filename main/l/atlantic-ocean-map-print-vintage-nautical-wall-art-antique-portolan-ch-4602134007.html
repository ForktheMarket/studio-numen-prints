This Atlantic Ocean map print is a vintage style portolan chart showing both sides of the ocean, with the Americas on the left and Europe and Africa on the right, made for offices, studies and hallways.

The land is drawn on aged parchment in warm tan with fine hatched mountains, and the sea is a soft pale blue that deepens along the coasts. Rhumb lines fan out across the whole sheet from small compass points, in the way early navigators laid out their charts, and a tiny compass rose marks the lower right.

The tall format follows the ocean from the Arctic down to the tip of South America, so it fills a narrow wall nicely. It suits a desk, a landing or a reading room, and anyone who has crossed the Atlantic by sea or air.
