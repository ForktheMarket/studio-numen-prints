This Mediterranean map print is a vintage style sea chart of Italy, Greece and the eastern Mediterranean, made for offices, studies and hallways that want an old explorer feel.

The sea is painted in deep navy blue and the land in aged parchment cream, with every mountain range drawn in fine hatched strokes so the coasts look raised off the sheet. A tall sailing vessel and two sea monsters cross the water near Sicily, and a large compass rose sits in the lower right corner. Faint rhumb lines run across the whole sheet.

The colors are warm and quiet, mostly cream, tan and slate blue, so it sits well above a desk, beside bookshelves or on a hallway wall. It suits anyone who has sailed or traveled around Italy and Greece, and anyone who likes old charts more than modern maps.
