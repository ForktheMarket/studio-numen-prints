This Cafe Reading print is a poster of a Black woman with long locs reading a book at a cafe window, a latte with a heart on top beside her, for anyone who does their best thinking in a corner seat.

She sits in profile in a sage sweater with a gold hoop at her ear, locs falling forward over the page. Through the tall window behind her the street is bright with sun and shadow, red doors and a tree, while the cafe itself is deep green and calm.

The colors are green, sage, cream, navy and warm brown, with the look of a screen print.
