This Close Up Face print is a cubist portrait poster cropped so tight that only one large eye, a long nose and a pair of lips fit on the page, for anyone who wants a face with real presence on a big wall.

The eye is a wide black and white almond under a heavy olive brow. The nose is a tall terracotta wedge running down the middle, the cheeks are planes of pink, olive and tan, and the lips are painted in three shades of red and rose with a thin black line between them. A bright orange disc sits by the temple and a bar of black hair runs down the left edge. The paint is thick and the edges are soft.

The colors are pink, olive, terracotta, mustard, orange and cream, warm and full. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a studio or a hallway, and it looks right above a sofa or a bed where it can be seen from across the room. A print for anyone who likes big, bold faces and mid century color.
