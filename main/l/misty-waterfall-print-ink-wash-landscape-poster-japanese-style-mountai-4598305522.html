This Misty Waterfall print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a tall waterfall dropping through layers of mist between pine covered cliffs, for anyone who wants a calm, traditional looking piece.

The fall is a single white line down the center, and everything around it is painted in soft gradations of gray and green ink on a warm cream ground. The cliffs on either side fade in and out of the mist, with dark pines clinging to their tops and to the rocks at the base. A small red seal sits in the lower corner in the traditional way. It is a picture to look into rather than at.

It suits a living room, a study, a hallway or a meditation room, and it hangs well with the other ink landscapes in the series.
