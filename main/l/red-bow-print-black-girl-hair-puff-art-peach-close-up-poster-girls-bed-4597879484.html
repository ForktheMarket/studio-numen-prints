This Red Bow print is a close up poster of the top of a Black girl's head, her high puff tied with a big red and pink bow, for anyone who knows the ritual of the morning bow.

The crop stops just above her eyes, so the picture is all forehead, brows, slicked edges and the great round puff of black curls above. The bow is painted in bright red with pink highlights, its tails trailing to the left. The background is a soft peach with nothing else to distract from the bow.

The colors are peach, black, red, pink and warm brown, bright and sweet. It suits a girls bedroom, a nursery, a bathroom or a salon, and it sits well above a bed or a dresser. A playful print for daughters, nieces and anyone who still owns a drawer full of ribbons.
