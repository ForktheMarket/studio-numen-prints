This Braids Silhouette print is a black and white poster of a Black woman in profile with long braids falling past her shoulder, cut as one solid black shape on cream, for anyone who wants their braids celebrated in the simplest way.

She faces right with her lips full and a single lash showing at the eye. The braids run from the crown of her head in thick twisted ropes down the left side of the page, each one picked out in the outline, while her bare shoulder slopes away at the bottom right.

There is no color at all, so it suits any wall. It fits a bedroom, a hallway, a living room or a salon, and it sits well above a bed or in a black and white gallery wall with the other silhouettes from the shop. A clean print for braid wearers and the people who braid them.
