This knitting badger print is a cozy woodland animal poster for knitters, crafters and anyone who likes a quiet evening in.

A badger in an oversized mustard cardigan sits in a low armchair with its eyes closed and a small smile, working a pair of wooden needles. A long rust red scarf runs from the needles down over its lap and onto the floor, where a ball of cream yarn has rolled away. Its back paws stick straight out in front, pads showing.
