This Pacific Ocean map print is a vintage style nautical chart of the world's largest sea, made for offices, studies and coastal homes.

Most of the sheet is open water, washed in pale blue that fades to cream at the center, with a single engraved compass rose floating in the middle. The coasts of Asia and Alaska run along the top, the Americas down the right edge, and Australia and New Zealand sit low in the left corner, all drawn with fine contour lines and soft tan relief. Scattered islands dot the sea like specks.

The feeling is calm and spacious, more empty sea than land, which makes it an easy print to live with. It suits a desk wall, a reading corner or a bedroom, and anyone who has crossed the Pacific or dreams about it.
