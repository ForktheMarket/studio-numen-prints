This Sage Green Abstract print is a textured poster of thick sage and olive paint dragged in a strong diagonal across a cream ground, for anyone who wants green in a room without a single leaf or plant in sight.

The green runs from the top left down to the bottom, heaped thick enough to cast its own shadow, shifting from pale sage through olive to a deep bottle green where the strokes pile up. The cream pushes back from the right in soft folds, and the two colors tangle along the seam. The bare ground shows a faint canvas weave.

The colors are sage green, olive, moss, cream and ivory, fresh and natural. It suits a living room, a bedroom above the bed, a bathroom or a kitchen, and it sits comfortably with white walls, light oak and linen. A good pick for anyone building a sage and neutral room.
