This Aerial Lagoon print is a painterly coastal poster of a deep turquoise pool seen from directly above, ringed by white surf and golden sand, for anyone who wants bright water on the wall.

The lagoon sits in the center as an irregular oval of dark teal that lightens toward its edges. Around it the water froths white where it meets the sand, and the sand itself is painted in warm gold and cream with a few dark rocks at the bottom corners. The brushwork is loose and swirling, so it feels more like a painting than a drone photo.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom or a beach house hallway, and the turquoise works well against white tiles and pale wood. Hung at eye level the center of the lagoon lands right where you look, which is part of why it works as a single piece.
