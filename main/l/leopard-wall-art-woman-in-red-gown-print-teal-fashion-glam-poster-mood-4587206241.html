This Woman in a Red Gown with a Leopard print is a fashion portrait for anyone who wants a bold, glamorous picture with strong color.

A tall woman stands against a teal blue wall in a floor-length scarlet wrap gown with long sleeves. A very wide black hat throws her eyes into shadow, so only her mouth shows. One hand, in a long black glove, rests on the head of a leopard sitting calmly at her side, its spotted tail curled along the floor. Strong light from the right casts her shadow large on the wall behind her. The floor is black and cream checkerboard, and the whole scene is painted in flat, confident blocks of color.

The red against the teal does most of the work. It is dramatic without being busy, and it reads clearly from across a room. The colors sit well with dark wood, brass, black accents and white or gray walls. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a dressing room, a hallway, or a salon or boutique. It is a good match for big cat fans and anyone decorating with red.
