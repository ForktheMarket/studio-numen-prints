This boy on a bicycle print shows a young Black boy riding hard in a cap and patchwork jacket, made for kids' rooms, teen bedrooms and anyone who loves bikes.

He leans forward over the handlebars of a cobalt blue bike, wearing a navy cap, striped socks and bright sneakers. His jacket and shorts are built from cut-out patterns in orange, blue, green and red. The background is a flat warm peach. The wheels are drawn in orange and black with thin spokes, and the whole bike leans slightly forward, which gives the picture real speed.

It feels free and confident, the summer afternoon feeling of riding fast with no plan. The cobalt and orange work well in boys' rooms and family spaces. Hang it above a bed or desk, in a playroom, in a hallway, or by the back door where the bikes live.
