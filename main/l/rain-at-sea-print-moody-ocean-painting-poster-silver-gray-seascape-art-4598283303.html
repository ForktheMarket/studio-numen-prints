This Rain at Sea print is a painterly seascape poster of a dull silver sea under falling rain, with the horizon almost lost in the murk, for anyone who finds bad weather restful.

A pale curtain of rain hangs in the top half of the picture, brighter where thin light comes through it, and the sea below is dark slate with small choppy waves and white flecks of foam. Everything is gray from top to bottom, from near white to near black, with no warm color anywhere. It is a very simple picture with a lot of atmosphere.

It suits an office, a study, a bedroom or a minimalist living room, and it is a natural companion to the gray sea and storm front prints. It is best hung where it can be looked at quietly, such as across from a desk or beside a reading chair.
