This Butterfly Release print is a poster of a Black woman in a cream and green gown holding out her hands as a great cloud of orange butterflies lifts away from them, for anyone who has let something go and felt lighter for it.

She stands in profile on the right with her hair pinned up and her face calm, palms open. The butterflies rise from her fingertips in a long sweeping curve up to the top left corner, painted in flat orange and rust with fine black veins. The background is a plain warm sand.

The colors are orange, sand, cream, green and warm brown, hopeful and warm. It suits a bedroom, a living room, a hallway or a nursery, and it sits well above a bed or a sofa. A print for new starts, fresh chapters and anyone who collects butterflies in some form.
