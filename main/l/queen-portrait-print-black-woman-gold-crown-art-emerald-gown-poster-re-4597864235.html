This Queen Portrait print is a painterly poster of a Black woman in an emerald green gown with a gold crown nestled in her afro, head tipped back, for anyone who wants the word and the picture to match.

She stands in profile with her chin raised and her eyes nearly closed, the gown draped off one shoulder in thick strokes of forest and jade green. The crown is a ring of gold leaves pushed into her hair. The background splits into cream above and sand below, with QUEEN in tall dark green serif capitals across the top.

The colors are emerald, cream, sand, gold and warm brown, rich and calm. It suits a bedroom, a living room, a hallway or a dressing room, and it sits well above a bed, especially next to the King print from the same set. A print for women who do not wait to be told.
