This Sun Rays print is a geometric boho poster of a sunburst cropped so tight that only the rays show, fanning out from the lower left corner in rust and cream stripes, for anyone who wants a graphic piece in warm neutrals.

The rays widen as they cross the page, alternating burnt orange and off white, with a soft painted texture that keeps the edges from looking too sharp. A sliver of the sun's disc shows in the corner. It works as a bold pattern from a distance and as a painting up close.

It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or an office, and it pairs with the desert sun print for a matching pair. It also makes a good single piece above a desk, where the rays lead the eye up and out of the corner.
