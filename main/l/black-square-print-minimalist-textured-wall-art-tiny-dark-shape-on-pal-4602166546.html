This black square print shows a single tiny square of deep black placed low and to the right on an otherwise empty pale plaster surface, made for living rooms, offices and modern neutral homes.

The square is small, sharp and matte, and because it is the only mark on the sheet it pulls the whole picture around it. The plaster behind is warm off white with soft gray clouding, darker at the edges and in the lower left corner, with fine scratches and stains that give it age. The balance of large empty space and one small dark shape is the whole idea.

The palette is bone, pale gray and black, so it fits any neutral room and reads well from a distance. It suits a desk wall, a hallway or a bedroom, and anyone who likes art that is calm, spare and a little bold.
