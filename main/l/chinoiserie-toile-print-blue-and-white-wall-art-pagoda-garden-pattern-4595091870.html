The chinoiserie toile print is a blue and white pattern poster for a powder room, a bedroom or a hallway.

The whole page is filled with a garden scene painted in cobalt on a cream white ground, in the style of an old toile fabric. Three pagodas with curved roofs sit among weeping willows and broad leafy trees, one at the center and one in each lower corner. A small arched bridge carries two tiny figures over a stream, a boat with a canopy drifts across the water at the left, and a lattice fence runs along the bottom. Open stretches of pale wash stand in for sky and water between the trees. The line work is dense and fine, so there is something new to look at from every distance.

It suits a powder room, a bedroom, a hallway or a dining room, above a basin or beside a bed. It goes with white paneling, cane, brass, ticking stripes and navy paint, and it fits anyone who loves wallpaper but is not ready to hang it. It pairs with the ginger jar from the same series.
