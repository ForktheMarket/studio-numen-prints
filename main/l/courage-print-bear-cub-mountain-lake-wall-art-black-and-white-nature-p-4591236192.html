This Courage print is a black and white nature poster showing a small bear cub standing alone on a rock at the edge of a mountain lake.

The cub has walked out to the very end of a flat, wet boulder and stopped, head low, looking at the water. Its reflection lies dark on the surface just below. Around it everything is huge: a wall of pine forest on the far shore, three snow-streaked peaks behind, and cloud sitting on the highest summit. The lake is rippled in the distance and glass-smooth up close, where it mirrors the mountains. The word COURAGE crosses the middle in white capitals with wide spacing.

The cub is tiny in the picture on purpose. Being brave here just means going to the edge and looking. The scenery gives it the feel of a classic wilderness photograph, crisp and cold, with strong contrast between the black trees and the pale sky. It suits a living room, a cabin, a lake house, an entryway, a nursery or a kids room. It is a good match for hikers, campers, new parents and anyone facing something bigger than they are.
