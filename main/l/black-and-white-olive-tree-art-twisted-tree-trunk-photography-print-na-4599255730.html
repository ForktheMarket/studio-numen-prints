This black and white olive tree print is a close study of an old olive trunk twisting up toward the light, made for living rooms, offices and lovers of Mediterranean landscapes.

The trunk winds diagonally across the picture, deeply grooved and knotted, its bark carved into ridges and hollows. At the top, thin branches and small leaves spread against a bright white sky. The bark is so deeply carved that you can almost feel the age of the tree, which may have stood for hundreds of years.

It is sculptural and full of texture, almost like carved stone. Black, white and gray make it easy to place. Hang it above a sofa, in a home office, a dining room or a hallway. It suits rustic, Mediterranean and modern interiors. It also looks good in a bathroom with stone or wood finishes, where the bark texture echoes the materials around it.
