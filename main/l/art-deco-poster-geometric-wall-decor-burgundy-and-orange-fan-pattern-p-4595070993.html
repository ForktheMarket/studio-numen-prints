The fan pattern print is an Art Deco style poster for a living room, a dining room or a hallway.

Rows of fan shapes repeat edge to edge across the whole page with no border, like a run of wallpaper from a 1920s hotel. Each fan opens upward from a narrow base into a cream plume of thin rays, with amber orange panels either side and a deep burgundy arch above it, all outlined in fine lines of gold. The rows are offset so each fan sits between two in the row below, which makes the pattern scale up the page like roof tiles. The thin gold lines give it the look of an inlaid panel rather than a flat print.

It suits a living room, a dining room, a hallway or a bar area, above a sofa, over a sideboard or beside a drinks cabinet. Burgundy, amber and cream go well with dark wood, brass, velvet, black lacquer and deep green walls. It fits anyone who likes the glamour of the 1920s, and it pairs with the arched doorway from the same series.
