This Dream Big print is a black and white poster showing a small girl with a cardboard box on her head, and the giant astronaut shadow she casts on the wall in front of her.

She stands with her back to us on sunlit concrete, in shorts and sneakers, the box sitting where a helmet would go. On the tall pale wall, her shadow has grown into a full spacesuit, many times her height, with a round helmet and a pack on its back. A hard diagonal edge of shade cuts across the top of the picture. The words DREAM BIG run across the middle in bold white capitals with wide spacing.

The box is the whole joke and the whole point. Any parent has seen a child turn an empty box into a rocket. The tones are soft grays with one deep shadow, so it sits easily on a white or colored wall. It suits a kids room, a playroom, a nursery, a classroom, a hallway or a home office. It is a good match for space fans, teachers, new parents and anyone who needs a reminder of what they wanted to be at seven.
