The Ankylosaurus print is a speckled picture book poster for a nursery, a playroom or a child who likes the armored dinosaurs.

An Ankylosaurus walks to the right across a flecked cream background, low and heavy on four thick legs. Its body is a deep leafy green covered in orange spots and bumps, with a row of orange plates standing up along its back from the neck to the tail. A short horn points out from its nose and the tail ends in a round orange club. Five small dark green fern fronds are scattered around it, two above, one on each side and one below. The word ANKYLOSAURUS is printed in rounded red orange capitals along the bottom. The texture is grainy and soft, like a page from an old nature book.

It suits a nursery, a kids bedroom, a playroom or a hallway, above a crib, a bed or a toy shelf. Green and orange go with white walls, natural wood, terracotta and rattan, and it pairs with the Stegosaurus and the Diplodocus from the same series.
