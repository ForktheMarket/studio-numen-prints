This Endure print is a black and white beach poster showing a tiny sea turtle hatchling crawling across wet sand toward the surf, where a full-grown turtle is already in the waves.

The hatchling is in the foreground, no bigger than a pebble against the wide beach, flippers spread, heading for a thin sheet of water that mirrors the sky. Further out, blurred by distance, the dark shell and flipper of an adult turtle rise from the breaking surf. Storm cloud fills the top of the picture, with one patch of light coming through on the right. The word ENDURE sits across the middle in white capitals with wide spacing.

Few journeys are harder for their length than a hatchling's first trip to the sea, and the adult in the water is proof that some finish it. That is the meaning: keep going. The picture is calm despite the weather, with smooth sand, soft gray light and a lot of open space. It suits a bathroom, a living room, a beach house, a hallway, a bedroom or a waiting room. It is a good match for ocean lovers, divers, distance runners and anyone on a long road.
