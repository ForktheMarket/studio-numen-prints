This Just One More Episode print is a funny two panel comic poster for binge watchers, night owls and anyone who has promised themselves an early night and lost.

In the top panel a small yellow egg-shaped character points an orange remote at a TV on spindly legs. It is smiling, and the sun is shining in the little window behind it. The caption reads JUST ONE MORE EPISODE. In the bottom panel nothing has moved except the time. The window now shows a crescent moon and stars, the character has heavy bags under its eyes and a flat line for a mouth, and the caption says IT'S 4AM. Thick black outlines and flat color sit in two cream panels on a strong cobalt blue background.

It is a joke most households will recognize from last night. It suits a living room, a TV or movie room, a bedroom or a shared apartment where someone always asks if you are still watching. Cobalt blue and cream go well with white walls, gray sofas and mustard or orange accents.
