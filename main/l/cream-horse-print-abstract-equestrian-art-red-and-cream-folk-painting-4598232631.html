This Cream Horse print is an abstract equestrian poster cropped so close to a tall pale horse that only its belly and two long legs fit in the frame, set against a deep brick red ground, for anyone who wants a horse print that reads as a bold abstract from across the room.

The body enters from the top edge and the legs drop almost the full height of the picture, one bent slightly at the knee, each ending in a small hoof. The cream is scumbled and chalky with soft gray shadows, and the red behind it is brushed in loose layers that shift from scarlet to rust.

The colors are brick red, rust, cream and bone, bold and warm. It suits a hallway, a living room above the sofa, a dining room or an entryway, and it holds its own on a big wall. A good match for rooms with white walls, dark wood and one strong color.
