This Slow Dance in the Kitchen print is a retro poster of a Black couple holding each other close in front of the stove, for anyone whose best evenings never left the house.

She rests her head on his chest with her eyes closed, barefoot in a mustard dress, one hand on his back. He holds her with both arms, glasses on, in a white tee and olive trousers, swaying on the kitchen tiles. Behind them a run of orange and olive cabinets and a stove top sit against a cream wall, with the words in bold black capitals above.

The colors are mustard, orange, olive, cream and warm brown, straight out of a seventies kitchen. It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a bedroom or a hallway, and it sits well above a table or a sideboard. A warm print for couples, newlyweds and anyone who turns the radio up while the pasta boils.
