This Three Generations print is a poster of a Black grandmother, mother and daughter stacked one in front of the other in profile, for any family that knows exactly where its strength comes from.

The grandmother stands at the top with a crown of silver hair, a cream blouse and a gold drop earring. The mother sits in front of her in rose with a full dark afro and tassel earrings. The daughter rests at the bottom in a green sweater, her mother's hands on her shoulders, her own hands crossed over her chest. All three have their eyes closed.

The colors are cream, rose, forest green, gold and warm brown, calm and rich. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a dining room, and it sits well above a sofa or a sideboard. A print for mothers, grandmothers, daughters and the people who are all three.
