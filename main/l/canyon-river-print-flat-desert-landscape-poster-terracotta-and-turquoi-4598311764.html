This Canyon River print is a flat painted desert landscape poster of a turquoise river winding down through red rock canyon walls, for anyone who wants the colors of the Southwest on one wall.

The canyon is built from layered flat shapes in brick red, coral, salmon and deep plum, stacked back toward a dark ridge at the top of the picture. The river snakes from the far distance to the bottom edge in bright teal with a pale sandy bank on either side. The paint is matte and slightly textured, with the simplified look of cut out shapes.

It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a den, and it pairs with the mesa and zigzag mountain prints. It also sits well with terracotta pots, rattan and plants, which share its palette, and it holds up to a plain white wall.
