This Red Sun Peaks print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a single red orange disc rising over layers of misty mountain ridges, for anyone who wants a calm landscape with warmth in it.

The sun is the only hard edged shape on the page, placed high and slightly left. Below it the mountains step back in soft washes of blue gray and charcoal, each ridge fading into the cream and peach haze between it and the next. Low cloud sits in the valleys. The warm sky and the cool mountains balance each other.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a dining room or a hallway, and it is a good choice if you want the ink wash look but with a little color. It also sits well with terracotta, linen and pale wood, which pick up the warmth in the sky.
