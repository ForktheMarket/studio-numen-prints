This golden hills print shows a dry grassland landscape of rolling hills with a single dirt road winding away into the distance, made for living rooms, bedrooms and anyone who loves wide open country.

The hills are painted in soft layers of gold, straw and tan, their slopes catching warm light on one side and falling into pale shadow on the other. The road curves in from the bottom edge as two pale tracks, passes a dark clump of bushes, and disappears between the far hills. Above, the sky is a calm wash of cream and pale apricot.

There are no animals here, just land and light, which makes it one of the quieter prints in the set. The palette is warm neutral gold and sand, so it fits easily into most rooms, and suits anyone who likes a long empty road.
