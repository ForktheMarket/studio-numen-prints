This Well Shit Here We Are print is a funny sweary quote poster for anyone who has looked around at their life and had to laugh.

Five words run down the poster in thin black brush capitals on a warm, sunny yellow background. The lettering is loose and a bit wobbly, with uneven spacing and dry, scratchy strokes, like a note painted on a wall in a hurry. The yellow has faint scuffs and marks in it, which gives the whole thing a worn, lived-in look.

It is a shrug in poster form, and it fits most situations: a new house, a new baby, a Monday. The yellow is cheerful enough to stop the line sounding bleak, and it goes well with white, gray, navy and natural wood. It suits an entryway, a hallway, the wall by the front door, a bathroom, a kitchen or a home office. It is a fitting choice for a first home or a couple who moved somewhere on a whim.
