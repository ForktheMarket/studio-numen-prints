This Folk Rooster print is a folk art kitchen poster of a tall rooster in rust and black with a striped cream tail, standing proud on a warm ochre ground, for anyone who wants a farmhouse picture with some character.

The bird is built from a few flat shapes: a red comb, a black neck and legs, a rust body with a dark heart shaped belly, and a big sweeping tail of cream and black stripes. The eye is a single dot. There is no background detail at all, just the rooster and the color behind it.

It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a pantry wall or a hallway, and it looks right above a table or a dresser. It also pairs with the two red horses print, which shares the same flat shapes and warm yellow ground.
