The French 75 print is a vintage style cocktail poster for a home bar, a dining room or a dressing room.

A coupe glass of pale pink sparkling drink sits in the center of a peach colored panel, with a dried rose laid across the top of the glass as the garnish. The panel is edged with a thin gold line and a speckled pink inner border, like an old label. Beneath it a cream ribbon banner carries the drink name in gold capitals, French 75, and the recipe is lettered below in the same gold on three lines: Gin, Lemon, Champagne. The whole print is soft pink, peach, cream and gold, in a warm, slightly hazy light.

It suits a home bar, a dining room, a bedroom or a dressing room, hung above a drinks trolley or beside a mirror. Pink and gold go well with blush paint, brass, white marble and velvet. It fits champagne drinkers and anyone who likes a cocktail with a bit of romance, and it pairs with the Dry Martini and the Orange Spritz from the same series.
