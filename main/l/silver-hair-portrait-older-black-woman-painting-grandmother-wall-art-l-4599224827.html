This silver hair portrait is a painting of an older Black woman with a warm, knowing smile, made for living rooms, family rooms and anyone who wants age shown with real dignity.

Her natural hair is gray and silver, swept up and loose around her face, and she wears small gold hoops and a dark navy wrap around her shoulders. She looks straight at us with a small smile. The background is a soft blue gray with brushy strokes and patches of light. The paint is looser in the hair and clothes and more careful in the face, so the eyes and smile are where you look first.

The palette is navy, slate blue, silver and warm brown. It is a gentle picture with a lot of character in the face. It suits a living room, a hallway, a reading room or a bedroom, and pairs well with family photos.
