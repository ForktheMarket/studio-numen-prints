The Verbier ski poster is a vintage style travel print for anyone who has watched the sky go pink over a snowy village at the end of a long day on the hill.

A small Alpine village with a slim church spire sits at the foot of a great snow covered peak, painted in deep teal greens against a coral pink evening sky with a few drifting clouds. A clean cream slope fills the foreground and the resort name is set in heavy dark green capitals along the bottom. The pink and green pairing is unusual for a ski print and gives it a softer, more decorative feel.
