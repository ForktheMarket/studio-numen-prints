This Prickly Pear print is a folk art botanical poster of a cluster of flat dark green cactus pads dotted with pale spots and topped with bright pink fruit, on a plain cream ground, for anyone who wants a cactus with some color.

The pads are stacked up from the bottom of the page like cut out shapes, each one a slightly different green, and the fruit sits along their top edges in hot pink ovals with tiny red dots. The spines are painted as soft pale dots rather than needles, so it is friendly rather than prickly. The cream background keeps it light.

It suits a kitchen, a bathroom, a bedroom or a sunroom, and it pairs with the saguaro print for a cactus pair. It also brings a cheerful note to a rented kitchen or bathroom where you cannot change the tiles.
