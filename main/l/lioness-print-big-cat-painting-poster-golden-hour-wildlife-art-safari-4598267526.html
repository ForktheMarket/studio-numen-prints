This Lioness print is a painterly wildlife poster of a lioness looking back over her shoulder in low golden light, her face and ears lit warm against a cool gray sky, for anyone who wants a big cat portrait that feels calm and watchful rather than roaring.

She is seen from behind and turned toward the viewer, one amber eye catching the light, the fur along her cheek and ears painted in bright strokes of gold and copper. Her shoulders and back run down into deep shadow, the coat brushed in long dark streaks. The background shifts from slate gray at the top to a peach glow at the bottom.

The colors are gold, copper, amber, dark brown and slate gray, warm against cool. It suits a living room above the sofa, a home office, a hallway or a bedroom, and it fits safari, neutral and darker modern rooms with leather, brass and wood. A strong piece for anyone who admires big cats or wants a watchful presence in the room.
