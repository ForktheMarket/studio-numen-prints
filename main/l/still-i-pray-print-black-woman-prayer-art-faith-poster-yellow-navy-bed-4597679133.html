This Still I Pray print is a faith poster of a Black woman in a white head wrap kneeling in prayer, for anyone who holds on to prayer through hard seasons.

She kneels in profile in a deep navy dress with her hands folded in her lap and her eyes closed, a pale gold sun rising behind her head like a halo. The background is a warm block of marigold yellow with a cream band above and below, and STILL I PRAY sits in clean navy capitals across the top.

The colors are marigold, navy, cream and warm brown, bright and steady rather than soft. It suits a bedroom, a prayer corner, a hallway or a living room, and it sits well above a bed or a side table with a candle. A grounding print for women of faith, church homes and anyone who starts the day on their knees.
