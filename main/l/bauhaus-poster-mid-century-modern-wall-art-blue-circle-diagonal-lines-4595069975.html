The blue circle print is a Bauhaus style poster for an office, a living room or a hallway.

A large pale sky blue circle sits just above the middle of a cream background, with a chalky, speckled texture across it. A wide cobalt blue band cuts across the page from the bottom left corner up to the top right, passing behind the lower half of the circle and then over its upper edge. Crossing the other way, two thin black bars run from the top left down to the bottom right, parallel to each other and laid over everything else. The three directions pull against each other, and the two blues against the black give it a cool, sharp mood.

It suits a home office, a living room, a hallway or a study, above a desk, over a sofa or on a landing. Cobalt, pale blue, black and cream go well with white walls, light oak, chrome, concrete and navy fabrics. It fits anyone who prefers cool colors and straight lines, and it pairs with the mustard and navy circle from the same series.
