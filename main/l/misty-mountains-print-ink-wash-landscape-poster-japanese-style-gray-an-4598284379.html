This Misty Mountains print is an ink wash style landscape poster of three mountain ridges stacked one behind the other into the haze, with a single small red tree on the nearest peak, for anyone who wants a calm picture with one spark of color.

The nearest ridge is painted in dense dark ink with rough texture, the middle one in softer gray, and the farthest one in the faintest wash, almost the same tone as the cream sky. The red tree is tiny but it is the first thing you see. The composition is tall and narrow and the mist does most of the work.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a study or a landing, and it pairs well with the lone pine and red sun prints. The tall format also makes it a good fit for the narrow wall between two doors or at the top of a staircase.
