This Mood Unavailable print is a funny two-word quote poster for introverts, teenagers and anyone who is not taking questions today.

The words are brushed in black across a hot neon pink background that leans toward red. Mood sits on top in wide, slanted capitals, and Unavailable runs underneath in taller, tighter letters that climb slightly to the right. The brush was loaded and fast, so the strokes are solid black with dry, streaky tails, most obviously on the last D.

It works like an out of office notice for your face. The pink is intense, so it holds its own on a white wall and needs very little around it. Black, white and chrome all go well with it. It suits a bedroom, a teen room, a dorm, a home office or the wall beside a desk where people keep stopping to chat. It also gives a strong single splash of color to an otherwise neutral room.
