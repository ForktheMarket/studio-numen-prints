This Grace print is a black and white ballet poster showing a little girl in a leotard and ballet slippers, standing still and watching a grown ballerina dance on pointe.

The child is in the foreground with her back to us, hair pinned up, arms at her sides, a long shadow trailing behind her on the studio floor. Across the room, soft and bright in the window light, the ballerina balances on her toes in a white calf-length tutu with one arm curved overhead and the other reaching out. An old paneled wall and a wooden barre run behind them. The word GRACE sits low in the picture in white serif capitals with wide spacing.

The girl is not dancing yet. She is just looking, and you can tell she is working out how it is done. The light is hazy and soft, like an old photograph from a dance school. It suits a girls room, a nursery, a bedroom, a dance studio, a hallway or a dressing area. It is a good match for young dancers, ballet teachers, dance moms and anyone who once stood at the barre.
