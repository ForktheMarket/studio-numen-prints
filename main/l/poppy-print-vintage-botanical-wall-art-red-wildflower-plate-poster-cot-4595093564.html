The poppy print is a vintage botanical plate poster for a kitchen, a living room or anyone who loves a red wildflower.

A single open poppy fills the upper half of the plate, four crumpled scarlet petals with creased folds like thin silk around a dark center of black stamens and a green seed head. Below it, two hairy green stems cross each other, one carrying a ripe seed pod with a flat crown and the other a bristly unopened bud still bowed on its stalk. The ground is a plain warm cream, and the word POPPY is lettered in small spaced red capitals at the bottom. The drawing has the fine lines of an old engraved plate, with the red kept bright against the cream.

It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a living room or a hallway, on its own or in a row with others from the same series. Scarlet, dark green and cream go well with white walls, oak, terracotta and black metal, and it fits gardeners and anyone who stops for poppies at the edge of a field.
