The Portland Bill print is a linocut style lighthouse poster for a coastal bathroom, a hallway or a Dorset loving home.

A white lighthouse with a single broad red band around its middle stands on a dark headland, slightly right of center. The tower is cut with fine scratchy lines that give the white render a worn look, and the red band carries the same marks so it looks carved rather than drawn. Below the rocks the sea is a mass of navy with cream slashes for spray, and a strip of pale gray coast sits on the left horizon. The sky is flat warm cream, which makes the tower stand taller. Three colors, navy, red and cream. The words PORTLAND BILL run across the bottom in bold navy capitals.

It suits a bathroom, a downstairs toilet, a hallway or a boy's bedroom, above a bath or on the stairs. Navy, red and cream go with white paneling, brass and striped towels. It fits sailors, swimmers and Dorset regulars, and it hangs well beside Longstone and Start Point from the same series.
