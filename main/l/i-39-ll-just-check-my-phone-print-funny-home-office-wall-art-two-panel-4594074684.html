This I'll Just Check My Phone print is a funny two panel comic poster for scrollers, procrastinators and anyone who has picked up their phone for one minute and looked up an hour later.

In the top panel a small yellow egg-shaped character holds up a blue phone and smiles at it. A wall calendar beside it reads JAN 1, and the caption says I'LL JUST CHECK MY PHONE. In the bottom panel the calendar reads DEC 31. The character has not moved. It is wrapped in cobwebs, one spider hangs on a thread above its head and another sits in a web in the corner, and it is still smiling at the screen. The caption reads IT IS NOW DECEMBER. Thick black outlines and flat color sit in two cream panels on a marigold yellow background.

The exaggeration is what makes it funny, and only slightly unfair. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a teenager's bedroom, a dorm room or a study. Yellow and cream go well with white walls, black shelving and natural wood.
