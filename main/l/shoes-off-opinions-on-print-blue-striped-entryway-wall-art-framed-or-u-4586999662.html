This Shoes Off Opinions On print is a funny entryway quote poster for households that like clean floors and a good argument over dinner.

The four words are stacked in bold cobalt blue block capitals on a cream background, with the longest word stretched almost edge to edge. Bands of diagonal blue stripes cut across all four corners, which gives it the look of an old shop sign or a vintage matchbox. The blue is bright and clean, and the edges of the letters are slightly rough, as if rolled on with ink.

It does a real job, since it asks guests to take their shoes off, but it does it with a joke instead of a rule. It suits an entryway, a hallway, a mudroom, the wall by the front door or the space above a shoe rack. The blue and cream also work well in a kitchen or dining room where the opinions usually come out.
