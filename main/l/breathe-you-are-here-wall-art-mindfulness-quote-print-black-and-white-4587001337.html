This Breathe You Are Here print is a calming black and white quote poster for anyone who needs a reminder to slow down and come back to the moment.

Four words are painted in black brush capitals across three lines, on a cream background ruled with thin horizontal lines like a page from a school notebook. The lettering is loose and quick, with dry, scratchy edges, and it ignores the ruled lines completely. It looks like a note someone wrote to themselves in big letters so they could not miss it.

It is a quiet instruction, and it works best somewhere you will see it when the day speeds up. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits with almost any color scheme and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a bedroom, the wall above the bed, a yoga or meditation corner, a therapy office, a bathroom or a home office. It is a good match for busy parents, students in exam season and anyone practicing mindfulness.
