This Upright Bass print is a painterly poster of a Black musician with his eyes closed and his head tipped back, fingers walking up the neck of a double bass, for anyone who feels the low end before they hear it.

He wears a cream shirt open over a dark vest, hair in loose twists, lost in the tune. The bass rises up the right side of the picture in warm wood and black strings, and behind him two bold vertical bands of teal and orange cut through the cream background.

The colors are cream, teal, orange, wood brown and deep brown, with loose visible brushwork. It suits a music room, a living room, a home bar or a study, and it pairs well with the Saxophone Player print from the same set. A print for jazz heads, bass players and anyone who notices the rhythm section.
