This indigo stripe print is a bold abstract in the style of resist-dyed cloth, for living rooms, hallways and anyone who likes navy with a spot of orange.

Five wide cream bands run from top to bottom, separated by bands of very dark indigo blue. The cream bands are covered in a fine crackle, like dried clay or a pebbled path, and their edges wobble slightly, so nothing looks ruled. Across the middle runs one narrow band of bright orange dots, cutting through all the stripes.

The orange band is what you notice first, and it keeps the stripes from feeling stiff. The picture has a hand-worked, textile look and is strong enough to carry a wall without anything beside it. Navy, cream and orange sit well with wood, leather, tan and white walls. It suits a living room, a hallway, a stair landing, a home office or a dining room. It is a good match for people who like mid century modern rooms, boho textiles and African and Japanese dyed cloth.
