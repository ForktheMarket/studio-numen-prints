This Nothing Is Wasted print is an encouraging quote poster for writers, makers, students and anyone who worries that a wrong turn was time lost.

Three words are painted in thick cobalt blue brush capitals across the middle of a cream page ruled with fine blue lines and a thin red margin, like a sheet from a legal pad. The letters slant and jostle, heavy with paint in some places and dry and streaky in others. There is plenty of empty ruled space above and below, so the words sit like a note left on an otherwise empty page.

The idea is that the failed drafts, the jobs that did not work out and the years spent on something else all count toward what comes next. Blue and cream go well with white walls, pale wood and brass. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, an art studio, a craft room, a classroom or a teen bedroom. It fits graduates, career changers, artists and anyone starting again.
