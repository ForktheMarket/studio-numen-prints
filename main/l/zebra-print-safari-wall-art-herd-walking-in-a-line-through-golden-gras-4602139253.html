This zebra print shows a long line of zebras walking away along a narrow track through sunlit golden grass, made for living rooms, nurseries and anyone who loves the savanna.

The nearest zebras are large in the foreground, their black and white stripes catching warm light, and the line winds back into the distance until the animals become a dark thread on the horizon. The grassland glows straw gold and amber, with a band of distant trees and a pale peach sky above. A thin stream catches the light at the bottom edge.

The colors are warm gold and cream with the sharp contrast of the stripes, so it brings both calm and pattern to a wall. It suits a living room, a hallway or a child's bedroom, and anyone who has watched a herd on the move.
