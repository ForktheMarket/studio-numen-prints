This toy xylophone print is a soft green nursery poster for a baby's room, a playroom or a music room.

A wooden xylophone on wheels lies at an angle across a muted green background, seen from above. Its bars run from two bright reds through orange, cream and golden yellow to a single teal bar, each fixed with small round pins. The ends are plain pale wood, and two round headed mallets rest beside it, crossed over each other. A fine speckle across the green gives it the feel of a page from an old toy catalog.

The colors are warmer and gentler than most rainbow toys, so it does not shout. It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or the wall above a piano. Green, red and amber go well with white or cream walls, light wood, rattan and terracotta fabrics, and it pairs well with the toy drum from the same series.
