This woman reading on the beach print shows a Black woman with an afro lost in a book under a green umbrella, made for beach houses, reading nooks and anyone who counts the days to summer.

She sits on a patterned towel in an orange print sundress and sunglasses, book open on her knees. Above her is a wide emerald umbrella, and behind her the sea rolls in in stripes of green and white foam under a pale blue sky. Her sunglasses and the strap of her sundress catch small bits of detail, and the water behind her is drawn in neat bands of foam.

Emerald, orange and sand tones make it bright but relaxed. It is the picture of a perfect day off. Hang it in a reading corner, a bedroom, a bathroom, or anywhere in a beach house or coastal home.
