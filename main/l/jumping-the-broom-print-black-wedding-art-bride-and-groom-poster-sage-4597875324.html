This Jumping the Broom print is a wedding poster of a Black bride and groom leaping together over a ribboned broom, for any couple who kept the tradition on their big day.

She soars in a long cream gown with her veil streaming behind her, arms wide and chin up. He jumps beside her in a cream suit with one fist punching the air. Below them the broom lies tied with gold ribbon, and a small crowd of guests cheers from the left. The background is a soft sage green with a faint speckle.

The colors are sage, cream, gold and warm brown, elegant and happy. It suits a bedroom, a living room, a hallway or an entryway, and it sits well above a bed or a dresser. A print for newlyweds, anniversaries and families who have jumped that broom for generations.
