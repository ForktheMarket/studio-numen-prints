This magnolia print shows a large magnolia bloom opening in soft blush petals against a black background, cropped close so the flower fills the picture, made for bedrooms, living rooms and anyone who loves spring flowers.

The petals are painted in warm peach pink, paler at the edges and deeper at the base, curling outward from a center of fine golden stamens. A second bloom sits half hidden at the top left, and a touch of dark branch shows at the bottom corner. Soft light from the right gives the petals a gentle glow.

The palette is blush, peach and black, romantic without being sweet, which suits a bedroom above the headboard, a dressing room or a living room with pink or neutral tones. It belongs to anyone who waits for the magnolias each year. It makes a gentle pair with the pink lotus or the blush protea from the same set.
