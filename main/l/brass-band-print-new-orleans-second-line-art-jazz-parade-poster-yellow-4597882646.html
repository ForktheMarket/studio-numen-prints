This Brass Band print is a painterly poster of a New Orleans second line marching across a wide yellow wall, sousaphone first and purple parasol last, for anyone who has followed a band down the street without knowing where it was going.

Six musicians in white jackets, straw hats and orange trousers stride left to right along the bottom of the picture, the sousaphone bell flashing gold, trombones raised. A man in green with a purple parasol leads the way. Above them is nothing but a tall wash of butter yellow with long shadows on the ground.

The colors are yellow, white, orange, purple and warm brown, sunny and loud. It suits a living room, a music room, a kitchen, a hallway or a home bar, and it sits well above a sofa or a sideboard. A print for jazz lovers, parade followers and anyone missing the city.
