This Silhouette Pattern print is a monochrome poster of dozens of small Black profiles in neat rows, each with a different hairstyle, for anyone who wants every kind of crown on one wall.

The heads face left and right in alternating rows, cut as solid black shapes on cream. There are afros big and small, locs, twists, cornrows, head wraps, high puffs, bantu knots and close fades, with a hoop earring here and there. No two are quite the same, and the rows shift a little each line so the eye keeps moving.

There is no color at all, which keeps it clean and lets the shapes do the work. It suits a salon, a bathroom, a bedroom or a hallway, and the repeat breaks up a run of single portraits in a gallery wall. A print for stylists, barbers and anyone who loves a hair chart.
