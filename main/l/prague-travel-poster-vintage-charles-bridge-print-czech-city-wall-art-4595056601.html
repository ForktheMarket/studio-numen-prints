This Prague print is a vintage style travel poster for a bedroom, a living room or anyone who has crossed the river at dawn.

The old stone bridge runs in from the left in charcoal silhouette, its arches and statues lined along the parapet against a peach pink sky under a pale sun. Behind it the castle and cathedral spires rise over slate gray hills, with the bridge tower and a cluster of rooftops below. The river fills the lower right and carries the pink of the sky in soft ripples. The shapes are flat and simple, closer to a screen print than a painting, and the mood is still and early. The word PRAGUE sits underneath in dark navy block capitals on a cream border.

It suits a bedroom, a living room or a home office, above a bed or a desk. Peach, charcoal and cream go with white walls, black metal, pale wood and blush textiles, and it fits anyone who has been to Prague or keeps a list of cities still to see. It hangs well beside the other cities in the series.
