This brown trout print is a vintage fishing poster for a cabin, a den or anyone who fishes rivers for wild trout.

A brown trout leaps clear of the water in a tight curve, mouth open and head turned up. Its back is olive brown, its flank a warm rust orange thick with dark spots, and its fins and tail are drawn in the same browns with fine darker lines. An arc of dark olive and white spray follows the fish up and over, and below it the river breaks into white foam over dark green water. The background is plain warm cream, and the words BROWN TROUT run along the bottom in heavy dark brown capitals. Flat color and rough edges give it the look of an old screen printed poster.

It suits a cabin, a hallway, a study or a kitchen, above a desk or beside a doorway. Cream, rust and olive go well with dark wood, tartan, leather and green walls. It fits trout anglers, and it pairs with the rainbow trout and the salmon from the same series.
