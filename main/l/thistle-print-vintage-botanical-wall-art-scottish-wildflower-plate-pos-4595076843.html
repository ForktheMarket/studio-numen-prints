The thistle print is a vintage botanical plate poster for a hallway, a living room or anyone with a soft spot for Scotland.

A single thistle stands upright in the center of the plate, a round purple flower head of fine silky threads sitting on a large scaly green cup. Below it the thick pale green stem throws out deeply cut spiny leaves to both sides, the spines picked out in gold, with a second small bud on a side shoot to the left. The ground is an aged yellow cream, darker toward the corners, and the word THISTLE is lettered in small spaced olive green capitals at the bottom with a full stop after it, the way old plates were labeled. It has the fine lines of an old engraved plate.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a study or a bar wall, and it holds its own beside the foxglove from the same series. Purple, green and gold go well with white walls, dark wood, tartan, leather and brass, and it fits gardeners, Scots and anyone who likes a plant with some fight.
