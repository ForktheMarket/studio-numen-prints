This Patience print is a black and white poster showing a baby elephant at a waterhole, with a huge tusked adult walking through the dust behind it.

The calf stands on dark, churned mud at the water's edge with its trunk hanging down and its ears forward. Behind it, the adult fills the right side of the picture, cut off at the shoulder, with long pale tusks and deeply wrinkled skin. A single baobab tree stands in the haze between them. Both elephants are mirrored in the still water below. The word PATIENCE crosses the middle in thin white capitals with wide spacing.

The small one and the big one in the same picture tell the story. Growing takes years, and nothing about it can be rushed. The mood is calm and dusty, with soft gray sky above and strong black shapes below. It suits a living room, a nursery, a kids room, a home office, a hallway or a waiting room. It is a good match for new parents, teachers, elephant lovers, safari travelers and anyone learning to take the long view.
