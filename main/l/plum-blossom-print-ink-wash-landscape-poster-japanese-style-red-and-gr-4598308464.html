This Plum Blossom print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a dark plum branch heavy with red blossoms reaching across the top of the picture, with misty mountains and a river valley far below, for anyone who wants a traditional landscape with a splash of color.

The branch comes in from the upper right, painted in rough black ink, and the blossoms are small clusters of vermilion and coral that are the only strong color on the page. Below it the mountains fade back in soft gray and blue washes, and bare trees and rocks sit in the foreground at the bottom. The blossom in front and the vast space behind give it depth.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a dining room or an entrance hall, and it pairs with the bamboo print.
