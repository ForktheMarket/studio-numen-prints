This Monstera Wrap print is a portrait poster of a Black woman in profile wearing a tall cream and gold head wrap against a giant green monstera leaf, for anyone whose home is already half jungle.

She faces left with her eyes closed and a large gold disc earring at her ear, painted in flat warm brown. The wrap is folded high in soft strokes of cream and honey. Behind her the leaf spreads edge to edge in deep green, its cuts and holes showing cream through the gaps.

The colors are forest green, cream, honey, gold and brown, lush and warm. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a bathroom or a hallway, and it looks right among real plants above a sofa or a bed. A print for plant parents, head wrap wearers and anyone who likes green walls without the paint.
