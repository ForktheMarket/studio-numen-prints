This bridge painting print shows a great stone and cable suspension bridge rising out of mist over a pale river, made for living rooms, offices and apartments.

The bridge is painted as a few dark strokes against a soft gray sky, its towers and cables sketched rather than drawn, with small lights strung along the span. Below, the water is cool silver and blue, broken by long reflections of amber lamps that reach almost to the bottom of the sheet. A faint skyline fades into the fog behind.

The feeling is calm and a little melancholy, the city seen from the waterfront on a wet evening. The colors are gray, slate and warm orange, so it suits a neutral living room, a dark study or a hallway. It belongs to anyone who loves bridges, rivers and quiet city moments.
