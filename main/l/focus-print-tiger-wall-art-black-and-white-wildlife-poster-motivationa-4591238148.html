This Focus print is a black and white wildlife poster showing a young tiger crouched at the edge of a jungle pool, staring straight out of the picture, with its face reflected in the dark water.

The tiger is low to the ground with one paw forward, ears pricked, white whiskers fanned out against the shadows. Its eyes are level and fixed on you. Tall grass and bamboo close in behind it. Below the bank the water is nearly black, and the reflected face looks back up from it, a little softer and broken by the surface. The word FOCUS sits across the middle in bold white capitals with wide spacing.

Everything about the animal is still except its attention. That is what the picture is for: one thing, looked at properly.
