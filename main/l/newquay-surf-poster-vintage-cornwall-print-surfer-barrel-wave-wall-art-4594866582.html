The Newquay surf poster is a vintage style travel print for surfers and for anyone who has spent a summer on the north coast of Cornwall.

A lone surfer crouches low on a longboard inside the curl of a tall green wave, the lip throwing over above them in a long curve of cream spray. The water is drawn in layers of teal, sea green and deep bottle green, with a pale mint sky in the corner, and the word Newquay sits in chunky orange capitals along the bottom. The orange against all that green makes the name jump out from across the room.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom, a hallway, a beach house or a surf shop, and it works well above a bath, a bed or a sofa. It pairs with the Bondi Beach, Biarritz and Nazare prints for a gallery wall of surf towns.
