The Malibu surf poster is a vintage style California print for anyone who has walked up a beach at the end of a session with the board under one arm and nothing left to prove.

A lone surfer carries a longboard across pale orange sand toward the dunes, casting a long dark shadow, with a soft mint sky fading to cream and a low green headland on the right. The lettering sits in warm coral at the top. The scene is mostly empty space, which is what gives it that late afternoon calm. Peach, mint and cream keep the whole thing light.

It suits a bedroom, a bathroom, a hallway or a beach house living room, and it works well above a bed or in a gallery wall of coastal prints. Pair it with the other breaks in the set for a wall of surf towns.
