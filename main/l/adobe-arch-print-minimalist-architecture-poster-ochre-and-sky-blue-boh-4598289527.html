This Adobe Arch print is a minimalist architecture poster of a tall arched doorway in a flat ochre wall, with nothing but clear blue sky showing through it, for anyone who likes simple shapes and warm color.

The wall fills the whole picture in a soft mustard yellow with a sandy floor along the bottom. The arch is outlined in a darker ochre shadow on one side and a pale cream edge on the other, and the opening is a block of flat sky blue. There is no detail beyond that. The contrast of the warm wall and the cool blue opening is the entire design.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a bedroom or a sunroom, and it pairs well with the saguaro and mesa prints from the same series. It also makes a quiet pair with the saguaro print, which uses the same cream and ochre.
