The blue orchid print is a chinoiserie still life poster for a living room, a bedroom or a plant lover.

Three stems of moth orchids rise from a small blue and white pot, with a dozen open blooms in cobalt and pale blue and a few buds at the tips. The leaves are the one break from the blue palette, a deep bottle green sitting low around the pot. The pot stands on a barrel shaped porcelain garden stool with pierced lattice sides and a band of peonies top and bottom, all on a soft white ground with a pale shadow beneath. The line work is fine and tidy, more like a painted plate than a loose sketch, and the mood is calm and formal.

It suits a living room, a bedroom, a sunroom or a hallway, above a console table or beside a window. Blue, white and green go with cream walls, cane, bamboo, brass and pale wood, and it fits gardeners and anyone drawn to coastal grandmother rooms. It pairs with the ginger jar and the butterfly peony from the same series.
