This Dark Horse print is panel two of a pair, a folk art poster of a tall black horse standing beneath a huge red sun that fills the top of the picture, on a strip of golden ground against a pale cream sky, for anyone who wants a strong, simple pair of prints that belong together.

The horse is a solid black silhouette with long slim legs, a lowered head and a short tail, its edges slightly rough. The sun is a big disc of rust red brushed in loose strokes, hanging directly above the horse's back. The sky is a soft plastered cream and the ground is a warm ochre band.

The colors are black, cream, ochre and rust red, bold and calm. It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a hallway or a landing, and it is made to hang beside its partner, which shows a white horse with the sun low at the horizon. Together the pair reads as morning and evening.
