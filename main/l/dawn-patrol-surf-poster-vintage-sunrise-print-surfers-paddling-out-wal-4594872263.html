The Dawn Patrol surf poster is a vintage style sunrise print for anyone who sets the alarm for first light and paddles out before the car park fills.

Three surfers sit on their boards in dark teal water, facing a half risen sun on the horizon under a flat peach sky. The words sit in deep green across the top. Light catches the ripples in thin lines of pink and cream, and the water gets darker toward the bottom edge. It has the flat color and visible ink of an old screen print, and a very still, early feeling.

It suits a bedroom, a bathroom or a living room with a coastal feel, and it works well above a bed where the warm sky picks up morning light. It also pairs with the other breaks in the set.
