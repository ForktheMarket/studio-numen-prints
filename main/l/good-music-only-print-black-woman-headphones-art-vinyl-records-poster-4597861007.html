This Good Music Only print is a painterly poster of a Black woman lying on her side with headphones on and her head resting on a stack of records, eyes closed, for anyone whose idea of a perfect evening is one album, start to finish.

She wears a white top with her hair tied up, cheek on her arm, the records stacked in cream, red and black beneath her. The scene sits in a wide band of mustard with a patch of teal, under GOOD MUSIC ONLY in dark green serif capitals on cream.

The colors are mustard, teal, cream and warm brown, loose and sunlit. It suits a music room, a bedroom, a living room or a den, and it sits well above a record player or a bed. A print for listeners, collectors and anyone who flips the record over instead of pressing skip.
