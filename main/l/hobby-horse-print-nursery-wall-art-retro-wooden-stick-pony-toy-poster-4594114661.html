This hobby horse print is a warm yellow nursery poster for a child's bedroom, a playroom or a home with a horse lover in it.

The head of a stick horse fills the upper half of the picture, tilted to the right on a long pale pole that runs down to the bottom edge. The horse is painted cream, with a thick navy blue mane swept back over its neck, a bright red bridle and a small dark eye. It has a calm, slightly shy expression. The background is one flat field of golden yellow with a fine fleck in it, in the style of a mid century children's book illustration.

It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or a child's reading corner, and it sits well next to the rocking horse from the same series. Yellow, navy and red go well with white walls, light wood furniture, denim blue and striped fabrics.
