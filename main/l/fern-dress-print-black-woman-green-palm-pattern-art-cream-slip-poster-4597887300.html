This Fern Dress print is a poster of a Black woman in a simple cream slip dress standing in front of a dense pattern of green palm fronds, for anyone who wants calm and jungle at the same time.

She stands small and still at the bottom center, hair cropped close, hands at her sides, her face left plain so she could be anyone. Around and above her the fronds fill every inch of the page in deep green strokes on cream, overlapping like the walls of a greenhouse.

The colors are forest green, cream and warm brown, just three, which keeps it restful. It suits a bedroom, a living room, a bathroom or a hallway, and it sits well above a bed or among real plants. A print for plant lovers, minimalists and anyone who feels calmer surrounded by leaves.
