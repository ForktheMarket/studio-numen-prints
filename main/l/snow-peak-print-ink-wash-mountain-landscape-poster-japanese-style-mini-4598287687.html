This Snow Peak print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a snow covered mountain shoulder rising out of mist, with a dark rocky ridge in the foreground, for anyone who wants a mountain picture that stays calm.

The snow is left as the pale cream of the page, shaped by gray shadows on the ridges and a few dark rock faces showing through. Below the peak the slopes dissolve into haze, and the near ridge is painted in heavy black ink with two faint red marks on it. A red seal sits in the lower right. The picture is mostly light, with weight only at the bottom.

It suits a living room, a bedroom, an office or a cabin, and it pairs with the misty mountains print for a cool, quiet pair. It also suits a room decorated in white, gray and pale wood, where the cream of the page looks warm by comparison.
