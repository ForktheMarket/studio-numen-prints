This indigo circle print is an abstract in the style of tie-dyed indigo cloth, for bedrooms, living rooms and anyone who likes deep blue on the wall.

One large circle fills the picture. It is built from rings of small cream dots, one inside the next, getting smaller toward a tiny ring at the center. The outer rings are soft and blurred at the edges, the way dye bleeds where cloth has been tied. The background is deep navy blue with the texture of woven fabric showing through, darker in some places and lighter in others.

It is calm and a little hypnotic. Your eye goes to the middle and stays there. With only two colors, deep indigo and warm cream, it fits blue, white and neutral rooms and looks good next to wood, linen and rattan. It suits the wall above a bed, a living room, a reading corner, a yoga or meditation space or a bathroom. It is a good match for people who like Japanese shibori, boho and coastal rooms, and simple pattern.
