This Pine Forest print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a dense line of pines painted as one dark mass across the lower half, with misty hills and a tiny red roofed temple behind, for anyone who wants a calm forest picture.

The trees are tall and thin, their trunks and crowns built up in layers of black and charcoal ink, and the mist between them lightens toward the right. Behind them softer gray hills roll back under a warm cream sky, and the red roof is a small dot of color just above the tree line. The picture is dark at the bottom and light at the top, which makes it feel settled.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room or a study, and it pairs with the mountain road print.
