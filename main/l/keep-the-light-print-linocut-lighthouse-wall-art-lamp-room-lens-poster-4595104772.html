The Keep the Light print is a linocut style lighthouse lamp room poster for a hallway, a study or anyone who likes a bold graphic.

You are inside the lamp room looking up. The lens fills the right half of the picture, a drum of ridged glass in thick black ink with yellow showing through, and a second lens sits in the bottom corner. Above it the iron glazing bars fan out in a web of black lines, with diamond panes of soft yellow and smoky blue gray. One burst of pure yellow marks the light behind the glass. The cut marks are heavy and quick, and only three colors are used, black, yellow and a muted blue. The words KEEP THE LIGHT run across the bottom in tall black capitals.

It suits a hallway, a home office, a stairwell or a dark living room, above a desk or on the stairs. Black and yellow go with white walls, charcoal paint and brass. It fits engineers, sailors and anyone who has climbed a lighthouse, and it pairs well with Start Point from the same series.
