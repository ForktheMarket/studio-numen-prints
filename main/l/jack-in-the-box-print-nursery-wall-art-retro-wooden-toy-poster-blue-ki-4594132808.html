This jack in the box print is a bright primary color poster for a child's bedroom, a playroom or a nursery.

A round wooden head has popped up on a coiled spring from a chunky yellow box. It wears a tall red cone hat with a ball on the tip, and it has two dot eyes, a peg nose, round ears and a small curved smile. The background is one solid, vivid blue. The box and head are lit from the right, so the yellow has a bright side and a deeper orange side, and a soft shadow falls to the left.

The face is friendly rather than clownish, so there is nothing here to spook a small child. It suits a toddler bedroom, a playroom, a nursery or a classroom wall. Blue, yellow and red go well with white walls, natural wood, gray and striped fabrics.
