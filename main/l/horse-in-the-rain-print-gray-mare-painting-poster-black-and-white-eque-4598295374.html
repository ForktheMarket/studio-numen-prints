This Horse in the Rain print is a painterly equestrian poster of a pale gray horse standing alone in the wet, head lowered, for anyone who likes animal art with some quiet in it.

The horse is lit from above against a near black background, so its back and mane glow white while its legs fade into the dark, wet ground and their own reflection. The mane hangs forward over the face and the stance is patient rather than sad. Apart from a faint sheen on the ground there is nothing else in the picture.

Because it is almost entirely black, white and gray it goes with most rooms without a fight. It suits a living room above the sofa, a hallway, a study or a farmhouse bedroom, and it holds its own as a single large piece.
