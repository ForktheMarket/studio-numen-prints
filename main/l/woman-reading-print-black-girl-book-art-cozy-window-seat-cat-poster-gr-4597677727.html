This Woman Reading print is a painterly poster of a Black woman curled up on a window seat with a book and a sleeping cat, for anyone whose idea of a good afternoon is a sunny corner and no plans.

She sits with her knees up and bare feet tucked in, round glasses on, chin resting on one hand, lost in a book with a coral cover. A tabby cat sleeps against her legs on a mustard cushion. Light pours through a tall sash window and throws long shadows across sage green walls, with a trailing plant on the left.

The style is loose brushwork in olive, mustard, denim blue and warm skin tones, closer to an oil sketch than a flat graphic. It suits a reading corner, a living room, a bedroom or a home office, and it holds its own as a single piece above a sofa. A quiet print for readers, cat people and anyone who guards their Sunday afternoons.
