This Keep Going print is a bold black and cream quote poster for anyone halfway through something hard, such as a degree, a training plan or a long year.

KEEP sits on the top line in cream brush capitals, with GOING below it in larger, heavier letters that stretch almost the full width of the poster. Under the last letter, a thin painted arrow points to the right. The paint is thick and mostly solid, with a few dry streaks inside the strokes, and the letters are tall and slightly uneven, like a hand painted road sign.

There is no period at the end, just the arrow, which is the point. One more page, one more mile, one more day. Because the letters are so large, it reads clearly from across a room. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a home gym, a home office, the wall above a desk, a hallway or a dorm room. It is a good match for runners, students, small business owners and anyone who needs a push on the slow days.
