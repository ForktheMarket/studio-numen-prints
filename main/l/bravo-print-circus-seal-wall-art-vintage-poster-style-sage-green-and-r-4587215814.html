Bravo is a vintage circus style poster of a seal balancing a striped ball on its nose, for anyone who likes to be reminded that small wins count.

A glossy blue-black seal sits up on a red drum with its nose in the air. On the tip of its nose is a red and cream striped ball. The seal sits inside a tall oval of soft sage green, and the rest of the background is warm, aged cream. The word BRAVO runs across the bottom in red capital letters with slab serifs. The seal is painted with pale highlights on its wet coat, like an old lithograph.

It is a happy, well-behaved picture. The seal looks pleased with itself and the word underneath agrees.
