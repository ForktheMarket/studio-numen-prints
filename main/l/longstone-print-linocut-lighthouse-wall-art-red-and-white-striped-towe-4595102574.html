The Longstone print is a linocut style lighthouse poster for a bathroom, a child's room or a nautical hallway.

A red and white striped lighthouse stands on a small rocky base in the middle of a calm sea, with the word LONGSTONE across the top in big red capitals. The tower has alternating red and white bands, tiny windows and a dark lamp room at the top. Below it the sea is drawn as rings, hundreds of thin navy lines circling out from the rock to the edges of the picture like ripples from a dropped stone. The sky is bare cream. Three colors, navy, red and cream, and the empty space makes the stripes jump out.

It suits a bathroom, a nursery, a hallway or a kitchen, above a toilet, above a cot or at the end of a landing. Red, navy and cream go with white paneling, pale wood and brass. It fits new parents, sailors and anyone from the Northumberland coast, and it sits well beside Portland Bill and Start Point from the same series.
