This You Look Good print is a retro bathroom poster of a Black woman in a head wrap shown from two angles, for anyone who wants a kind word on the wall before they leave the house.

The print is split down the middle. On the left she looks over her shoulder at the viewer with a small smile, on a pale cream panel. On the right the same woman is shown in profile with a gold hoop earring against a deep emerald green. Her wrap is a loose twist of orange, teal and gold, and the two halves are divided by a thin gold line. The words YOU LOOK GOOD run across the top in dark green capitals. It has the symmetry of a vintage fashion ad and the warmth of a note from a friend.

It suits a bathroom, a hallway by the front door or a dressing table, and the orange and green pair well with brass fittings and white tile.
