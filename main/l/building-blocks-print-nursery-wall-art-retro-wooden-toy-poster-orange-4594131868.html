This building blocks print is a bold orange and teal poster for a nursery, a playroom or a modern living room.

Three wooden cubes are stacked in a slightly wobbly tower on a bright orange-red background. The top and bottom blocks are deep teal and the middle one is pale cream wood. A fourth teal block has tipped over and leans against the base on one corner. Strong side light gives every cube a bright face and a dark face, and the brushed paint and worn edges show clearly.

Because it is all squares and flat color, it reads almost as an abstract picture until you notice what the shapes are. That lets it work in grown up rooms as well as children's ones. It suits a nursery, a playroom, a hallway or a home office. Orange, teal and cream go well with white walls, walnut furniture and navy or mustard fabrics.
