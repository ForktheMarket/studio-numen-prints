This Zigzag Mountains print is a flat desert landscape poster of a band of jagged mountains in browns and rust running across the middle of the picture, between a peach sky and a plain ochre ground, for anyone who wants a landscape pared down to three bands.

The mountains overlap in sawtooth layers of umber, rust, slate gray and sand, each ridge a flat shape with no detail inside it. Above them the sky is a soft gradient from peach to pale gold, and below them the desert floor is a single block of warm ochre. It is calm, warm and very simple.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a hallway or an office, and it pairs with the canyon river and mesa prints. It is also a good choice for a rented room, since it brings warm color without needing a painted wall behind it.
