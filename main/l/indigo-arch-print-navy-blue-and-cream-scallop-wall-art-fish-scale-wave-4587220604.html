This indigo arch print is a repeating scallop pattern in deep navy blue on cream, in the style of hand-dyed and stitched indigo cloth, for bedrooms, bathrooms and anyone who likes blue and white rooms.

Rows of nested arches overlap like fish scales or rolling waves, each row tucked behind the one below it. Every arch is a thick band of indigo with a fine line of cream stitches running along its middle. The bands are brushy and uneven, darker in some places and washed out in others, with rough edges where the blue stops. The background is a warm cream.

It is a bold pattern with a calm rhythm. Because it repeats evenly from edge to edge, it reads as texture from across the room and as detail up close. Navy and cream fit coastal, Japanese inspired, boho and plain white rooms and sit well next to wood, linen and rattan. It suits the wall above a bed, a bathroom, a hallway, a living room or a stair landing. It is a good match for people who like the sea, old textiles and blue and white china.
