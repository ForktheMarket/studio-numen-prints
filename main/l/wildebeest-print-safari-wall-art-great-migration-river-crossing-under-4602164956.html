This wildebeest print shows a herd crossing a river as one dark mass, low in the picture, beneath a sky that burns red and orange, made for living rooms, offices and anyone who loves wild drama.

The animals are painted as a dense line of dark shapes plunging through the shallows, dust and spray rising around them in a haze of gold. The water in the foreground is deep slate and black, catching a few streaks of reflected fire. Above, the sky moves from smoky purple gray at the top to a blaze of scarlet and amber at the horizon.

It is the most intense picture in the set, strong color and movement rather than calm. The palette is red, orange, charcoal and slate, so it suits a dark study, a living room with bold furniture, and anyone who has seen or dreams of seeing the great migration.
