This High Wire print is a minimalist vintage circus poster of a tiny tightrope walker, for anyone who likes quiet wall art with a bit of nerve.

A single thin black line runs uphill across the top half of the picture. On it stands a very small figure in a red leotard, holding a blue parasol above her head, one hand out for balance. Small blue stars are scattered loosely around her and down toward the bottom, where the words HIGH WIRE sit in large, plain blue capitals. Everything else is empty, warm cream.

Most of the picture is open space, and that is the point. She is a long way up and she looks fine about it. The colors are cream and a soft denim blue with one spot of red, so it stays calm on the wall and fits blue, white and neutral rooms easily. It suits a nursery, a kids room, a home office, a reading corner, a hallway or a gallery wall. It is a good match for anyone starting something new, such as a job, a business or a move, and for children who like the circus.
