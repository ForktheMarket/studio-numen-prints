This waterhole print shows a still pool at dusk reflecting a blazing gold sky, with dark trees and reeds along its banks, made for living rooms, bedrooms and anyone who loves a warm landscape.

The sun sits low behind a stand of trees on the right, turning the clouds amber and orange, and the whole sky repeats itself in the glassy water below. Reeds and grass in the foreground are painted in dark umber, and the far bank fades into a soft misty haze. The light is the subject here, more than any animal.

The palette is gold, orange and deep brown, rich and quiet at the same time. It suits a wall above a bed or sofa, a dining room with wood furniture, and anyone who remembers the color of the sky over open water at the end of a hot day.
