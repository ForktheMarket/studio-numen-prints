This sage green circle print is a minimalist abstract of one large dark green disc on a textured cream ground, made for living rooms, bedrooms and modern neutral homes.

The circle sits slightly off center and fills most of the width of the page. Its color shifts from deep olive at the top to a softer sage at the bottom, with a faint linen texture running through the paint. A pale glow around the edge of the circle makes it look like a sun seen through haze or a moon on a warm evening. Nothing else is on the page.

It is a simple, calm piece that works as a single statement print or as the anchor of a gallery wall in greens and neutrals. The warm cream and muted green pair well with oak, white walls, black metal and natural linen. Hang it above a sofa, over a console table, or in a hallway where one bold shape is enough.
