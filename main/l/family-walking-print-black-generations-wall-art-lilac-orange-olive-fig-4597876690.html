This Family Walking print is a poster of seven members of a Black family striding across the page, from a father carrying a baby to a grandmother with a cane, for anyone whose family moves together.

The figures are scattered loosely over a cream background with no ground line. A man in olive carries a baby on his shoulder, a woman in a wide lilac hat strides in a long lilac dress, a girl in orange runs with her arms pumping, two children race along the bottom row, and a silver haired grandmother in a green coat brings up the rear.

The colors are lilac, orange, olive, cream and warm brown, soft and cheerful. It suits a hallway, an entryway, a living room or a family kitchen, and it works well near the front door or above a bench. A print for big families, reunions and anyone with a grandmother who still sets the pace.
