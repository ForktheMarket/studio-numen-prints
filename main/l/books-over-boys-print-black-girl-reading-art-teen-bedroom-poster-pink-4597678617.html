This Books Over Boys print is a playful poster of a Black teenage girl with afro puffs and headphones lying on her bed reading, for any girl who would rather finish the chapter.

She lies on her front with her feet kicked up, nose in a bright pink book, headphones over her puffs, on white sheets against a lilac wall. A border of hand drawn bookshelves runs around the edge, scattered with small red hearts, and BOOKS OVER BOYS sits in bold navy capitals across the top.

The colors are lilac, pink, coral red and navy, painted with a loose brush so it feels like a page from a sketchbook. It suits a teen bedroom, a dorm room, a girls room or a bookish corner, and it reads clearly from across the room. A print for daughters, nieces and best friends who always have a book in their bag.
