This Tiger Face print is a close cropped painterly wildlife poster of a tiger looking straight at you, the face filling the whole page, for anyone who wants one strong image on the wall.

The crop cuts in tight so the amber eyes sit near the top third and the nose and whiskers run off the bottom edge. The orange fur, black stripes and white cheeks are painted in loose directional strokes, with the brightest light on the bridge of the nose and the fur around the eyes. The stare is direct and calm rather than aggressive.

It suits a living room, an office, a teenager's room or a hallway that needs a focal point, and it is best given a wall of its own. The colors are warm enough to sit with wood and terracotta and bold enough for a white room.
