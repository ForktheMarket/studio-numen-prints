This Nothing to Prove print is a black and cream quote poster for anyone who has stopped keeping score and wants a reminder of what matters.

The full line reads: Nothing to prove, just people to love and a life to enjoy. It is painted in thick cream brush capitals across seven lines on a solid black background, with a small cream flower after the final period. The letters are chunky and uneven, with visible brush strokes inside each one and lines that step slightly left and right, so it looks like a sign lettered by hand in one sitting.

It is a calm, grown up sentiment. It is not about hustle or goals, just the people around you and the days you have. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a bedroom, the wall above a bed, a living room, an entryway or a home office. It is a good match for retirees, new parents, anyone changing pace after a busy stretch, and friends who need to hear it.
