The marble bust print is a moody painted poster for a study, a library or anyone who likes classical sculpture.

A young man's head and shoulders fill the picture, turned to the right and lit from the upper left so the light picks out the curls, the brow and the nose. The hair is a mass of thick carved curls in cream and pale ochre, and the cloth draped over the shoulder is pinned with a round brooch. The face carries a faint warm flush, as if the stone were about to breathe. The background is flat black, so the figure looks lit by a single lamp in a dark room. The brushwork is thick in the hair and the cloth, smoother across the face.

It suits a study, a hallway, a living room or a bedroom, above a desk or beside a bookcase. The cream, ochre and black go with dark wood, leather, brass and deep green or burgundy walls. It fits readers and students of history, and it pairs with the leather books and the brass globe from the same series.
