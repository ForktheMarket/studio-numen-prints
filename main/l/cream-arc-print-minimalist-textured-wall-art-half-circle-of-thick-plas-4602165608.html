This cream arc print shows a large half circle of thick cream plaster pushing in from the right edge of the picture over a smooth warm beige ground, made for living rooms, bedrooms and modern neutral homes.

The curve is heavy and sculptural, built up in layered strokes so its rim casts a soft shadow and the surface shows every drag of the knife. The beige behind it is flat and quiet, with just a hint of gradient toward the corners. The shape is cut off by the edge, so it reads as a detail of something larger.

The palette is cream, oat and warm beige, with no strong color at all, which makes it one of the easiest prints to place. It suits a bedroom wall above the headboard, a living room above the sofa or a hallway, and anyone who likes abstract art that feels calm and handmade.
