This Soft Cloud Sky print is a painterly seascape poster where the sky takes up most of the picture, with a bank of cream and peach clouds rising over a thin strip of sea, for anyone who wants something gentle and airy.

The clouds are painted in soft rounded masses of white, apricot and blush, lit from low on the horizon, against a pale blue sky. The sea below is a band of calm teal with a gold reflection in the middle, and the horizon sits low so the whole thing feels open. It is the most pastel piece in the series.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a nursery or a bathroom, and it works wherever you want light color and a sense of space. It is also a sensible choice for a nursery because there is nothing in it to grow out of.
