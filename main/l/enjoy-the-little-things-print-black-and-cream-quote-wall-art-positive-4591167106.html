This Enjoy the Little Things print is a black and cream quote poster for people who know the best parts of a day are usually the small ones.

The four words are painted in tall cream brush capitals across three lines on a solid black background, and they fill the poster from edge to edge. Three round dots trail off after the last word. The paint looks thick, almost like frosting, with clear ridges where the bristles ran through it, and the letters tilt and vary in height. The top and bottom words are large and the middle line is smaller, which gives it an easy rhythm.

It is about the first coffee, a clean kitchen, a dog asleep in the sun, a text from an old friend. The trailing dots leave room for you to add your own. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a living room, a breakfast nook or an entryway. It is a good match for new homeowners, grandparents, hosts and anyone who likes a slow morning.
