This sage green landscape print shows rolling dunes seen from above as soft curved bands of green and cream, made for living rooms, bedrooms and modern neutral homes.

The bands sweep across the page from left to right, starting in pale mint and cream near the top and deepening through sage and olive to a dark forest green at the bottom. Two ribbons of white run between the darker layers like light catching the crest of each dune. The paint is soft and slightly hazy, so the shapes feel like a landscape and an abstract at the same time.

It is a calm piece with a lot of depth, and it works alone above a sofa or bed, or in a group with the hills and clouds prints from this series. The greens sit well with oak, linen, black metal and white walls.
