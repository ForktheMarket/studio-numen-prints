The ginger jar print is a blue and white chinoiserie poster for a dining room, a hallway or anyone who collects porcelain.

A single lidded ginger jar fills the page, drawn in cobalt and indigo on a soft white ground. The domed lid carries a band of leaves and blossoms, and the same foliage wraps the shoulder around a white cartouche. Below it the body holds a full garden scene: a pagoda with a curved roof and steps, a weeping willow bending over it, and a distant cottage on a hill. A key fret band runs around the foot. The washes move from pale blue sky to deep indigo shadow, with fine line work in the roof tiles and willow fronds, and a faint shadow under the jar makes it read as an object, not a flat pattern.

It suits a dining room, an entryway or a living room, above a sideboard or beside a mantel. It goes with white walls, dark wood, brass, rattan and navy or sage fabrics, and it fits anyone who loves grandmillennial rooms. It pairs with the blossom vase from the same series.
