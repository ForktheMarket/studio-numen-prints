This Baobab Tree print is a minimalist poster of a single giant baobab standing on open ground under a flat red sun, for anyone who has seen one at dusk and never quite got over it.

The tree fills the lower half of the page, its thick trunk and bare spreading crown drawn in dark silhouette with a hint of rust where the light catches the bark. The sky is a wide wash of peach, the sun a perfect red disc high above, and a thin line of distant trees runs along the horizon.

The colors are peach, red, rust and near black, warm and still. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or an office, and it sits well above a sofa or a bed. A print for travelers, tree lovers and anyone who wants a quiet piece with a strong shape.
