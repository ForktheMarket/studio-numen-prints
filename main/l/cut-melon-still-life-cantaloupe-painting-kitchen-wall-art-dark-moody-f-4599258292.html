This cut melon still life is a dark painting of half a cantaloupe and a silver knife on white linen, made for kitchens, dining rooms and lovers of old master style.

The melon is split open, showing bright orange flesh and a hollow of seeds, with a few seeds scattered on the cloth. An ornate silver knife lies beside it on crumpled white linen. The background is solid black. The white linen is painted in deep folds and creases that catch the light, which makes the bright orange melon stand out even more.

The bright orange against white and black makes it one of the most vivid pieces in the series. It suits a kitchen, a dining room, a breakfast nook, or a dark painted wall. It feels fresh and summery even against all that black, and works well in a bright white kitchen where it adds contrast and a bit of drama.
