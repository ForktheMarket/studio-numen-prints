This dark red roses still life is a moody painting of an overblown bouquet dropping its petals, made for bedrooms, romantic spaces and lovers of dark floral art.

A tall clear glass vase holds a big bunch of deep red roses, some still tight and some fully open. Fallen petals and loose blooms are scattered over a dark marble surface below. The background is pure black, so the red burns out of it. Some petals have curled and darkened at the edges, while others still look fresh, which gives the bouquet a lovely sense of time passing.

It is lush and a little dramatic, about beauty at its peak. Crimson and black suit dark painted walls and warm lighting. Hang it above a bed, in a living room, a dining room, or a dressing room. It looks best with warm, low lighting.
