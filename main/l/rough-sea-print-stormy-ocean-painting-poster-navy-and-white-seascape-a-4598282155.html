This Rough Sea print is a painterly seascape poster of heavy navy swells rolling across the whole picture under a bright, torn sky, for anyone who likes the sea when it is not being gentle.

The waves fill the bottom two thirds, painted in deep blue, slate and black with foam streaking their backs and breaking white on the crests. The sky is pale and luminous with soft gray cloud and a hint of warm light near the horizon, which makes the water look even darker. The painting has the feel of being on deck rather than on shore.

It suits a living room, an office or a hallway, and it sits well with navy, oak and brass. It works well on its own above a desk or a sideboard, and it holds up to a dark painted wall.
