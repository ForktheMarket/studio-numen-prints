This Italy map print is an antique style chart of the Italian peninsula, Sicily, Sardinia and the Adriatic coast, drawn in sepia on aged parchment, made for offices, kitchens, dining rooms and hallways.

The land is shaded with fine hatched mountains running down the spine of the country, and the sea is washed in a soft faded teal. Six small galleons are drawn at sea around the coast, one off Sardinia, others in the Tyrrhenian and Adriatic.
