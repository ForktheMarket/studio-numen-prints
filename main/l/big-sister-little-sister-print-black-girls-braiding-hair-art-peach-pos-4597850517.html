This Big Sister Little Sister print is a poster of two Black girls sitting on a rug while the older one braids the younger one's hair, for any home where two sisters share a room.

The big sister kneels in a rust dress with a full afro, eyes down on her work, a long braid already running through her hands. The little sister sits facing her in a peach dress with her puff tied high, patient and still. They sit on a teal and lilac rug against a cream background, with the title in two lines of mauve and rust lettering above.

The colors are peach, rust, mauve, teal and cream, warm and soft. It suits a shared bedroom, a nursery, a playroom or a hallway, and it sits well above a bed or a dresser. A tender print for sisters, cousins and anyone who remembers sitting still for hair day.
