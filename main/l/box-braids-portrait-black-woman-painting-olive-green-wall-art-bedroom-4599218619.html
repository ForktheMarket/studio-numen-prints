This box braids portrait is a painting of a young Black woman seen from behind and turned in profile, for a bedroom, a bathroom or a dressing area.

Long braids fall over one bare shoulder and down her back, with a small gold bead tucked into one of them. Warm sunlight lands on her shoulder and cheek and leaves the rest in shadow. The background is a flat olive green with loose brushwork, which makes the bronze skin tones glow.

It is a quiet, private image, more about light and texture than about pose. The green and bronze pair well with wood furniture, rattan and houseplants. It suits a wall above a bed or a vanity, or a spot near a window where it picks up daylight. A good match for the cream head wrap and natural hair portraits in the same batch.
