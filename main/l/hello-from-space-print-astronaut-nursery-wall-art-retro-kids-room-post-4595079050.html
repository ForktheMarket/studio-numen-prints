This hello from space print is a retro astronaut poster for a nursery, a kids bedroom or a playroom.

An astronaut in a cream spacesuit floats in the middle of a deep navy sky, one arm raised in a wave, the other hanging loose, with legs bent as if drifting. The visor is a warm orange gold and the suit is drawn with bold navy linework for the folds, straps and chest pack. Tiny cream stars are scattered thickest at the top. The word HELLO sits above the astronaut and FROM SPACE below, both in big rounded cream capitals, and a rough edged cream border runs around the whole picture like an old stamp. The style is flat and grainy, in just three colors.

It suits a nursery, a boys bedroom, a playroom or a bathroom with navy tiles, and it works above a crib or a bed. Navy, cream and orange go with white walls, natural wood, rust cushions and striped fabrics, and it pairs with the rocket and Saturn prints from the same series. It fits any child who waves at the moon.
