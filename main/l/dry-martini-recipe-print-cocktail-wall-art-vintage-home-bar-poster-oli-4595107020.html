The Dry Martini print is a vintage style cocktail poster for a home bar, a dining room or a kitchen.

The words Dry Martini run across the top in thin black serif capitals, widely spaced. Beneath them a tall coupe glass fills most of the print, the drink inside it a cool olive green with a single green olive resting at the bottom of the bowl. The glass is beaded with condensation and its long stem and foot catch the light. The recipe is lettered small in the lower left corner on three lines: Gin, Dry Vermouth, Olive. The background is a soft parchment cream, and the whole thing has the slightly faded, photographic look of an old drinks catalog.

It suits a home bar, a dining room, a kitchen or a study, hung beside the bar cart or above a drinks cabinet. Green, cream and black go well with walnut, marble, brass and dark painted walls. It fits anyone who takes their martini cold and dry, and it pairs with the Negroni and the French 75 from the same series.
