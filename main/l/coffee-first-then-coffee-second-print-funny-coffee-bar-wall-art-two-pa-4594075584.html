This Coffee First print is a funny two panel comic poster for coffee drinkers who should not be spoken to before the first cup, or the second.

In the top panel a small yellow egg-shaped character scowls straight at you, eyebrows down, holding one steaming blue mug. The caption reads COFFEE FIRST. In the bottom panel the scowl has not changed, but now there is a steaming mug in each hand, one blue and one green. The caption reads THEN COFFEE SECOND. The big pink cheeks make the bad mood more sweet than threatening. Thick black outlines and flat color sit in two cream panels on a warm tangerine orange background.

It says what a lot of people feel at seven in the morning and saves them saying it out loud. It suits a kitchen, a coffee bar or coffee station, a breakfast nook, an office kitchen or the wall above a desk. Orange and cream go well with white cabinets, wood counters and black appliances.
