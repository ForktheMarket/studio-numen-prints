This cocker spaniel print is a warm retro poster for spaniel owners and anyone who falls for long ears and soft eyes.

The spaniel sits inside a tall oval of burnt orange on a cream background, head tilted and looking up to the left. The coat is drawn in three tones, a bright orange for the lit fur, a deep brown black for the nose, the eyes and the shadows in the long wavy ears, and cream for the blaze down the face and the chest. The ears fall in loose curls either side of the head and break the edge of the oval at the bottom. The words COCKER SPANIEL sit below in heavy orange capitals. The mood is gentle and a little hopeful, a dog hoping for a walk.

It suits a living room, a hallway, a kitchen or a bedroom, hung above a sofa, a sideboard or a bedside table. Orange, brown and cream go with white walls, walnut, mustard and olive, and it fits anyone with a spaniel on the sofa. It pairs with the other breeds in the same series.
