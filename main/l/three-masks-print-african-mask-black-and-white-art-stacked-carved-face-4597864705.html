This Three Masks print is a black and white poster of three carved African masks stacked one above the other, for anyone who wants heritage on the wall with a clean modern edge.

The top mask is round with hollow eyes and a small square crest. The middle one is long and narrow with a pair of curved horns sweeping out to each side. The bottom one is broad with a heavy brow and a wide mouth. All three are drawn in dense black with a stippled texture so the carved surfaces catch the light, on a plain white background.

There is no color at all, which makes it an easy fit for a modern room. It suits a hallway, a living room, a study or an entryway, and it anchors a black and white gallery wall. A print for collectors, travelers and anyone with a shelf of carvings at home.
