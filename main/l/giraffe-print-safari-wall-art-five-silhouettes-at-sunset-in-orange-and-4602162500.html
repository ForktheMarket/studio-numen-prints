This giraffe print shows a line of five giraffes walking in silhouette across a glowing orange sunset, made for living rooms, nurseries and anyone who loves the African savanna.

The sky fills most of the picture in deep amber fading to pale gold at the horizon, with no clouds and no detail, just light. The giraffes stand along the lower third as tall dark shapes, each at a slightly different angle, their long necks making a slow rhythm across the sheet. Dry grass in the foreground is painted in loose dark strokes.

The palette is almost entirely orange, gold and black, warm and simple, so it brings heat and calm to a room at the same time. It suits a wall above a sofa, a child's room or a hallway, and anyone who has been on safari or dreams about it.
