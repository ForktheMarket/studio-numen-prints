The Gin and Tonic print is a retro cocktail poster for a kitchen, a home bar or a garden room.

The drink name is stacked in the top right in dark green block capitals, Gin And Tonic on three lines, and the recipe runs down the right side beneath it in bright lime green: Gin, Tonic, Lime. On the left a tall highball glass stands full of a pale, cloudy drink, fizzing with bubbles that rise out of the top and scatter across the cream background. A fat wedge of lime is pushed into the rim. The glass is drawn in deep green and pale mint and throws a long, hard green shadow out to the left, with a grainy halftone texture that makes it look screen printed.

It suits a kitchen, a home bar, a dining room or a conservatory, hung beside the bar cart or above a kitchen counter. Green, lime and cream go well with white walls, pale wood, rattan and terracotta. It fits gin drinkers and summer hosts, and it pairs with the Mojito and the Margarita from the same series.
