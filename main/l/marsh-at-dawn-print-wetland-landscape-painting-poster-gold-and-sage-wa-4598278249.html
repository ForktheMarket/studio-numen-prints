This Marsh at Dawn print is a painterly landscape poster of a flat wetland at sunrise, laid out in horizontal bands of oat, pale green, water and gold, for anyone who likes low, wide, quiet country.

The sky is a wash of peach and pale blue with a few streaks of cloud. Below it, strips of marsh grass and shallow water alternate toward the viewer, and the rising sun drops a broad gold reflection straight down the middle of the picture. The grass in the foreground is painted in loose vertical strokes of straw and olive.

It suits a living room, a dining room or a lake house, and the warm gold makes it an easy match for wood and linen. The horizontal bands make it a natural fit above a long piece of furniture such as a sideboard or a console table.
