This blast off print is a retro rocket poster for a kids bedroom, a playroom or a nursery.

The words BLAST OFF sit across the top in tall cream capitals on a deep navy background. Below them, a small red rocket with a round teal porthole and two swept back fins climbs straight up the middle of the print. A long plume of exhaust trails from its base, cream in the center and fading to a speckled teal spray at the edges, filling the lower half of the picture. The whole thing has a grainy screen printed texture, with just four colors doing all the work, and it reads as cheerful, fast and simple.

It suits a boys bedroom, a playroom, a nursery or a homework corner, and it is a good one for above a bed or a toy chest. Red, navy and teal go with white walls, pale wood, striped bedding and primary colored toys, and it pairs with the Saturn and astronaut prints from the same series. It fits children who build rockets out of cardboard boxes.
