This Lagos print is a vintage style travel poster of the old Lagos harbor, with tall palms, a yellow steamship and a cluster of painted fishing boats on blue water, for anyone with Nigeria in their heart or their passport.

Three palms lean across the picture in the foreground, the city a pale line of buildings on the far shore. The steamer sits mid harbor with a red and black funnel, and a flotilla of small boats with patterned hulls crowds the water in front. A blue triangle border runs around the edge and LAGOS sits below in wide dark blue capitals.

The colors are blue, cream, yellow, green and rust, with the faded look of an old steamer company poster. It suits a living room, a hallway, an office or a study, and it sits well above a sofa or a desk. A print for Lagosians, travelers and anyone with family back home.
