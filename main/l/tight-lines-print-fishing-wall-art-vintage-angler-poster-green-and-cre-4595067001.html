This tight lines print is a vintage angling poster for a lake house, a cabin or anyone who fishes.

The words TIGHT LINES run across the top in dark navy capitals, and most of the print is open cream sky. In the bottom third a lone angler stands in silhouette on a grassy bank, rod raised, and the rod bends in one long arc across the sky with the line dropping to the water on the far right. The bank is cut in flat bands of navy and olive green, the hills behind are two tones of sage and olive, and the water is pale gray green with cream streaks of light. It is a flat screen print style with four colors and no outlines, calm and simple.

Tight lines is what anglers say for good luck. It suits a lake house, a hallway, an office or a cabin bedroom, above a desk or a bed. Cream, navy and olive go well with pale wood, linen and green walls. It fits anyone who fishes, and it pairs with the gone fishing boat from the same series.
