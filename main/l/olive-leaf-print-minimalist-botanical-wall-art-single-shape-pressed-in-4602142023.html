This olive leaf print shows one large leaf in muted olive green pressed into a pale plaster surface, its veins and stem left behind like a fossil, made for living rooms, bedrooms and calm neutral homes.

The leaf sits slightly left of center with its tip pointing up, painted in soft mossy green that darkens toward the stem and fades at the edges. The plaster around it is cream and warm sand, clouded with faint stains and a few fine scratches. A thin stem trails down toward the bottom edge.

The palette is olive, cream and sand, gentle and earthy, so it suits Japandi, boho and Scandinavian rooms, especially those with sage or green accents. It works above a bed, a sofa or a console, and belongs to anyone who likes botanical art without bright color. It pairs well with the dandelion print from the same set as a matched botanical pair.
