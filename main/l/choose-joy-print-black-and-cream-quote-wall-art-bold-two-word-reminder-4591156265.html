This Choose Joy print is a bold black and cream quote poster for anyone who wants a two word reminder they can read from across the room.

The word CHOOSE runs across the top in cream brush capitals, and JOY sits below it at more than twice the height, filling the lower two thirds of the poster. A small painted sun with petal shaped rays sits beside the Y. The paint is dry brushed, so the black background shows through the edges of each letter in a rough, woven pattern, a bit like an old stenciled crate or a hand painted fairground sign.

It is the loudest print in the set and also the simplest. Joy is treated as something you pick, on an ordinary Tuesday as much as on a good day. Because the letters are so large, it reads clearly from a distance and holds its own on a big wall. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits an entryway, a living room, a kitchen, a stairway or a playroom. It is a good match for optimists, busy families and friends who light up a room.
