This wooden duck print is a bright nursery poster for a baby's room, a playroom or anyone with a soft spot for old pull along toys.

A small duck in pale, grainy wood sits on four chunky wheels in the middle of a solid red-orange background. It has a navy blue wing, a rounded orange beak and one black dot for an eye, and it looks off to the left as if waiting to be taken for a walk. A navy string runs from its front axle and winds down the picture in one long, lazy S curve until it leaves the bottom edge. The colors are flat and bold, with a fine speckled grain over the top like a page from a mid century children's book.

It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or a hallway that needs one strong block of color. The red, navy and warm wood tones go well with white walls, natural wood furniture, navy bedding and mustard or cream accents.
