This dandelion print shows a single dried seed head on a long thin stem, drawn in one umber stroke against a soft mottled plaster ground, made for living rooms, bedrooms and calm neutral homes.

The seed head sits high in the picture, each fine filament radiating from a dark center, and the stem drops straight down almost to the bottom edge. The background is a wash of cream, pale gray and warm sepia, stained and clouded like old limewash, with darker patches in the corners. The plant looks printed into the surface rather than painted on it.

The palette is sepia, bone and soft gray, gentle and faded. It suits a bedroom, a reading corner or a bathroom wall, pairs well with linen and pale wood, and belongs to anyone who likes dried flowers and worn surfaces.
