This Elephant print is a painterly safari poster of a bull elephant walking straight toward you through a cloud of warm dust, for anyone who wants a wall piece with real presence.

The elephant is lit from behind and to the side, so the ears and the top of the head catch a rim of light while the trunk and legs stay in shadow. Dust kicked up by its feet fills the lower half of the picture in beige and amber, and the sky above is a plain warm brown. Everything is in one family of earth tones, from sand to dark chocolate.

It suits a living room, a hallway or an office with wood, leather and other natural materials, and it reads well from across a room. Hang it on its own or with the cheetah and tiger from the same series.
