This Minimalist Woman print is a modern portrait poster of a Black woman with close cropped hair, her face painted in deep brown and her shoulders drawn in a single black line, for anyone who wants a figure on the wall with almost nothing else.

Her head is tilted slightly, eyes lowered, lips full, the whole face held in one shape of dark paint with her features suggested by shadow. Below the chin the body drops away into a thin continuous line that sweeps out to the edges as shoulders. The background is plain white.

The colors are brown, black and white, nothing more, so it fits any scheme. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a home office, and it holds its own above a sofa or in a gallery wall of other faces. A quiet, confident print for people who like their walls edited.
