This Mother and Child print is an abstract poster of a parent and a small child drawn as two stacked circles and curved planes, for anyone who wants a warm family piece without a literal picture of anyone.

The larger figure is a big mustard circle up top with a rust and orange head beside it, and the child is the smaller red circle below with a bright red center like a heart. A tall cream column runs between them like an arm holding the two together, and the shapes overlap in olive, brown and black so the two bodies read as one. The background is divided into quiet cream and olive panels.

The colors are rust, mustard, olive, red, cream and black, rich and warm. It suits a nursery, a bedroom, a living room or a hallway, and it looks right above a crib, a sofa or a changing table. A print for new parents, for a family wall, and for anyone who likes mid century shapes with some feeling behind them.
