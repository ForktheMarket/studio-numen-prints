This Sunday hat print shows a Black woman in an enormous patchwork church hat, made for bedrooms, dressing rooms and anyone who knows the joy of a Sunday best outfit.

The hat takes up most of the picture, built from wedges of pink, magenta, blue, orange and black patterns, with a wide brim tilted low over her eyes. Only her lips, chin and a big gold earring show beneath it. Her blouse is a bold floral in orange and pink on a lavender background. The way the hat hides her eyes adds a little mystery and a lot of attitude, and the big round earring finishes the outfit.

It is playful, proud and full of color. The lavender and pink suit bedrooms and dressing areas. Hang it above a vanity or dresser, in a hallway, or with other bright pieces on a family wall.
