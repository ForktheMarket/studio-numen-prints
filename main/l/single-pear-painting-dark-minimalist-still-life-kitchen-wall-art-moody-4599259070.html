This single pear painting is a dark minimalist still life of one large golden pear on a wooden table, made for kitchens, dining rooms and minimal modern spaces.

The pear fills most of the picture, its skin speckled in gold, orange and russet, with a long curved stem. A soft highlight shines on its side. It sits on a dark wooden table edge against a plain black background. The skin is covered in small freckles and soft bruises, and the light sits on its shoulder in a single warm highlight that makes it look ripe.

It is bold in its simplicity, one object and a lot of space. Warm orange against black makes it easy to see across a room. Hang it in a kitchen, a dining room, a hallway, or a modern living room. It looks great on a narrow wall.
