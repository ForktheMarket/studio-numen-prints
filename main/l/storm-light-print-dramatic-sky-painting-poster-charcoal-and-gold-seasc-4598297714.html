This Storm Light print is a painterly seascape poster of heavy charcoal clouds rolling over a calm sea, with late sun breaking through in gold, for anyone who likes weather on the wall.

The clouds take up the top two thirds of the picture in soft layered washes of gray, with a bright gap where the sun gets through and lights their edges. Below, the sea is flat and dark teal, and the sun lays one long gold reflection across it toward the viewer. The light is the subject here, the storm is just what it shines through.

It suits a living room, a study or an office, especially one with dark walls or wood, and it pairs well with the calmer pieces in the series. It is also one of the few pieces in the series that suits a room with little natural light, since the picture brings its own.
