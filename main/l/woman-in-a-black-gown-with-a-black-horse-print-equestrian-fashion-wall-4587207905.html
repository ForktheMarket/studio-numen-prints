This Woman in a Black Gown with a Black Horse print is an equestrian fashion portrait for riders and anyone who likes simple, high-contrast wall art.

A woman stands in a long black gown with a short cape over the shoulders and a train that trails behind her. She is almost a silhouette. A tall black hat with a very wide brim hides her eyes, and the only color on her is a pair of red lips. To her right a black horse steps in from the edge of the picture, bridled, with its head lowered toward her. Both stand on a black and cream checkerboard floor against a plain, warm cream wall.

The picture is nearly two colors, black and cream, which makes it one of the easiest in the series to place. It works with neutral rooms, black and white gallery walls, pale wood and linen. The mood is calm and a little mysterious. It suits a bedroom, the wall above the bed, a living room, an entryway or a hallway. It is a good match for riders, horse owners and anyone who prefers neutral colors.
