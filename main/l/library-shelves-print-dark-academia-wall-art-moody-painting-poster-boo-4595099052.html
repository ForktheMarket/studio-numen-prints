The library shelves print is a moody painted poster for a study, a library wall or a book lover.

The view looks straight down a narrow aisle between two tall walls of bookshelves that climb into darkness, with more shelves closing off the far end. A wooden rolling ladder stands in the center against the back wall, its rungs lit warm orange, and a red patterned runner leads along the floor toward it. Loose piles of books sit at the foot of each wall. The spines are dabs of ochre, cream, rust, dark green and blue, painted loosely so they read as a crowd. The light is low and warm, catching the ladder and the rug while the upper shelves fall into black, and the paint has a thick, broken texture.

It suits a study, a home library, a hallway or a classroom, above a desk or an armchair. The reds, ochres and deep browns go with dark wood, leather, brass and green walls. It fits readers and teachers, and it pairs with the reading table and the leather books from the same series.
