This Wolf print is a painterly wildlife poster of a lone gray wolf standing small in a vast field of pale snow, facing the viewer with its ears up and its eyes steady, for anyone who wants an animal print that is mostly quiet white space.

The wolf is painted in soft layers of gray, black and cream, its thick winter coat ruffled around the neck and its legs sinking into the snow. The face is detailed, with a black nose and pale amber eyes, while everything around it dissolves into a soft white haze. The snow is brushed in loose pale strokes with a faint blue shadow near the horizon.

The colors are gray, charcoal, cream and cool white, calm and wintry. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a home office or a cabin, and it works especially well in minimalist, Scandinavian and black and white rooms. A good piece for anyone who likes wolves, winter or a lot of breathing room on the wall.
