This overhead train print shows a dark railway bridge cutting across a canyon of tall buildings, with a lone figure under an umbrella walking the wet street below, made for living rooms, offices and apartments.

The buildings rise on both sides in gray and brown stone, their windows lit in rows of warm amber, and fade into pale rain mist at the top of the picture. The train track crosses as one hard horizontal stroke, breaking the vertical lines. Below it the street shines with reflected lamps and shop lights in orange and gold.

The feeling is cinematic and a little lonely, a single person in a very big city. The palette is charcoal, taupe and warm orange, so it suits dark interiors, home offices and gallery walls, and anyone who loves old downtown streets. It looks especially good on a dark painted wall where the lit windows can glow.
