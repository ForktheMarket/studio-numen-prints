The Margarita print is a retro cocktail poster for a kitchen, a home bar or a dining room.

The drink name runs across the top in dark green block capitals. Beneath it a bright orange panel holds a short tumbler of pale yellow green margarita with a thick salt rim and a wheel of lime hooked over the edge. Five whole limes sit around the base of the glass, two on the left and three on the right, drawn in deep green with a speckled texture. The recipe is lettered below the panel on three lines, each in its own color: Tequila in orange, Lime in green, Orange Liqueur in orange. The background is cream and the whole thing looks screen printed.

It suits a kitchen, a home bar, a dining room or a patio room, hung beside the bar cart or above a kitchen counter. Orange, lime and cream go well with white walls, terracotta, pale wood and cane. It fits tequila drinkers and anyone who hosts taco nights, and it pairs with the Mojito and the Gin and Tonic from the same series.
