This Plant the Damn Thing print is a funny garden quote poster for gardeners, houseplant collectors and anyone with a seedling still sitting in its pot.

Four words are painted in dark forest green capitals, one to a line, on a cream panel with a wavy, scalloped edge. Around the panel the whole background is a loose green gingham check, with slightly uneven lines like a picnic cloth. The letters are thick and streaky, and you can see each pass of the brush.

It is a nudge to stop overthinking and get on with it, in the garden or anywhere else. Green and cream are easy colors to live with, and the gingham gives it a country kitchen feel that goes well with wood, terracotta pots and real plants. It suits a kitchen, a sunroom, a porch, a potting shed, a mudroom or the wall above a plant shelf. It also works for anyone putting off a decision that has nothing to do with plants.
