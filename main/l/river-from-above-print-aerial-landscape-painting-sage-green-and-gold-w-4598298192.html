This River From Above print is a painterly aerial landscape poster of a pale river winding between dark green banks toward the sea, for anyone who wants green on the wall without a forest.

Seen from high up, the river is a soft S of milky aqua and cream, and the land on either side is painted in deep sage, moss and teal with flecks of gold where the light catches sandbanks and ridges. The far end fades into haze at the top of the picture. It has the loose, layered feel of a painting rather than a map.

It suits a hallway, a living room or a home office, and it is a natural partner to the aerial lagoon and tide pool pieces in the series. The greens are muted enough to sit with navy, charcoal or warm white without looking like a plant print.
