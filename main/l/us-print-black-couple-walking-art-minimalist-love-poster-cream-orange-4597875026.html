This Us print is a minimalist poster of a Black couple walking hand in hand across a wide empty field, for anyone who likes a love story told in one word.

The two figures are tiny at the bottom of the picture, him in a gold shirt and brown trousers, her in cream and tan, shadows stretching behind them on a band of burnt orange ground. Above them is nothing but a tall wash of warm cream, with US in small heavy black letters near the top.

Almost the whole print is open space, which makes it restful and easy to hang anywhere. The colors are cream, burnt orange, gold and brown, warm and plain. It suits a bedroom, a hallway, a living room or a landing, and it sits well above a bed or beside a mirror. A quiet print for couples who do not need to shout about it.
