This Wooden Mask print is a minimalist poster of a single tall carved African mask, long and narrow with a ridged crest, for anyone who wants one strong object on the wall.

The mask is painted in warm wood brown with the right half falling into deep shadow, the brow a long arch, the eyes two narrow slits, the nose a straight ridge down to a small set mouth. The crest at the top is cut with fine vertical lines like a comb. The background is a plain cream.

The colors are wood brown, black and cream, simple and warm. It suits a hallway, a living room, a study or an entryway, and it pairs well with the Three Masks print from the same set. A print for collectors, travelers and anyone who likes a quiet piece with real presence.
