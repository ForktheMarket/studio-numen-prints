This Woman in a Hat print is a cubist portrait poster of a face under a wide, flat olive hat, painted in rough strokes of ochre, black and cream, for anyone who wants a portrait with attitude and a worn, hand painted look.

The hat is a single green half circle that fills the top of the page. Below it the face is split into an ochre side and a pale side, with two long black lined eyes set at different heights, a straight nose, a small red mouth and a red dot earring. Black bars of hair frame the face and the background is a scraped cream with patches of tan and dark olive, so the surface looks scuffed and aged.

The colors are olive, ochre, black, cream and a touch of red, muted and earthy. It suits a bedroom, a dressing room, a hallway or a living room, and it sits well above a bed, a dresser or a vanity. A print for anyone who likes loose brushwork, moody neutrals and faces with real character.
