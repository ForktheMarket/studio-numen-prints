This sage green leaf print is a dense botanical pattern of overlapping leaves in sage and olive filling the whole page, made for bathrooms, bedrooms and hallways.

The leaves are long and pointed, painted in layers so that pale sage sits over deep forest green and darker shapes show through from behind. Small gaps of warm cream appear between them, which keeps the pattern from feeling heavy. The brushwork is soft and slightly uneven, like painted wallpaper, and the greens shift from light to dark across the page.

It is the busiest print in the series and works best where it can be the main piece on a wall, in a bathroom with white tile, a bedroom with linen and wood, or a hallway in a cottage style home. It also balances the simpler single stem prints when hung together.
