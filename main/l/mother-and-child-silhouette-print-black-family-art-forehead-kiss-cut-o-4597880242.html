This Mother and Child Silhouette print is a minimalist poster of a Black mother and her child standing forehead to forehead, both cut as solid black shapes on cream, for anyone who wants that bond on the wall without a single detail getting in the way.

The child stands on the left with a short natural cut, head tipped up. The mother leans down from the right with her hair gathered high, her hands at the child's back. Where the two faces meet, a thin sliver of light shows between the noses.

There is no color at all, which keeps it calm and easy to live with. It suits a nursery, a bedroom, a hallway or a living room, and it sits well above a crib or in a black and white gallery wall. A print for mothers, sons, daughters and anyone who still leans in like that.
