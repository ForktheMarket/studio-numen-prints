This Stay Curious print is a black and cream quote poster for people who ask a lot of questions, and for the kids, students and coworkers they hope will do the same.

The two words are painted in tall cream brush capitals on a soft black background, stacked one above the other in the middle of the poster. A period follows the last letter, and a small hand painted cream star sits just beside it. The strokes are rounded and a little uneven, with dry streaks where the brush ran low on paint. There is plenty of open black around the words, so it feels calm and uncluttered.

It is a short instruction that works at any age. Keep reading, keep poking at things, keep asking why. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a classroom, a library corner, a kids room or a playroom. It is a good match for teachers, scientists, writers, students and anyone who never grew out of asking questions.
