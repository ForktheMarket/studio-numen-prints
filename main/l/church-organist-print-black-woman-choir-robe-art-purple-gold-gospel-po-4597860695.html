This Church Organist print is a painterly poster of a Black woman in a purple choir robe seated at the organ, hands on the keys, for anyone who grew up with Sunday morning sounding exactly like this.

She is seen from behind and to the side, hair cropped close, a gold drop earring catching the light, the robe falling in heavy folds of violet and lilac. The organ keys and stops are blocked in with quick strokes of black and white, and the whole scene sits in a warm wash of golden yellow.

The colors are gold, violet, lilac, wood brown and deep brown, warm and glowing. It suits a music room, a living room, a hallway or a church office, and it pairs well with the Rejoice print from the shop. A print for organists, choir members and anyone who still hums the hymns.
