This Morning Fog Sea print is a painterly seascape poster of a flat calm sea under thin early fog, everything pale blue and silver, for anyone who wants the quietest possible coastal picture.

The sky is a soft blend of white and powder blue with the sun somewhere behind the haze, and its light falls in a bright column onto the water. The sea is a little darker, rippled, and reflects the sky in broken strokes of blue and white. There is no land, no boat and no bird. The whole picture is one gentle tone.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a bathroom or a nursery, and it is a good choice where a stronger picture would be too much. It also makes a soft pair with the pink dawn sea print, which shares the same calm water in a warmer tone.
