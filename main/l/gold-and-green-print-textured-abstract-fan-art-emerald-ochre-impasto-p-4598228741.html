This Gold and Green print is a textured abstract poster of thick gold and ochre paint fanning out from the bottom corner across a deep emerald ground, for anyone who wants a rich, warm piece that catches the light.

The gold starts as a dense knot in the lower left and spreads upward in long combed ridges, each one standing proud of the surface, with a torn ragged edge where the knife lifted off. The green behind it is dark and glossy with its own brush marks, and a few stray flecks of gold are scattered across it. The two colors meet in a hard, bright edge.

The colors are antique gold, ochre, honey, emerald and forest green, warm and a little glamorous. It suits a living room, a dining room, a hallway or a bedroom with dark walls, and it looks especially good with brass, velvet and dark wood. A natural pick for a green room that needs one warm accent.
