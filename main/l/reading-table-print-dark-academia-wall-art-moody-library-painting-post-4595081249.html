The reading table print is a moody painted poster for a library, a study or anyone who misses long evenings in a reading room.

A long polished wooden table runs down the center of a tall library hall, lined with wooden chairs and set with rows of brass lamps under green glass shades. Books lie open along its length. Above, a line of green pendant lamps hangs from a coffered ceiling and recedes into the dark, each one a glowing ring of warm light. Bookshelves climb both walls, and on the left a small painting and a stone bust sit over a mantel. The palette is dark green, deep brown, black and lamp gold, and the brushwork is loose, with the shelves painted as flecks of color.

It suits a study, a library, a dining room or a hallway, above a desk or a sideboard. The greens and browns go with dark wood, leather, brass and forest green walls. It fits readers, students and academics, and it pairs with the library shelves and the rainy window from the same series.
