This Woman in a Cream Suit with Two Whippets print is a fashion portrait for dog people who like their wall art bold and a little deadpan.

A woman sits on the floor in a loose, roomy cream trouser suit, one knee raised and her head tipped back. A cream bucket hat covers her eyes, leaving only red lips and long black hair. Two slim gray dogs sit bolt upright on either side of her like bookends, both staring straight out. They have the lean build and folded ears of a whippet or a small greyhound. The wall behind is a flat mustard yellow and the floor is large black and cream checks, all painted in thick, visible brushstrokes.

The symmetry is what makes it funny. The dogs look more serious than she does. Mustard, cream, gray and black are warm colors that sit well with wood floors, tan leather and white walls. It suits a living room, an entryway, a home office or a hallway. It is a good match for whippet, greyhound and Italian greyhound owners, and for anyone who wants a strong block of yellow in a room.
