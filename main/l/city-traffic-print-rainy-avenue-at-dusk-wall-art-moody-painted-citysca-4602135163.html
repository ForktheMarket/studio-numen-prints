This city traffic print looks down on a long wet avenue at dusk, a river of headlights and tail lights running away toward a misty skyline, made for living rooms, offices and apartments.

The street is painted from above, the cars reduced to glowing dabs of red, orange and white that smear into the wet road. On either side the buildings sit in cool slate gray and blue, with a few lit windows, and the tall towers at the far end fade into evening haze. The brushwork is loose and energetic, so the picture feels like movement rather than a still photo.

The palette is blue gray with a strong warm streak of orange down the middle, which makes it a striking piece above a sofa or behind a desk. It suits anyone who loves the energy of a big city at rush hour.
