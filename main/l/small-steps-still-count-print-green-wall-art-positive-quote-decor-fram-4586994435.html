This Small Steps Still Count print is a positive quote poster for anyone working toward something slowly, from a new habit to a long course of study.

Four words are stacked in thick, rounded capitals in deep forest green. They sit on a soft arch shape painted in a sandy beige, one shade darker than the cream background around it. The paint looks heavy and a little dragged, so you can see the brush lines inside each letter, and the edges of the arch are left rough.

It is a quiet, steady print. Green and cream are easy colors to place, and they look good next to plants, linen and light wood. It suits a bedroom, a home office, the wall above a desk, a nursery or a kids room where someone is learning to read, tie laces or ride a bike. It also fits a therapy room, a classroom or a home gym.
