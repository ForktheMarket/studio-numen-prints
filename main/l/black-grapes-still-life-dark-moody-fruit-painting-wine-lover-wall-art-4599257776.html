This black grapes still life is a dark painting of a heavy bunch of grapes hanging from the vine, made for kitchens, dining rooms and wine lovers.

The grapes are deep blue black, each one round and glossy with a small highlight. They hang from a twisting green stem with a large vine leaf behind. The background is close to black, so the fruit seems to float out of the dark. A thin bloom sits on the skins of the grapes, and the vine leaf behind them is a dull olive green that adds a little warmth to the dark.

It is rich and simple, with a cool blue cast that sets it apart from warmer still lifes. It suits a dining room, a kitchen, a home bar or a wine cellar. A natural partner to the plums and figs in the same series.
