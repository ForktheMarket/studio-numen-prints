The fountain pen print is a moody still life poster for a home office, a writing desk or a writer.

A black fountain pen with a gold band lies diagonally across a handwritten letter, its nib pointing down to the left. Three cream sheets overlap on a dark table, covered in loose cursive painted as script rather than readable words, with a round seal of dark red wax on the top sheet. Behind the pen a glass tumbler holds black ink, and round wire spectacles rest at the top right. The light comes from the upper right, throwing a long shadow from the pen and glinting off the glass and the rims. The palette is cream, warm brown, black and one dot of red, with thick brushwork in the sheets and the table.

It suits a home office, a study, a bedroom or a hallway, above a desk or beside a bookcase. The cream and dark brown go with walnut, black leather, brass and green walls. It fits writers and desk workers, and it pairs with the leather books and the red apple from the same series.
