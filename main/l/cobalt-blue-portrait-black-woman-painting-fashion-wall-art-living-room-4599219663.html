This cobalt blue portrait is a painting of a confident Black woman in a huge silk gown, made for living rooms, offices and anyone who likes fashion portraiture.

She sits facing us with close cropped hair, gold drop earrings and a steady, level gaze. The gown has a high ruffled collar and enormous gathered sleeves, painted in broad slabs of navy and cobalt that catch the light like real fabric. The background is plain cream, so the blue takes over the whole picture.

It has the poise of a magazine cover shot but the texture of an oil painting. The deep blue and warm skin tones work in navy, cream or wood interiors. Hang it above a sofa or a desk, or in a dressing room. It also holds its own as the single piece on a large blank wall, where the blue can carry.
