This Sand Dunes print is a painterly desert poster of soft dunes rolling across the lower half of the picture under a wide pale blue sky, for anyone who wants a neutral landscape with some light in it.

The dunes are painted in sand, ivory and warm tan, with their crests lit bright and their hollows in soft shadow, and flecks of gold where the wind has ridged the surface. Above them the sky is a clear blue with a few thin clouds near the horizon. There is nothing else, no plants and no people, just shape and light.

It suits a living room, a bedroom or a boho style space, and it works with most neutral palettes because the blue is so soft. It also pairs well with the golden hill print, which shares the same sand tones and soft sky.
