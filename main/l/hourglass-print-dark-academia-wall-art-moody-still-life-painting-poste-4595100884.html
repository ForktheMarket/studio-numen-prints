The hourglass print is a moody still life poster for a study, a hallway or anyone drawn to old vanitas paintings.

A wooden hourglass stands at the left, its two glass bulbs held between four turned posts, the pale sand half run through. Behind it, slightly out of focus, a round brass clock with a cream face and dark Roman numerals reads close to ten o'clock. In front lies a large dried rose, its petals dusty pink and rust, its gray green leaves trailing behind the hourglass. A brass key rests at the front. The background is black and the table a rough dark brown, lit from the upper left so the glass and the key catch sharp highlights. The paint is thick on the petals and the table, smooth on the glass.

It suits a study, a hallway, a bedroom or a living room, above a desk or a console. The pink, brass and dark brown go with walnut, velvet and black or deep green walls. It fits readers and collectors, and it pairs with the violin and the brass globe from the same series.
