This Pink Face print is an abstract portrait poster of a Black woman's face cropped so close that only the brow, nose, lips and a bare shoulder fit on the page, for anyone who wants a bold pink piece with real presence.

Her skin is painted in two shades of hot pink and magenta, with her closed eye, nose and lips drawn in a fine black line and shaded in deep brown. A block of brown shoulder fills the lower right, and a slice of cream shows at the top corner.

The colors are magenta, hot pink, chocolate brown and cream, and they do not hold back. It suits a bedroom, a living room, a dressing room or a salon, and it sits well above a bed or a vanity. A print for anyone whose walls can take a strong color and whose style can too.
