This Today Was Sh*t Tomorrow Might Not Be print is a funny, honest quote poster for anyone who has had a bad day and does not want to be told to look on the bright side.

The words are painted in rough black brush capitals on a cream background, inside one loose black line that runs around the edge and does not quite join up. The swear word is written out in full on the print and sits on a line of its own, bigger than the rest. The strokes are uneven and a little dry, which suits the mood of the line.

It is dry humor with some real comfort underneath, since the second half is as hopeful as the first half is blunt. Black on cream goes with almost any wall color and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a bedroom, a bathroom, a home office, a dorm room or a first apartment.
