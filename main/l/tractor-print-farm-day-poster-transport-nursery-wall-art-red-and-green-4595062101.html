This tractor print is a flat retro farm poster for a nursery, a playroom or a child who waves at every tractor that passes.

A bright red tractor stands in a field between two round hay bales, facing left, with its tall exhaust pipe, steering wheel and big ridged rear tire picked out in red and olive green. The bales are drawn in loose cream and khaki strokes, one close in the foreground on the right and one behind the tractor, and each throws a short dark green shadow across the grass. The sky is a pale washed blue above a band of olive field. The picture sits inside a cream border, and the words FARM DAY run underneath in black slab capitals with a slightly rough printed edge. The colors are kept to red, olive, cream and pale blue, the feel of an old countryside poster.

It suits a nursery, a toddler bedroom, a playroom or a farmhouse kitchen. Red and olive go well with white walls, gingham, oak and natural linen. It fits farming families and small children who know every vehicle by name.
