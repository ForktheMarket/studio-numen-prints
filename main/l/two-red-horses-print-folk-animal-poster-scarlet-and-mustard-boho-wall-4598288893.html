This Two Red Horses print is a folk art poster of a mirrored pair of scarlet horses standing nose to nose on a mustard yellow ground, for anyone who wants bold color and a bit of charm.

The horses are painted as flat shapes with long necks, pricked ears and simple round haunches, their outlines a little rough where the brush has dragged. They are exact mirrors of each other, which gives the picture a formal, almost embroidered feel. The red against the yellow is strong and warm.

It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or a child's room, and it pairs with the rooster and coyote prints for a set of folk animals. They also look right above a cot or a small bed, since the shapes are simple enough for a child to enjoy and the colors are cheerful.
