This French bulldog print is a bold retro poster for Frenchie owners and anyone who wants a dog with attitude on the wall.

The dog is seen head on, filling the lower two thirds of a warm cream background, ears up and eyes fixed on you. The portrait is cut into three flat tones, near black for the ears, the eye patches and the jowls, a hot red orange for the chest, the inner ears and the shading, and cream for the white blaze down the face and the muzzle. The words FRENCH BULLDOG sit above the head in two lines of heavy red capitals. A light grain across the color gives it the look of an old printed poster rather than a photo.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a home office or a bar area, hung above a sideboard or in a row with other breeds. Red, black and cream go with white walls, dark wood, brass and leather, and it fits anyone who shares a sofa with a Frenchie. It pairs with the others in the same series.
