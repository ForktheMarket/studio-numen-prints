This Become print is a minimalist black and white poster showing a caterpillar on a leaf, with a butterfly inside the water drop hanging beneath it.

A dark, ribbed caterpillar rests near the tip of a wet leaf that enters from the right side of the picture. One clear drop hangs from the leaf's edge, and inside it, small and sharp, is a butterfly with patterned wings. Everything else is empty space, a smooth fade from pale gray at the top to solid black at the bottom. The word BECOME sits low in the dark area in small white serif capitals with wide spacing.

It is the quietest print in the set. You have to step closer to find the butterfly, and that small discovery is what people remember. The meaning is change, and the patience it takes. The open, simple layout suits modern and minimalist rooms. It works in a bedroom, a home office, a hallway, a reading corner, a yoga or therapy room or a nursery. It is a good match for graduates, people changing careers, nature lovers and anyone in the middle of becoming someone new.
