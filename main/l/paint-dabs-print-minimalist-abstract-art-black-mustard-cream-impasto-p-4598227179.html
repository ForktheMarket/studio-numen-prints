This Paint Dabs print is a minimalist textured abstract poster of a few separate thick dabs of black, mustard and cream paint scattered on a pale gray ground, for anyone who likes a lot of calm space with small bursts of texture in it.

Two black dabs sit low on the left, four mustard ones are spread through the middle, one cream smear sits alone near the bottom, and a large heaped mass of cream paint spills in from the top right corner, folded and creased like thick icing. The gray ground has a fine linen weave and takes up most of the picture.

The colors are black, mustard yellow, cream and soft gray, light and playful. It suits a bedroom above the bed, a home office, a living room or a nursery wall that wants something grown up, and it goes with gray, white and light wood. An easy pick for a modern minimalist room.
