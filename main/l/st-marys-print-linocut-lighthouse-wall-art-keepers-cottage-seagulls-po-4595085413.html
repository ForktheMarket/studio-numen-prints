The St Marys print is a linocut style lighthouse poster for a coastal kitchen, a hallway or a bedroom with a view of the sea.

A tall white lighthouse stands to the right of the picture with a low keeper's cottage beside it, two chimneys on the roof and a flat sea behind. Three gulls wheel in the sky on the left, cut as simple pale shapes with dark edges. The foreground is a rough slope of golden grass and dark shadow, scratched with long angled marks that run toward the buildings. The sky is streaked blue with pale clouds scored through it, and the printed block has a scuffed edge. Four colors, blue, ochre, navy and cream. The words ST MARYS run along the bottom in plain navy capitals.

It suits a kitchen, a hallway, a bedroom or a cabin, above a bed or a kitchen table. Blue and ochre go with white walls, warm oak, wicker and cream linen. It fits walkers, birdwatchers and anyone who knows Whitley Bay, and it hangs well beside Longstone and Bell Rock from the same series.
