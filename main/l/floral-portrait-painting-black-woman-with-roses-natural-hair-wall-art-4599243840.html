This floral portrait is a painting of a young woman with natural curls looking out from a bank of cream garden roses, made for bedrooms, dressing rooms and guest rooms.

Her hair is gathered high, a few curls falling loose around her face, and she rests her chin on a bare shoulder with a direct, calm look. Big blush and cream blooms crowd in around her, painted thickly against a dark green garden background. Late sun warms her cheeks, a scatter of freckles and a gold earring.

The colors are blush pink, cream, deep green and warm brown. It feels romantic without being sugary. It looks good above a bed, over a dresser or vanity, or in a bathroom with warm lighting. The roses soften the direct gaze, so the picture feels both bold and gentle.
