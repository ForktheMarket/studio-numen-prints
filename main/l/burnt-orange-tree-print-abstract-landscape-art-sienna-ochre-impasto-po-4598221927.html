This Burnt Orange Tree print is an abstract landscape poster of a small dense knot of burnt sienna and ochre paint standing like a lone tree on a wide pale plain, for anyone who likes a lot of empty space with one strong thing in it.

Most of the picture is a warm off white sky, brushed smooth and almost blank. Low in the frame the tree flares up in thick rust and orange, with a thin pale trunk and a drift of white paint blowing off it like smoke. Along the bottom the ground is scraped on in heavy ochre and taupe, with the orange glow bleeding into it at the horizon.

The colors are burnt orange, sienna, ochre, taupe and warm white, calm with one hot spot. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a study or a landing, and works especially well in a minimalist room that needs one point of color. It also has an autumn feel for anyone who decorates by season.
