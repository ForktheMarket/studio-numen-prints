This Mare and Foal print is a folk art poster of a tall orange mare with a white saddle patch standing beside a small spindly foal, both drawn as simple elongated shapes on a pale sand ground, for anyone who wants a mother and baby picture without any sweetness laid on too thick.

The mare fills the right of the frame on four long tapered legs with a bright white patch across her middle. The foal stands to the left, half her height, on legs that look too thin to hold it up, with the same dot eye and pricked ears. The ground is a warm cream with soft brush marks.

The colors are burnt orange, apricot, white and sand, warm and gentle. It suits a nursery, a child's room, a living room or a hallway, and it goes well with cream walls, natural wood and linen. A natural gift for a new baby or a new mother, and a quiet piece for a family home.
