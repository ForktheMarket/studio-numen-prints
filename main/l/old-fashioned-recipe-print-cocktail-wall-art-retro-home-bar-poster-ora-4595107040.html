The Old Fashioned print is a retro cocktail poster for a home bar, a den or a kitchen.

The drink name is lettered across the top in dark rust red capitals with a worn, inky texture, and the recipe sits beneath it on the left on three lines: Bourbon, Sugar, Bitters. A short rocks glass stands at the front, the drink inside a warm amber orange over one big square ice cube, with a dark cherry and a wide twist of orange peel resting on top. Behind it a tall bottle of bourbon, drawn in flat orange and rust, fills the right side, and a spray of dark green shadow spreads across the cream background to the left. The style is flat and screen printed.

It suits a home bar, a den, a dining room or a kitchen, hung beside the whiskey shelf or above a leather armchair. Amber, rust and dark green go well with leather, oak, brass and tan. It fits bourbon drinkers and anyone who likes an old school cocktail, and it pairs with the Negroni and the Whisky Sour from the same series.
