The Godrevy print is a linocut style Cornish lighthouse poster for a surfer's bedroom, a coastal bathroom or a beach house.

The picture is split in two. At the top, a squat round lighthouse in mustard and dark teal sits on a rocky island against a plain cream sky, with a smaller rock breaking the surface to its right. Below, a wall of teal sea rolls across the full width, cut with hundreds of fine horizontal lines, and a single surfer in black silhouette drops down the face of a breaking wave on a mustard board, the white foam left as bare cream. The whole scene uses only teal, mustard and cream. The word GODREVY runs along the bottom in wide mustard capitals.

It suits a bathroom, a teenager's room, a holiday let or a hallway, above a bath or beside a board rack. Teal and mustard go with white walls, pale wood, rope and faded denim. It fits surfers, swimmers and anyone who has looked across the bay from St Ives, and it pairs well with Bell Rock and The Needles from the same series.
