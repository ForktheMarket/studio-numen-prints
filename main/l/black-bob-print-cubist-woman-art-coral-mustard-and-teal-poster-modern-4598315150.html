This Black Bob print is a cubist portrait poster of a woman with a flat black bob, a long neck and one closed eye, her face painted in coral and brick, for anyone who wants a calm, stylish figure on the wall.

Her hair is a single black shape that wraps the whole head. The face is cut into planes of coral and salmon with a dark red lid and lip, and a teal disc earring with a red drop hangs from her ear. The neck runs straight down to the bottom edge in two tones of orange, and the background is split into blocks of mustard, cream, olive and rust.

The colors are coral, mustard, teal, black, olive and cream, warm with one cool accent. It suits a bedroom, a dressing room, a hallway or a living room, and it sits well above a bed, a dresser or a vanity. A print for anyone who likes mid century portraits, strong hair shapes and faces with their eyes closed.
