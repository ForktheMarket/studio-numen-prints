The Bell Rock print is a linocut style Scottish lighthouse poster for a bathroom, a hallway or a sea loving home.

A single tall stone tower rises from the open sea with no land in sight, its base flaring out at the waterline. The tower is cut with fine curved lines that follow the stonework, with four small windows climbing the wall and a dark lamp room at the top. White water breaks around the base in rough splashes, and below it the sea is heavy navy with pale ripples. The sky is a soft washed teal, and the printed area has a ragged, uneven edge where the ink runs out. Three colors, teal, navy and cream. The words BELL ROCK run along the bottom in heavy navy capitals.

It suits a bathroom, a downstairs toilet, a hallway or a study, above a toilet or a desk. Teal, navy and cream go with white walls, slate, dark wood and linen. It fits sailors, engineers and anyone from Scotland's east coast, and it hangs well beside Longstone and Beachy Head from the same series.
