This Caribbean map print is an antique style chart of the Caribbean Sea, from Florida and the Gulf coast down to the northern shore of South America, made for beach houses, offices and hallways.

Cuba, Hispaniola, Jamaica and the chain of smaller islands are drawn in sepia with fine hatched hills, and the sea is washed in a deep faded teal that is richer than on the other maps in this series. Central America and the Yucatan curve along the left, and an empty ornate cartouche sits in the top right corner. A compass rose anchors the bottom left, with rhumb lines fanning out across the water.

Sepia, cream and teal give it a warm nautical feel that suits a coastal living room, a home office or a bathroom with white walls. It pairs with the Italy and British Isles maps from the same series.
