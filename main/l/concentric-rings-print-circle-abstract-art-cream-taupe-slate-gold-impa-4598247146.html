This Concentric Rings print is a textured circle abstract poster of wide rings of cream, taupe and slate paint spiraling in from a dark outer edge to a small bright center, for anyone who wants one strong shape that pulls the eye in.

The rings are combed into the paint with a knife, so each one stands up in a ridge and catches the light differently. The outer edge is a broken circle of charcoal, slate and gold, scuffed and rough, and the rings grow smoother and paler toward the middle, where they finish in a tight cream spiral. The ground around it is a flat putty gray.

The colors are cream, taupe, slate gray, charcoal and soft gold, calm with a little shine. It suits a living room above the sofa, a hallway, an entryway or a dining room, and it works with gray, black and warm wood. A good single piece for a wall that only wants one thing on it.
