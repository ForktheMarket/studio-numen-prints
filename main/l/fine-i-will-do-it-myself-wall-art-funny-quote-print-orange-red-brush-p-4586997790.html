This Fine I Will Do It Myself print is a funny quote poster for the person who ends up doing everything, at home or at work.

The line is painted in thin, tall orange red brush capitals on a cream background, with the first word alone at the top like a sigh. Four faint red corner marks stand in for a border, brushed so lightly that they fade out before they meet. There is a lot of open cream space around the words, so it feels lighter and drier than a block of heavy type would.

The tone is mock annoyed instead of angry, which is why it works in rooms other people use. The orange red shows up well on white, gray and pale blue walls. It suits a home office, the wall above a desk, a kitchen, a laundry room or a shared workspace where the dishwasher never gets emptied.
