This look up print is a retro telescope poster for a kids bedroom, a study or a nursery.

The words LOOK UP sit across the top in bold marigold yellow capitals on a dusty blue night sky. Below them a single four pointed cream star hangs in the center of the picture. A telescope on a three legged tripod stands in the lower half, tilted up toward the star, drawn in dark teal with thin yellow highlights catching its edges. A low line of dark hills runs across the bottom under a near black foreground. A cream border surrounds the whole picture. The style is flat and grainy with a slightly faded look, and the mood is still and hopeful.

It suits a boys bedroom, a girls bedroom, a study or a landing, and it works above a desk or a bed. Blue, yellow and cream go with white walls, oak furniture, navy bedding and brass lamps, and it pairs with the Saturn and earth prints from the same series. It fits children who ask about the stars and families who like a simple poster with a message.
