This Folk Moth print is a bold decorative poster of one large cream moth with black patterned wings, filling the page against a deep tomato red ground, for anyone who wants a single strong graphic shape on the wall.

The moth is painted flat and symmetrical, its wings marked with rows of black dashes and spots and its striped body running straight down the center. The edges are soft and hand painted rather than crisp. The red background is rich and slightly uneven, with brush texture showing through.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a hallway or an eclectic dining room, and it holds its own next to busy wallpaper or plain white walls alike. It also makes a strong pair with the striped clay pots print, which shares the same red and cream.
