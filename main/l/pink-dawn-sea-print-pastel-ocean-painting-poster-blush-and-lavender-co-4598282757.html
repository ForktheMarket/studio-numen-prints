This Pink Dawn Sea print is a painterly seascape poster of a blush pink sky over a sea of lavender gray swells, for anyone who wants coastal art in soft pink rather than blue.

The top half is a smooth gradient from rose to pale apricot at the horizon, with no clouds. Below it the sea rolls in toward the viewer in loose strokes of mauve, dove gray and white, the foam on the waves picking up the pink from the sky. The water is lively but the colors keep it calm.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a girl's room, a bathroom or a dressing room, and it pairs with the morning fog print for a soft pair. The gray in the water stops the pink from feeling sugary, so it works in an adult bedroom as well as a child's.
