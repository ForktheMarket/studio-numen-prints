This white peony still life is a dark painting of a single full peony in a black glass vase, made for bedrooms, living rooms and anyone who loves moody floral art.

The bloom is large and soft, its creamy white petals loose and layered, sitting above a few dark green leaves. The vase is almost black and nearly disappears into the background. A single light source makes the flower glow out of the dark. Every petal is painted with soft folds and shadows, and a few leaves curl out to the side, giving the flower a natural, unposed look.

It is simple and elegant, with very little color beyond cream and black. It suits a bedroom above a bed or dresser, a living room, a hallway, or a dark painted wall. A calm counterpoint to the red roses in the same series.
