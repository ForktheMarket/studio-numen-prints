This Turquoise Sea print is a painterly ocean poster where the sea fills almost the whole picture, with just a sliver of pale sky at the top, for anyone who wants water and light and not much else.

The horizon sits high, and below it the water rolls toward the viewer in loose strokes of turquoise, teal and mint, with white foam on the swells and a bright path of gold reflection running down from the sun. The foreground waves are thicker and more textured than the distance, which gives the picture depth without any land in it.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom or a sunny living room, and the color sits well with white walls, rattan and pale wood. Hung in a windowless bathroom it does the job of a window, which is more or less what it is for.
