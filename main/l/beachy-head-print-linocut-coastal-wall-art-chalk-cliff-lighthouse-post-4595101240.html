The Beachy Head print is a linocut style coastal poster for a seaside home, a bathroom or anyone who loves the Sussex cliffs.

A tall white chalk cliff fills the left two thirds of the picture, its face cut with rough vertical gouge lines in cream and teal so the rock looks weathered and flaking. The clifftop is a solid block of black, and a small striped lighthouse stands at the edge against the sky. A single chalk stack rises from the flat teal sea below. The sky is the same soft teal, speckled where the ink has not quite covered. Three colors only, teal, black and cream. The words BEACHY HEAD run along the bottom in bold black capitals.

It suits a bathroom, a hallway, a coastal bedroom or a study, above a bath or beside a front door. Teal, black and cream go with white walls, pale oak, rattan and navy textiles. It fits walkers, sailors and anyone from the south coast, and it pairs well with The Needles and Bell Rock from the same series.
