This Open Ocean print is a painterly seascape poster of deep teal water seen from above, the surface ruffled by wind into endless small waves, for anyone who wants the sea as texture rather than a view.

There is no horizon, no sky and no land. The water fills the whole picture, darker and smoother at the top, brighter and more broken toward the bottom where white foam streaks across the swells. The color runs from deep blue green to turquoise, with light catching the ridges of the waves. It works almost as an abstract.

It suits a bathroom, a bedroom or a modern living room, and it pairs well with the gray sea and wave face prints from the same series. The texture gives it plenty to look at up close, so it works in a small room as well as a large one.
