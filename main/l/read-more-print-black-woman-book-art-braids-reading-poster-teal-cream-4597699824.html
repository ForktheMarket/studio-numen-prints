This Read More print is a retro poster of a Black woman with long braids sitting cross legged on a rug with a tall stack of books, for anyone who always has one more on the pile.

She wears a cream jacket over a coral sweater and dark teal trousers, with coral socks, and holds an open orange book in both hands. Books in rust, cream and teal are stacked beside her up to shoulder height. The background splits into cream above and deep teal below, with READ MORE in wide teal serif capitals across the top.

The colors are teal, cream, coral and rust, with a light speckled texture that gives it a screen printed feel. It suits a bedroom, a home library, a reading nook or a study, and it works well above a bookshelf or a desk. A plain reminder for readers, students and anyone trying to swap scrolling for pages.
