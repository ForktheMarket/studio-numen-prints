This camper van print is a soft retro road trip poster for a nursery, a kids bedroom or a family that lives for summer vacations.

A mint green and cream camper van sits side on in the middle of the page, with a bright coral surfboard strapped to its roof and a single palm tree rising behind it. The van has a split windshield, round headlights, a row of side windows and whitewall tires with mint hubcaps. It is parked on a strip of sand above a dark teal road with two pale lines running across it. The background is plain warm cream, and the words ROAD TRIP sit above the palm tree in dark teal spaced capitals. The style is painted and slightly grainy, with a soft shadow under the van, and the mood is sunny and unhurried.

It suits a nursery, a kids bedroom, a playroom or a beach house hallway. Mint, coral and cream go well with white walls, light oak, rattan and striped cotton. It fits surfers, campers and new parents who already have the family road trip planned.
