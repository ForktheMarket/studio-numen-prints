The foxglove print is a vintage botanical plate poster for a bedroom, a hallway or anyone who loves a cottage garden.

A single tall foxglove spike stands upright in the middle of the plate. The lower two thirds are crowded with drooping pink bells, each one tube shaped and flared at the mouth, with crimson spots inside a pale throat. The bells get smaller toward the top, where a dozen small green buds crowd the tip, still closed. Two broad green leaves sit at the base. The ground is a plain warm cream, and the word FOXGLOVE is lettered in small spaced brown capitals at the bottom. It has the fine lines and gentle shading of an old engraved plate.

It suits a bedroom above the bed, a bathroom, a hallway or a landing, especially where the wall is tall and narrow. Rose pink, dark green and cream go well with white walls, pale wood, linen and sage green, and it hangs well beside the dog rose from the same series. It fits gardeners and anyone who lets a corner go wild.
