The Lisbon poster is a vintage style travel print for anyone who has squeezed onto the little yellow tram and rattled uphill through the old town.

A yellow tram climbs a steep, narrow street between tall sunlit houses in lemon, apricot and terracotta, with the rest of the city stacked up the hill behind it under a pale green sky. The name sits below in bold black capitals. The palette is warm yellow, orange, cream and a cool mint sky, printed flat with crisp shadows that make the street feel hot and bright.

It suits a kitchen, a hallway, a living room or a bedroom, and it works well above a bed or a sofa, or in a gallery wall of city prints. It is a good fit for anyone who loves Portugal and wants a bit of its light on the wall.
