This I Got You Bro print is a funny goose poster for brothers, best friends and the one person who always turns up when you need them.

A white goose stretches its long neck up from the bottom of the picture and looks straight at you through a pair of square black sunglasses. Its beak is set in a flat, serious line, like a bodyguard on duty. Above its head, in small typewriter letters, are the words I got you bro.. and nothing else. The goose is drawn with a loose black outline and soft pale green shading, on a flat mustard yellow background with a brushed shadow behind it.

The joke is in the gap between the tiny, quiet line of text and the very serious bird. It is warm underneath, a way of saying you have someone's back without making a speech about it. It suits a bedroom, a dorm room, the hallway of a shared apartment, a home office or a games room. The yellow sits well with wood, white walls and navy.
