This Hair Day print is a poster of a Black mother sitting on a stool braiding her daughter's hair while the girl sits cross legged on the floor below, for anyone who spent Sunday evenings exactly like this.

The mother leans over in a cream top and wide mustard trousers, her own afro loose, working a section of the girl's hair between her fingers. The daughter sits still in a mustard tee with her eyes down and her puff half done. A jar of hair cream and a comb sit on the floor beside them, and the background is a band of peach on cream.

The colors are mustard, peach, cream and warm brown, soft and close. It suits a girls bedroom, a bathroom, a hallway or a salon, and it sits well above a bed or a dresser. A print for mothers, daughters and anyone who knows the smell of that jar.
