The garden insects print is a vintage entomology plate poster for a kitchen, a cottagecore living room or a gardener.

Three garden insects are set out on a warm cream plate. At the top a red ladybug with black spots has its clear veined wings spread wide from under its shell. Below it a round bumblebee sits side on, thick with fur in bands of yellow and black with a pale tail. To the right a small chestnut brown beetle with a black head and reddish legs stands on its own. The drawing has the fine hatching of an old engraved plate with color laid over the top, and GARDEN INSECTS is lettered in small capitals at the bottom. The mood is cheerful and old fashioned.

It suits a kitchen, a hallway or a child's bedroom, and works above a kitchen table or a desk. Red, yellow and cream go with white walls, pine, terracotta and sage green, and it pairs with the British moths and stag beetle from the same series. It fits gardeners, bee keepers and families with curious kids.
