This Cobalt Blue Stroke print is a textured abstract poster of one thick curved stroke of cobalt paint sweeping across a cream ground, cropped so close that the stroke fills most of the frame, for anyone who wants a single confident mark on the wall.

The blue arcs in from the top right, every bristle line visible, and tears into the cream at its left edge in sharp torn peaks of paint. The cream is heaped and scraped around it, with small flecks of blue caught in the ridges. There is nothing else in the picture, which is the point.

The colors are cobalt, royal blue, sky blue, cream and white, clean and energetic. It suits a bedroom above the bed, a living room, a bathroom or a coastal style hallway, and it works with white, pale wood and brass. It is a natural fit for blue and white rooms.
