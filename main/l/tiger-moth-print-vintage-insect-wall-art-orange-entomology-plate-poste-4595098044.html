The tiger moth print is a vintage entomology plate poster for a cottagecore bedroom, a hallway or a moth lover.

A single tiger moth is spread flat in the center of the plate with its wings open wide. The forewings are cream with bold chocolate brown blotches and spots laid across them like a leopard print. Beneath them the hindwings are a bright orange red with a row of black spots along the edge, and the furry body is banded in the same orange and black. Two long thin antennae sweep up and back. The moth casts a soft shadow onto the warm cream background, and TIGER MOTH is lettered in small capitals below. The style is painted and detailed.

It suits a bedroom, a hallway or a study, and sits well above a bed or a desk. Orange, black and cream go with white walls, dark wood, rattan and sage green, and it pairs with the emperor moth and British moths from the same series. It fits gardeners and anyone who likes a bit of color.
