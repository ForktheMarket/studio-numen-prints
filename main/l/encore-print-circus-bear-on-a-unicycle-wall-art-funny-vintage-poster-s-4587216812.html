Encore is a funny vintage circus style poster of a brown bear riding a unicycle in a tiny red fez, for kids rooms, music rooms and anyone who likes an animal taking its job seriously.

A big brown bear rides a blue unicycle toward you with both arms stretched out wide for balance. On its head is a very small red fez. The word ENCORE sits above in teal blue capitals with slab serifs. The bear is drawn in fine lines with shaggy fur, like an old engraving, and there is a lot of open, warm cream space around it.

The joke is in the scale: a very large animal, a very small hat and one thin wheel. The bear looks like it is concentrating hard. Brown, teal, cream and one spot of red are warm, easy colors that sit well with wood furniture and white walls. It suits a nursery, a kids room, a playroom, a music room, a home office or a hallway. It is a good match for bear lovers, cyclists, musicians, performers and anyone who keeps getting asked to do it one more time.
