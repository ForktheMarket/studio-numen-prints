This pug print is a vintage poster for pug owners and anyone who wants a small dog with a big personality on the wall.

A fawn pug sits in three quarter view with its head tilted up to the right, as if it has just heard its name. The dog is drawn in black, warm gray and cream, with the black mask, ears and eyes against a pale body and a soft chest. Around it runs a black rectangular border with a speckled worn edge, backed by a band of apricot, and at the top a curling apricot ribbon carries the word PUG in black slab capitals. The whole thing sits on a cream background and has the look of an old seed packet or tobacco label.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a kitchen or a downstairs toilet, hung at eye level where people pass. Apricot, black and cream go with white walls, dark wood, brass and green plants, and it fits anyone who lives with a pug. It pairs with the other breeds in the same series.
