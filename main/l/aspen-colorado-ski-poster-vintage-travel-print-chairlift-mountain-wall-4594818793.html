The Aspen Colorado ski poster is a vintage style travel print for skiers who love the quiet ride up as much as the run down.

A single chairlift tower rises into a pale ice blue sky, seen from directly below, with empty chairs climbing away on either side and two tiny figures in red at the top. A thin line of snowy peaks sits along the bottom edge and the resort name is set in tall black capitals beneath. It is the most minimal print in the set, mostly sky and cream, with the two red figures as the only color.

The pale palette suits a bedroom, a bathroom, a hallway or a calm living room, and it works well above a bed or hung as a pair with another light print. It pairs with the Alpine resorts in the set for a gallery wall of mountain towns.
