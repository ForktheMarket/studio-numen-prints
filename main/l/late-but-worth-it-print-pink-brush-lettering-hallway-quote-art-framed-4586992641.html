This Late But Worth It print is a funny quote poster for the person who is never on time and never sorry about it.

The four words are stacked in tall hot pink brush capitals on a cream background, with the last word sitting on its own at the bottom like a punchline. A border of short pink dashes runs around all four edges, a bit like the stitching on a blanket or the edge of a postage stamp. The pink is bright and warm, closer to raspberry than pastel, and the strokes are thick enough that the words read from across a room.

It is a confident, slightly cheeky print, so it suits the places where you get ready and the places you rush past on the way out. Think a hallway, an entryway by the front door, a bedroom, a dressing room or the wall above a vanity. The pink pairs easily with white walls, brass, rattan and other bright prints.
