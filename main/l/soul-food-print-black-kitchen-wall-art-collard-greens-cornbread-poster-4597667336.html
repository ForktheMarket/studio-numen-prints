This Soul Food print is a kitchen poster of a Black woman's hands tossing a pot of collard greens over a table of cornbread, peaches and sweet potato, for anyone who grew up around a Sunday spread.

The view is straight down from above. Two hands with gold rings and orange nails work through dark green leaves in a black cast iron pot, and around it the table is packed tight: squares of yellow cornbread, wedges of roast sweet potato, a dish of baked beans, a jar of iced tea and halved peaches with the stones still in. The colors are rich and hot, orange, mustard, deep green and brown, with a cream band across the top for the words SOUL FOOD in bold black serif. It is a print you can almost smell.

It suits a kitchen, a dining room or the wall above a breakfast table, and it holds its own in a restaurant or cafe too.
