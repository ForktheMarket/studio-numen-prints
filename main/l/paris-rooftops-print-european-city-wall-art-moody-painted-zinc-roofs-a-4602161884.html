This Paris rooftops print looks across a steep tumble of old city roofs at dusk, with lit windows glowing orange against gray zinc and slate, made for living rooms, bedrooms and apartments.

The rooftops run in a tight diagonal from the upper left, a jumble of dormer windows, chimney pots and mansard slopes painted in loose strokes of charcoal, cream and rust. Down the center a long street of warm lights leads to a distant church spire against a pale evening sky. A few windows blaze amber, the rest sit dark.

The palette is gray, soft cream and glowing orange, warm and romantic, which makes it a good fit above a bed or sofa. It suits anyone who has spent an evening in a top floor European apartment or wishes they had. It pairs well with any of the rain scenes in the set for a two city gallery wall.
