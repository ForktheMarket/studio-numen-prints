This Freedom print is a black and white horse poster showing a young foal standing on open moorland, with its mane blown sideways by the wind and its reflection in still water below.

The foal is almost a silhouette, long-legged and narrow, head dipped, standing on rough ground under a heavy bank of cloud. There is nothing else on the horizon, only a low dark hill. The bottom half of the picture is a sheet of flat water that mirrors the animal and the sky. The word FREEDOM runs across the middle in white serif capitals with wide spacing.

The feeling is space. No fence, no rider, no building, just weather and a young horse that has not been told where to go yet. The layout is split evenly between land and water, which keeps it steady and easy to live with, and the tones are mostly soft gray with one strong black shape. It suits a living room, a bedroom, an entryway, a farmhouse kitchen, a tack room or a study. It is a good match for riders, horse owners, country people and anyone who wants more open sky in their day.
