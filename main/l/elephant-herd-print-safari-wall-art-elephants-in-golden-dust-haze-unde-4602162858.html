This elephant herd print shows a family of elephants walking low across a dusty plain, small beneath a vast amber sky and a pale round sun, made for living rooms, offices and anyone who loves African wildlife.

The herd sits in the lower third of the picture as a line of soft dark shapes, trunks swinging, half lost in the dust they kick up. Above them the sky is one broad wash of gold and apricot haze, with the sun a faint white disc high in the heat. Dry grass in the foreground is painted in loose brown strokes.

The palette is warm gold, amber and umber, with nothing cold in it, so it brings a soft heat to a neutral room. It works above a sofa, behind a desk or in a hallway, and suits anyone who has watched elephants cross open ground.
