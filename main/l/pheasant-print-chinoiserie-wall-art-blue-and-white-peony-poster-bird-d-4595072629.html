The pheasant print is a blue and white chinoiserie poster for a dining room, a study or a country house kitchen.

A long tailed pheasant perches on a bare branch with its head raised and its beak open, as if calling. The head is a deep indigo with a white ring at the neck, the breast is scaled with fine crescent marks, and the tail sweeps down to the bottom right in long cobalt streaks. Peony blooms surround the bird, one large flower at the top right and a cluster of five more along the branch below, each petal built up in layers of pale and dark blue. The ground is a warm white with a faint mottled wash, and the line work in the feathers and petals is fine and confident.

It suits a dining room, a study, a kitchen or a hallway, above a sideboard or next to a bookcase. Blue and white goes with dark wood, tartan, brass, leather and green walls, and it fits bird watchers and anyone who likes old sporting prints. It pairs with the crane from the same series.
