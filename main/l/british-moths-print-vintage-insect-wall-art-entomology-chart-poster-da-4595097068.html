The British moths print is a vintage entomology chart poster for a hallway, a study or a moth lover.

Nine small moths are arranged in three neat rows of three on a warm cream plate. The top row holds a dark brown moth with orange hindwings, a soft gray moth with folded wings, and a dark moth with orange and red spotted wings. The middle row has a bright scarlet moth, a dark navy moth with red blotches, and a pale white moth at rest. The bottom row shows a mottled charcoal moth, an orange and cream patterned moth, and a pale buff moth. Each is painted in detail, and BRITISH MOTHS is lettered in small capitals at the bottom. The mood is calm and orderly.

It suits a hallway, a study or a bedroom, and works above a desk or a sideboard. Orange, red, gray and cream go with white walls, dark wood, brass and sage green, and it pairs with the single moth plates from the same series. It fits gardeners, collectors and readers of old nature books.
