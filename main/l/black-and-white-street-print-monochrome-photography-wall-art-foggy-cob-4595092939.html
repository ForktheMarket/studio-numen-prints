This cobbled street print is a black and white photographic poster for a hallway, a study or anyone who likes old cities after dark.

A narrow lane of wet cobblestones curves away between tall old stone buildings and disappears into thick fog. On the right a single street lamp glows warm against the mist, the only light in the picture, and it catches the rounded tops of the cobbles so they shine like scales. The buildings on both sides are nearly black, with bay windows, chimneys and railings just visible, and the far end of the street fades to pale gray. Almost the whole image is deep shadow, with the highlights saved for the lamp and the wet stones. The mood is quiet and mysterious, like the start of a story.

It suits a hallway, a study or a living room, above a desk or a console table. The dark tones go with cream walls, dark wood, leather and brass, and it pairs with the spiral staircase from the same series. It fits readers, city lovers and anyone who likes a dark, atmospheric wall.
