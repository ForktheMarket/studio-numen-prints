This cracked plaster print shows a surface of thick warm white plaster split by a web of fine cracks, with a single patch of raw ochre showing through near the center, made for living rooms, hallways and calm neutral homes.

The cracks run across the sheet like a dry riverbed, deeper at the edges where the plaster has lifted, and the texture is heavy and tactile, with ridges and crumbled lumps catching soft light. The ochre patch is the one warm note, a rough square of golden clay where the white has fallen away.

The palette is cream, bone white and warm gold, so it works in rooms built around linen, wood and stone. It suits a wabi sabi or Japandi interior, a gallery wall of neutral prints, and anyone who finds beauty in worn and weathered surfaces.
