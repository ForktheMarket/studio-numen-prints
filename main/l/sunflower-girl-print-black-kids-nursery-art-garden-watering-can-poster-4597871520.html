This Sunflower Girl print is a nursery poster of a little Black girl with afro puffs watering a patch of tall sunflowers, for anyone making a bright, happy room for a daughter.

She stands in blue overalls over a white tee, smiling to herself as water arcs out of a yellow can onto the ground. The sunflowers tower over her in loose painted strokes of gold and amber, with big green leaves and a clear blue sky behind. A few small yellow blooms peek in at the bottom corners.

The colors are sunflower yellow, leaf green, denim blue and warm brown, cheerful without being sugary. It suits a nursery, a girls bedroom, a playroom or a hallway, and it works well above a crib or a reading corner. A gentle print for little gardeners and the grown ups who are raising them.
