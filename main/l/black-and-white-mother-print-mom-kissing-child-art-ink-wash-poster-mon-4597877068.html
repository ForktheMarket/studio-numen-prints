This Black and White Mother print is an ink wash poster of a Black mother kissing her small child on the lips, for anyone who wants that moment on the wall in the plainest, truest form.

The child tips their head back with eyes closed, soft curls painted in loose black strokes. The mother leans down with her hair pinned up and her eyes shut, both faces built from washes of gray and black on white. Rough brush marks fade out at the bottom edge.

There is no color at all, which keeps it calm and lets it sit beside anything. It suits a nursery, a bedroom, a hallway or a living room, and it fits straight into a black and white gallery wall. A tender print for new mothers, grandmothers and anyone who still remembers being kissed like that.
