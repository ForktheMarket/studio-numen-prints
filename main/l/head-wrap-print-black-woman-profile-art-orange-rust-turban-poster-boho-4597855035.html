This Head Wrap print is a boho portrait poster of a Black woman in profile wearing a tall striped head wrap, for anyone who wants warm color and a strong silhouette in one piece.

She faces right with her chin lifted, her face and neck a single shape of deep brown with the features drawn in a fine line. The wrap is built from thick curved bands of orange, rust and cream, stacked high above her head. Behind her is a soft peach arch on a cream background with a faint plaster texture.

The colors are orange, rust, peach, cream and brown, earthy and bright at once. It suits a living room, a bedroom, a hallway or an entryway, and it sits well above a sofa, a bed or a console. A print for anyone who loves a head wrap, and a good partner for the Blue Braids print from the same set.
