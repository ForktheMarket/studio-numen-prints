This Distant Islands print is a painterly coastal poster of low islands on the horizon at sunset, with gold light laid across a blue sea, for anyone who wants a view to look at from the sofa.

The sky is a soft wash of peach and gray blue with a few loose clouds. Below it the sea runs from pale silver at the horizon to deep navy in the foreground, painted in broad strokes with gold and cream flecks where the light hits the water. The islands are small dark shapes with gold edges, far enough away to feel like somewhere you might sail to.

It suits a living room, a bedroom or a beach house, and it pairs well with the storm light and calm sea prints from the same series. It is a good one for a room you relax in at the end of the day, since the light in it is evening light.
