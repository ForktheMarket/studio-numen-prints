This Green Horse print is a folk art poster cropped tight on the head and long neck of a tall olive green horse, rising from the bottom corner across a plain cream ground, for anyone who wants a single strong animal shape with almost nothing else in the frame.

The neck is one broad curved shape with a thin black stripe of mane running down its back edge. The head has two rounded ears, a single dark almond eye and a black muzzle, and it looks calmly off to the right. The green is brushed in soft layers from olive to dark moss, and the background is a warm, lightly textured cream.

The colors are olive green, moss, black and cream, calm and slightly unusual. It suits a hallway, a living room, a child's room or a home office, and it pairs well with sage, cream, oak and brass. A good choice for anyone decorating around green who wants a face in the room.
