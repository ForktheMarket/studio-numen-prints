This What Is Meant for You Will Not Pass You print is a black and cream quote poster for anyone waiting on news, getting over a near miss or trying to stop chasing.

The nine words are painted in chunky cream brush capitals across four lines on a soft black background, with a single cream leaf brushed in after the last word. The letters are thick and a little uneven, with dry edges where the brush ran out of paint, so it reads as lettered by hand and not typeset. The black has a faint chalky variation to it, like a painted board.

The saying is an old one, the kind grandmothers pass on. It means the job, the home or the person that is right for you will still be there, and the ones that slipped away were never yours. It is calm, not pushy. Black and cream are neutral, so it fits most color schemes and mixes easily into a black and white gallery wall. It suits a bedroom, the wall above a bed, a hallway, a living room or a home office. It is a good match for job hunters, house hunters, the newly single and anyone who needs to hear that they have not missed their chance.
