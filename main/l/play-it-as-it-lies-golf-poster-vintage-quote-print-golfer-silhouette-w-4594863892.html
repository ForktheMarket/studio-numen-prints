The Play It As It Lies golf poster is a vintage style quote print for golfers who take the oldest rule in the game as advice for life as well.

A lone figure in a flat cap walks up the long slope of a dark green hill with a bag over one shoulder and a club in hand, a tiny silhouette against a huge sky of soft peach pink. The hill fills the lower half of the print and the saying is set across it in cream block capitals, small on the first line and large on the second. Two colors do all the work, a warm pink and a deep bottle green, and the result is calm rather than busy.

It suits a hallway, a home office, a bedroom, a study or a living room, and it works well above a desk, a bed or a console table. It pairs with the Never Up Never In print for a pair of old golf sayings.
