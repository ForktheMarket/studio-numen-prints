The London poster is a vintage style travel print for anyone who has crossed Westminster Bridge on the top deck and looked up at the clock.

A bright red double decker bus sweeps over the bridge toward the viewer, with the clock tower rising behind it in soft gray and a pale sky washed in cream. The name runs along the bottom in chunky red letters. The palette is pillar box red, slate gray and cream, with a brushed, slightly grainy finish that gives it the look of a 1930s railway poster.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a study or a bedroom, and it works well above a bed or a desk, or as the anchor of a gallery wall of city prints. It is a good fit for Londoners, people who moved away and anyone who has had a good week there.
