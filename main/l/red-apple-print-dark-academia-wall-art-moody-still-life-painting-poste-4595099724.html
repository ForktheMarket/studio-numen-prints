The red apple print is a moody still life poster for a teacher, a classroom or a study.

A single bright red apple with a short brown stem sits on three old leather books stacked on a dark wooden table. Its skin is glossy, with a white highlight at the upper left and small specks across it, the only strong color in the picture. The books are worn brown and tan with raised bands on the spines and rough cream page edges. To the right a black feather quill leans against the stack beside a small dark glass inkwell. The background is nearly black, so the apple seems to glow under light from the upper left. The paint is thick on the page edges and the table, and smooth on the apple.

It suits a study, a kitchen, a classroom or a hallway, above a desk or on a shelf. The red, brown and cream go with dark wood, chalkboard black, brass and green walls. It fits teachers, students and readers, and it pairs with the leather books and the fountain pen from the same series.
