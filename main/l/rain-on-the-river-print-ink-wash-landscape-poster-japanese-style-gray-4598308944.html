This Rain on the River print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a wooded shore reflected in still water, with rain falling as long vertical streaks of ink down the page, for anyone who likes quiet, moody pictures.

The trees along the far bank are painted in soft gray and black washes with a few sharp pines, and their reflection stretches down into the water in dragged vertical strokes that double as the rain. The sky is a warm cream haze. There is very little hard detail anywhere, which is what gives it its stillness.

It suits an office, a study, a living room or a bedroom, and it works well with gray, oak and linen. It also pairs with the lake and boat print, which shares the same still water and soft gray trees, for a quiet pair.
