This Guitar Player print is a cubist poster of a seated musician reduced to arcs and circles, with the round body of the guitar at the center of it all, for anyone who wants a music picture that is more shape than portrait.

A big orange and dark green arc curves over the top like the player's bent arm and head, a bright orange disc sits where the face would be, and the guitar's sound hole is a ringed circle in olive and black in the middle of the page. Below, the body and legs fall away in blocks of sage, black, rust and mustard. The background is a plain warm cream so the shapes do all the work.

The colors are orange, olive, black, rust, mustard and cream, bold and earthy. It suits a music room, a living room, a study or a bedroom, and it looks right above a sofa, a record shelf or a piano. A print for guitar players, for anyone who collects records, and for anyone who likes mid century art built from circles.
