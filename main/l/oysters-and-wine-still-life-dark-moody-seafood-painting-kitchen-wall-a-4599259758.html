This oysters and wine still life is a dark painting of fresh oysters on a pewter plate with a glass of white wine, made for dining rooms, kitchens and seafood lovers.

Six open oysters sit on ice on an old pewter plate with a cut lemon half beside them. Behind them a glass of pale white wine catches the light. The background is close to black, and the shells shine in soft pearly grays. Small pieces of ice glint between the shells, and the lemon is cut clean, its bright yellow the warmest color on the plate.

Cream, gold and silver against black give it a cool, elegant feel. It suits a dining room, a kitchen, a home bar, or a coastal restaurant. It pairs well with the lemons and copper pot in the same series.
