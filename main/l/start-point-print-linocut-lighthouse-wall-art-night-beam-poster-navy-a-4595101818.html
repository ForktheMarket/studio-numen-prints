The Start Point print is a linocut style lighthouse poster for a dark hallway, a bedroom or anyone who likes a bit of drama.

A lighthouse stands dead center at night, its tower in deep blue shadow against a slate blue sky. From the lamp room a fan of yellow rays bursts out to every edge of the picture, and one solid beam drops straight down the front of the tower and spreads across the black water as a wide yellow path. The sea is cut with short pale ripples that catch the glow, and a black headland sits low on the right. Three colors, slate blue, black and warm yellow. The words START POINT run across the bottom in rounded navy capitals.

It suits a hallway, a landing, a bedroom or a home office, above a bed or at the end of a corridor. Navy and yellow go with white walls, dark wood and brass lamps. It fits night owls and anyone who grew up on the Devon coast, and it sits well beside Keep the Light from the same series.
