This Cliff and Crane print is an ink wash style landscape poster of a sheer cliff painted in heavy wet black ink along the right edge, with a single crane flying across the empty sky beside it, for anyone who likes bold ink and open space together.

The cliff face is dark and textured, its top fringed with trees and its base lost in a soft green gray mist. The rest of the page is cream, with the crane a small dark mark in the upper left and faint calligraphy and a red seal at the edges. The contrast between the solid cliff and the open air is the whole idea.

It suits a hallway, a living room, a study or a stairwell, and it works especially well in a tall narrow space. It also sits well in a modern room with black, white and oak, since the palette is the same.
