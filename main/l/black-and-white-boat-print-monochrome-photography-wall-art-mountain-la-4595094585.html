This rowing boat print is a black and white photographic poster for a living room, a study or anyone who loves lakes and mountains.

A small wooden rowing boat sits on perfectly still water, bow toward the viewer, with its reflection hanging beneath it so sharply that the two halves almost make a single oval. Behind it a steep, rocky mountain rises to a pointed peak and mirrors itself across the lake, and a single dark rock breaks the surface in the lower right. The sky is pale and empty, the boat and the rock are deep black, and the mountain carries the mid grays with every crag and scree slope picked out in fine detail. The foreground water fades to near white, so the boat seems to float on nothing. The mood is calm.

It suits a living room, a study or a hallway, above a sofa or a desk. The blacks and grays go with white walls, oak, tweed and dark green, and it pairs with the lighthouse from the same series. It fits hikers, fishermen and anyone who finds still water restful.
